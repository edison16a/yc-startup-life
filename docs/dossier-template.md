# Dossier template

`FINAL.md` must contain these exact `##` headings (checked by `scripts/check_run.py`). Start the file with a `# <Idea name>` title.

## One-liner and verdict
The idea in two sentences, the Judge's verdict and conviction, and the run id. If the cap was reached, say "best found, not confirmed" here.

## Customer and pain
Who exactly, how many of them, the painful job, how they solve it today, and what that costs them.

## Why now
The specific shifts that make this possible or urgent today, and the evidence of current need, with sources.

## Product and MVP scope
What the first version does and deliberately does not do. What a small team can ship in about 3 months.

## Pricing and business model
Price, who pays, and evidence from comparable products or current spend.

## Market size (bottom-up)
Customers x price, each input sourced, arithmetic shown, and the sanity check.

## Competition and whitespace evidence
Every closest competitor and overlapping past YC company found, their traction, and the specific gap. Include the searches run. Name the moat and the strongest argument against it.

## Technical complexity and takeover risk
What is hard and why a small team can still do it; who could build or absorb this, how fast, and why they might not; what the analog YC companies show.

## Fit with YC patterns
Which findings from the knowledge base this idea matches or violates. Comparable YC companies and their outcomes, with caveats about sample bias.

## Funding outlook (evaluator)
The YC acceptance estimate and the round-by-round table from the full evaluation (`evaluation/EVALUATION.md`), with the biggest reasons it could fail at each round.

## Name and brand
The chosen name with the `yc-name-creator` evaluation: score, name-check result, domain and handle options, trademark and connotation risks, the brand idea (mascot, voice, visual direction), and the shortlist of alternatives.

## Go-to-market
First 10 customers: where they are and how to reach them. Then the scaling channel.

## Founder profile needed
Skills and experience that make a team credible here, and the biggest gap to close.

## First 30 days: experiments
The cheapest tests of the 3 biggest risks, each with a pass/fail threshold.

## Risks
Top risks ranked, with mitigation or why unmitigated.

## Investor / Doubter / Judge summary
Strongest points from each side, how the rebuttals went, and the Judge's reasons and open risks.

## Runners-up and why they lost
Other finalists with the Judge's reason for each.

## YC application draft
Draft answers: what the company does, who the customer is, why now, how it makes money, competitors and why you win, what you understand that others don't. A draft for the founders to rewrite in their own voice.

## Research limits
Coverage limits inherited from the knowledge base, claims that are assumptions, and anything not verified.

## Sources
All URLs relied on.
