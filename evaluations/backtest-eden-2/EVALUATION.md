## General assessment

Medical diagnostics AI in mid 2018 was a real and funded opportunity, but it is capital intensive, slow to sell and regulated. For a company whose 2018 product and team are only thinly known, the honest forecast is the cohort base rate discounted for healthcare. Most similar YC companies in the data either stalled or stayed small, with a minority reaching an acquisition. Upside exists only if the team lands real clinical data and a regulatory path early. The cumulative odds from acceptance are low in absolute terms because each stage is discounted for slow hospital sales cycles and clinical validation, and the starting acceptance rate is only about one percent. Treat the figures as wide priors given thin facts about the 2018 team.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 1.5%

**How we got the number.** Prior is about 1 percent per knowledge/yc_acceptance.md (range 0.6 to 2 percent, 2018 batches had roughly 8,000 to 9,000 applicants and about 150 accepted per the Listen Notes W18 estimate). Adjusted up slightly because medical AI was a hot theme in 2018 and YC accepted several diagnostics companies in 2016 to 2018, and left near the prior because team, founder market fit and traction are unknown. Capped low because regulated healthcare with no stated customer evidence is a harder pitch.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly pass because a diagnostics AI pitch with no named hospital customers, no clinical validation and no clear regulatory path looks like a technology looking for a buyer. Healthcare is slow to seed and A in the data, and past YC diagnostics companies such as Unima and Bikanta stalled on clinical and capital needs. Without a founding team with clinical credibility the interview would likely end there.

## Pros

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show a credible team and one paying or committed clinical partner.

1. Secure a letter of intent from one clinic or lab.
2. Build a working demo on real, de-identified data.

You know it worked when: One signed pilot agreement before applying.

### Reach Seed

Convert pilots into a validated result that closes a seed.

1. Run a retrospective validation study with a clinical partner.
2. Pitch healthcare specialist funds at demo day.

You know it worked when: Published validation metric and a closed seed round.

### Reach Series A

Reach repeatable revenue across several providers.

1. Convert pilots to annual contracts.
2. Start the clearance path with the FDA if required.

You know it worked when: Recurring revenue from five or more providers.

### Reach Series B

Show multi-system rollout and expansion revenue.

1. Standardise integrations with major imaging and lab systems.
2. Hire an enterprise sales lead.

You know it worked when: Net revenue retention above 120 percent.

### Reach Series C and beyond

Prove category leadership and path to profit.

1. Expand to adjacent diagnostic areas.
2. Secure reimbursement or a pricing model that scales.

You know it worked when: Contracts across dozens of systems with positive gross margin.

## Assumptions and limits

- Only the tags and a later one-liner were available, so the 2018 product, team and traction are assumed and unknown.
- Healthcare only cohort 2012-2017 has n=7 researched, so the broad all-industry 2012-2017 cohort (n=114) was used as the main anchor.
- Web search was not run; competitor facts come from general 2018 knowledge and cited public pages that were not re-verified.
- Round sizes are 2018 era values and not adjusted to today.
- Calibration-only cohort data used; funding coverage is partial so rates carry the lower to upper range.
