## General assessment

Termius is an existing, revenue earning SSH client with team collaboration, sold to engineers and DevOps teams. It has a credible technical team and a real daily pain point, which helps the YC odds, but the category has free defaults and a small paid segment, so investors may see a utility rather than a venture business. Odds of reaching each round sit below the B2B cohort anchors at the later stages because the base rates come from a larger market mix. The most likely path is a seed or modest raise, a plateau around a small revenue base and an eventual small acquisition or continued independence.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 4.0%

**How we got the number.** Start from the roughly 1.5 percent Winter 2019 rate (about 189 companies from about 12,000 applicants, per press coverage of the YC blog) and the 1 percent prior in yc_acceptance.md. Adjust up for a technical team with a shipped cross platform product and paying subscribers, and for a painful problem the founders know first hand as engineers. Adjust slightly up again because the batch grew by over 50 percent versus the previous one. Adjust down for a small niche market and a product that is an incremental upgrade to free tools. Net about 4 percent, inside the 0.3 to 15 percent guardrail.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because an SSH client looks like a small, crowded market with free defaults (PuTTY, OpenSSH, iTerm) and a handful of paid clients, so the ceiling looks like a profitable utility rather than a venture scale company. If interviewers see only a modest installed base and no clear path from individual to team and enterprise revenue, the team collaboration pitch may not feel like a big enough wedge.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show a paying, growing user base and a team plan story.

1. Publish revenue and seat growth numbers
2. Write a clear pitch for team and enterprise expansion

You know it worked when: Monthly recurring revenue with a rising share from team plans

### Reach Seed

Convert YC interest into a seed with growth in team seats.

1. Launch paid team plans with invites and shared vaults
2. Gather customer references from DevOps teams

You know it worked when: Team seats growing at least 15 percent monthly at Demo Day

### Reach Series A

Reach venture scale revenue with enterprise features.

1. Ship audit logs, SSO and access controls
2. Hire first enterprise sales rep

You know it worked when: About 1M to 2M annual recurring revenue with strong retention

### Reach Series B

Prove expansion beyond the client into infrastructure access.

1. Build server side components and integrations
2. Sign enterprise contracts in security teams

You know it worked when: Net revenue retention above 120 percent and several six figure contracts

### Reach Series C and beyond

Become a platform for secure infrastructure access.

1. Expand internationally and to compliance buyers
2. Consider acquisitions

You know it worked when: Revenue above 20M with a clear leading position
