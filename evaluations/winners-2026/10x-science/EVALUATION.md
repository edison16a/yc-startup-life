## General assessment

10x Science sells AI models that speed up protein characterization for drug developers. The problem is real, pharma already pays for analytics, and the team has academic credibility plus a repeat YC founder. The difficulty is that biotech tooling is sold slowly, incumbents like Genedata, Dotmatics and Revvity already bundle comparable features, and early traction is unverified. A seed above the standard YC check within a year is likely given AI and bio funding conditions. Series A depends on turning pilots into recurring contracts, and reaching a Series B or later is closer to the cohort base rates than to a breakout. About 45 percent of forecast paths end in a stall or shutdown.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 50%

**How we got the number.** The company is already accepted to YC, so this is a note only. A random applicant starts near 1 percent. Strong domain founders, a prior YC founder, claimed enterprise pharma customers and a technical team would lift a typical application well above that, so a bounded but high number is shown for completeness.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because biotech tooling sells slowly and the traction claim is unverified, with five people selling into pharma where procurement and data security reviews take quarters. A reviewer could also read the pitch as an academic method looking for a market, and ask why a model would beat the incumbent analytical vendors.

## Pros

- **Founders with deep field expertise**: The team says it built next-generation protein characterization in a Nobel laureate's lab, with many publications.
- **Customers already spend on this problem**: Analytical testing outsourcing is a multi billion dollar market and vendors sell competing software. (https://drug-dev.com/special-feature-analytical-testing-diverse-demands-therapies-require-diverse-analyses/)
- **Repeat YC founder and claimed enterprise pharma traction**: Stated in the batch description; unverified.

## Roadmap

### Reach Seed

Two or more pharma customers on paid annual contracts.

1. Convert paid pilots into annual contracts with a measured time saving.
2. Publish validation against wet lab characterization results.

You know it worked when: Annual recurring revenue from at least two named pharma accounts.

### Reach Series A

Repeatable sales motion and expansion within accounts.

1. Hire a first enterprise seller with biologics experience.
2. Add a second assay type to expand each account.

You know it worked when: Net revenue retention above 120 percent and five or more paying accounts.
