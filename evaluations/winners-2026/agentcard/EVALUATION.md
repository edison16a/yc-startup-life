## General assessment

Agentcard offers cards so assistant products can buy things online. Timing is good and investors fund agent infrastructure, so a seed above the YC deal is more likely than not. The weakness is that Stripe, Visa and Coinbase are moving into the same space, card programs need bank and compliance work, and the customer base is early. Fintech analogs such as Lyra and Bridgecard stalled while Slash and Jeeves succeeded after finding large customer bases. The ladder follows the Fintech and B2B anchors with wide uncertainty.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 2.0%

**How we got the number.** Test note: this company was accepted to YC, so this is only the forward estimate. Starting prior is about 1 percent for a random applicant (0.6 to 2 percent third party range, per references/yc_acceptance.md). Agent payments is a current YC-style topic with real urgency (up), four person team (neutral), fintech and regulated cards are hard for a young team (down), and large incumbents Stripe, Visa and Coinbase are active (down). Net about 2 percent.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most likely say that card issuing for agents depends on banks and networks, that Stripe, Visa and Coinbase are shipping agent payment rails, and that the customer base of personal assistant companies is tiny and early. Without a regulatory path and live volume, partners may see a feature that incumbents will absorb.
