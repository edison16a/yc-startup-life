## General assessment

Aikido Technologies proposes gigawatt scale floating wind platforms with data halls cooled by the ocean. The need is real, since AI compute is constrained by power, and the pitch connects to a hot theme, which makes a seed above the YC check likely though not certain. The difficulty is that offshore marine hardware is expensive, slow to permit and slow to build, onshore power competes, and the nearest analogs in marine and wind hardware stayed small or failed. Reaching Series A needs a demonstrator and a named buyer, and later rounds depend on project finance, not venture capital. About 60 percent of simulated paths stall or shut down, and the upside is large but rare.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 40%

**How we got the number.** Already accepted, so this is a note. A random applicant starts near 1 percent. Energy and AI infrastructure sit close to YC's interest in hard problems, the pitch is specific and the platform is described as proprietary, which would lift odds, while capital intensity and permitting lower them.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly decline because a floating offshore platform needs hundreds of millions of dollars, years of permitting and marine engineering, which does not fit a three month batch or a small seed check. Reviewers would also doubt that a five person team can show traction before Demo Day.
