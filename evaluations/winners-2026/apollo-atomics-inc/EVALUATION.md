## General assessment

Apollo Atomics claims the most compact nuclear reactors and gave no further detail, so the forecast rests on the sector. Nuclear funding was strong in 2025 and 2026, YC has funded Oklo and recent fusion companies, so a seed above the YC check is likely. Beyond that, the company needs a licence, fuel and hundreds of millions of dollars, and most hard tech teams stall before a demonstrator. The ladder follows the Industrials anchors, raised a little for the sector tailwind, with a majority chance of ending in shutdown or stall.

## Roadmap

### Reach Seed

Show a credible design and team to investors and DOE.

1. Publish the design basis and hire a licensing lead.
2. Apply to the DOE reactor pilot or a national lab test partnership.

You know it worked when: A signed lab or site agreement and a seed above 2M

### Reach Series A

Fund a first demonstration.

1. Secure fuel supply commitments.
2. Sign letters of intent from a data center or remote site customer.

You know it worked when: Funded test reactor plan with customer commitments
