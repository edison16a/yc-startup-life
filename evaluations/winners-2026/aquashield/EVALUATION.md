## General assessment

AquaShield sells AI water leak detection to large real estate portfolios, where damage can cost hundreds of millions a year. Spring 2026 batch with a team of 3 in San Francisco. The pain is large and insurable, which gives a clear payment story, but the YC record for building sensor and property tech companies is mixed: PowerX and Kapacity were acquired small and Social Construct shut down despite raising far more. The seed hinges on signed portfolios and measurable avoided loss within a year.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Land one portfolio pilot with real loss numbers.

1. Instrument one large building and record leaks caught.
2. Interview insurers about premium credit programs.

You know it worked when: One portfolio owner pilot with measured events.

### Reach Seed

Show paid deployments and avoided loss data.

1. Convert pilots to annual contracts across several hundred units.
2. Sign one insurer partner.

You know it worked when: Three paying portfolios and one documented avoided claim above 100K.

### Reach Series A

Show a repeatable install and sell model.

1. Standardize a retrofit kit under a set install time.
2. Add an insurer channel.

You know it worked when: About 1.5M recurring revenue and gross margin above 60 percent.
