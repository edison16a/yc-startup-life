## General assessment

Arga Labs sells simulated copies of services such as Stripe and Slack so teams can test and train agents safely. The need is real and growing, and a team of 6 suggests a working product. The problem is crowding: mocking vendors, sandbox platforms and other YC teams sell similar products, and platforms could bundle twins. A seed above the YC deal looks likely in 12 months because agent infrastructure is a favored category. Later rounds depend on turning labs and developers into recurring revenue, and on owning fidelity at scale. The base rates for AI B2B companies show about one in four reaching a Series A.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 4.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting rate is about 1 percent with a 0.6 to 2 percent band (knowledge yc_acceptance.md). The company is already accepted, so this is a note only. A team of 6 working on agent testing infrastructure fits the current RFS interest in AI tooling and likely carries technical founders, which would raise the odds several times over the base rate.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because simulated API twins for agent testing look like a feature of a mocking or CI product, and several teams were building the same thing at once. Without live usage from agent builders the pitch rests on a thesis about demand, and the interview would probe why customers would not simply use mocks or vendor sandboxes.
