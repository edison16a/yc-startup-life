## General assessment

Asakana has the strongest early signals among order entry teams in the batch: a founder from seafood distribution and live use at US distributors. The category is crowded with funded and YC rivals, so each later round is about average for B2B. The likely path is a small to normal seed and then a contest for the Series A. It is a forecast from the input file and the base rates only, with no knowledge of what happened after the batch started, and the numbers should be read with wide error bars because the cohorts for these categories are small.
