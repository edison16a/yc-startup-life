## General assessment

Axonnic is a two person team building wearable EMG sensors to collect human skill data for robot learning. Physical AI is a hot theme and Industrials companies reach seed fast, so a seed above $500K is likely but not certain. The core risk is whether EMG adds value over video, tactile and teleoperation data, and the customer base of robotics labs is small. Similar YC companies such as Relari and Sensei had no priced rounds in the data, and later rounds depend on a clear lift in robot performance. A pivot toward robotics products or human computer interaction is a possible upside.
