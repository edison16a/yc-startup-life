## General assessment

Bernard sells AI employees to appliance repair companies, covering calls, scheduling, dispatch and a diagnosis tool that tells technicians which parts to bring. The metric, first visit fix rate, is clear and buyers care about it. The team of 9 and the specific vertical help, but the market is small and fragmented, and the leading field service platforms already ship AI answering. A seed above the YC deal is likely because vertical AI is fundable, and the later steps depend on whether Pre-ID becomes a data advantage platforms cannot copy. Stalling is more likely than failing outright, since paying servicers tend to stay.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show servicers will pay for a measured first visit fix gain.

1. Run a pilot with several servicers and measure first visit fix rate before and after.
2. Collect two written customer references.

You know it worked when: Two or more paid servicers with a measured gain.

### Reach Seed

Reach about $1M recurring revenue pace and a lead seed.

1. Integrate with the top three field service platforms.
2. Expand Pre-ID to more appliance types.

You know it worked when: A seed above $500K and recurring revenue growing month over month.
