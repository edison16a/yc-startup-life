## General assessment

Beyond Reach Labs aims to sell deployable solar arrays that extend far beyond stowed size, targeting a surge in satellite power demand tied to orbital data centers and large platforms. The founders have rare, relevant experience, and YC space peers have raised $3M to $9M seeds. The risks are the physics and the pace: deployable structures need flight proof, Redwire and Rocket Lab are already selling large arrays, and capital intensive companies like Sunfolding can fail even after a C. Industrials cohort data give about 68 percent for a seed, but only about 30 to 47 percent of seeded companies reach A. I forecast a likely seed, a slightly below even Series A, and a high failure share given technical risk.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 15%

**How we got the number.** Test assumes acceptance, so this is a note. Baseline YC acceptance is low single digits. Strong founder market fit (NASA deployables research, SpaceX flight hardware), a patented design and a clear tailwind from orbital data center power demand lift this to about 15 percent, while hardware qualification risk and the long road to flight revenue hold it back.

**Why it fails here.** YC could say a deployable structure is a long, capital heavy hardware program with no flight heritage, that Redwire (ROSA) and others already sell roll out arrays with flight history, and that the 10 times claim is unproven. Satellite buyers are conservative and slow to adopt unflown power systems, so the first revenue could be years away.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show a working deployment demonstration.

1. Build a one tenth scale ground deployment unit
2. Sign a letter of intent from one satellite maker

You know it worked when: Video and loads data of a repeatable deployment and one signed LOI

### Reach Seed

Raise on demonstration and a first contract.

1. Apply for NASA and Space Force SBIR awards
2. Book a rideshare slot for a flight test

You know it worked when: A funded contract or SBIR and a booked flight

### Reach Series A

Flight heritage and first revenue.

1. Fly the demonstration and publish results
2. Sign a paid production order

You know it worked when: Successful orbital deployment and over $3M in contracts
