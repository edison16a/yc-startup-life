## General assessment

Billow is an AI native accounting and FP&A firm that works inside existing finance stacks such as NetSuite and Coupa, built by repeat founders with an ERP exit and Bloomberg data experience. Summer 2026 batch. It has strong founder market fit and a clear budget line to displace, so a seed above the YC check is likelier than the cohort average. The weak points are a services shaped pitch, a crowded field with funded rivals and large ERP vendors adding agents, and an integration heavy product. The closest peer, Campfire, shows the upside path, while Finnt shows the stall path.

## Pros

- **Repeat founders with a platform exit**: One founder built and exited an accounting and ERP platform reaching 45,000 businesses in four years, as stated in the application description. (https://thebillow.ai/)
- **Relevant finance advisors**: Advisors include CFOs, controllers and a Big 4 auditor, which helps with trust and first customers. (https://thebillow.ai/)
- **A paid, budgeted workflow**: Accounting and FP&A headcount is a clear existing budget line. (https://www.deloitte.com)

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show founder market fit with a first design partner.

1. Recruit three controllers as paid design partners.
2. Automate one close workflow end to end with audit logs.

You know it worked when: One paid pilot before the batch.

### Reach Seed

Convert pilots into paid software contracts.

1. Move pricing from hours to per workflow software fees.
2. Publish accuracy and audit readiness metrics.

You know it worked when: Five paying finance teams and software gross margin above 70 percent.

### Reach Series A

Prove a repeatable sales motion with net retention.

1. Build a standard connector library for the top ERP and AP systems.
2. Hire a head of customer success from a close software vendor.

You know it worked when: About 1.5M to 2M recurring revenue and net retention above 110 percent.
