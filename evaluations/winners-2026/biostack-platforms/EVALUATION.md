## General assessment

BioStack packages clinical and preclinical data into training environments for healthcare and drug discovery AI. The category is hot: labs buy expert data and environments, and Scale AI and Mercor are expanding into healthcare. A team of 13 and the hot theme make a seed above the YC deal reasonably likely. The difficulties are data access from hospitals and labs, a small number of lumpy buyers, and the chance that labs or labelers build it themselves. Healthcare cohorts are slower to seed and to Series A. The likeliest paths are a seed then a stall, an acquisition by a data or labeling company, or a pivot into a different data niche.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 4.0%

**How we got the number.** Accepted to YC in this test, so this note is for completeness. From roughly 1 percent for a random applicant, the lifts are a hot AI data category and a team of 13. The cuts are that data access from hospitals and labs is the hard part and is only claimed, and many YC peers pitch RL environments and data for labs.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly pass because RL environment and training data startups were abundant in 2025 and 2026, and the pitch claims proprietary clinical data pipelines without naming a hospital or lab partner that actually grants access, which is the hardest part for a reviewer to believe at this stage of the company.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Prove access to real clinical or preclinical data from named partners.

1. Sign a data agreement with at least one hospital or CRO.
2. Document privacy and consent handling.

You know it worked when: One signed partner with usable data in a pipeline.

### Reach Seed

Show paying AI lab or biotech customers for the environments.

1. Run paid pilots with two model builders.
2. Measure the model improvement from the environments.

You know it worked when: Paid contracts and a seed above $500K.
