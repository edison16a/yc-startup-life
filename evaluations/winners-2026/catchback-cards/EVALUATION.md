## General assessment

CatchBack Cards runs custom mystery packs for Pokemon and sports cards with buyback. The category is booming and Courtyard's $7M seed and $30M A prove investors will pay. But randomized pack sales face lottery claims, margins are thin, and the leaders are far ahead. Consumer cohorts show 56 percent reaching a seed, and later stages are thin. I keep seed above the anchor for the boom and keep the later stages near anchors, because later consumer data is thin and right censored. The biggest swing factor is the legal status of randomized packs, which no analysis here can settle.

## Roadmap

### Reach Seed

Prove repeat pack creators and clean legal posture.

1. Get a gaming law opinion.
2. Measure weekly returning packs per user.
3. Fix buyback margin per pack.

You know it worked when: Positive contribution margin per pack and 30 percent monthly repeat.

### Reach Series A

Scale with healthy margins.

1. Sign creators and sports card shops.
2. Move inventory into partner vaults.
3. Add risk controls on fraud.

You know it worked when: GMV above $5M per month with margin above 8 percent.

### Reach Series B

Become a top three pack platform.

1. Expand states and countries.
2. Add sports leagues licenses.
3. Build compliance team.

You know it worked when: Profitable at scale.
