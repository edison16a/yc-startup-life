## General assessment

Clara is an AI primary care practice from the founders of Circle Medical, which gives it unusual credibility with investors and clinicians. A seed is very likely and later rounds depend on safety data and unit economics. The forecast treats the day one description as the best case, anchors on the 2022 to 2024 cohort of researched YC companies, and widens the uncertainty because funding data for recent batches is incomplete and right censored.
