## General assessment

Complir sells AI product compliance to retailers. A new EU regulation gives it a clear trigger and a thirteen person team is large for a spring 2026 company. The likeliest path is a seed, then a harder Series A as investors test whether it scales like software. UpCodes and Pluralith show slower paths. Data on recent B2B Series B outcomes is thin. The step odds are anchored on the 2022 to 2025 B2B cohorts and the older 2018 to 2021 cohort, which differ a lot, so treat the later rounds as wide ranges.
