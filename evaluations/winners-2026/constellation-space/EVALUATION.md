## General assessment

Constellation Space sells network operations software for giant satellite constellations. The founders have relevant backgrounds and space software has raised money, as quindar and basalt show. But the buyer set is tiny, the largest operator builds its own system, and the YC text lists capabilities with no customers. A seed above the YC check is a coin flip within a year, and later rounds depend on converting pilots to recurring contracts. Space cases in the data include both a shutdown after a long hardware path and a firm that reached a Series A.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show a credible technical demo and named design partner.

1. Publish simulation benchmarks
2. Sign one letter of intent

You know it worked when: One named operator in a pilot

### Reach Seed

Convert a pilot into paid revenue.

1. Secure a funded pilot
2. Apply for SBIR or similar

You know it worked when: Signed paid pilot above 100K

### Reach Series A

Land multiple recurring contracts.

1. Expand to three operators
2. Prove link efficiency gains

You know it worked when: 1M ARR from at least three customers

### Reach Series B

Scale to mid sized operators and defense.

1. Hire sales and compliance
2. Certify for classified work

You know it worked when: 5M ARR

### Reach Series C and beyond

Become the standard operations layer.

1. Expand to compute scheduling
2. Partner with primes

You know it worked when: 20M ARR or acquisition interest
