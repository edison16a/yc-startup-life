## General assessment

Corvera sells an AI sales agent to CPG brands that sell through independent stores and distributors. The pain is real: brands pay brokers and cover tens of thousands of stores with tiny teams. The team has repeat founder and data credentials and cites a named customer, and the description itself reports a $6.2M raise, so a seed above the YC check is very likely. Beyond seed the picture is average: B2B cohorts reach Series A about a third of the time, the ICP is small, and data platforms like Crisp can add similar agents. Neighbors in YC CPG (Muffin Data, Jupiter, Milky Way AI) mostly stayed small or ended, while Confido and Glimpse scaled slowly.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 15%

**How we got the number.** Test assumes acceptance, so this is a note. Baseline acceptance is low single digits. A repeat founder, an ex Google data lead, a named customer with usage numbers and a stated raise would lift this to about 15 percent, offset by the narrow CPG focus.

**Why it fails here.** YC could say the market is a small group of mid size CPG brands and that data platforms like Crisp already connect UNFI data. A single named customer with 12 users is thin evidence, and AI sales agents for specific verticals are common, so the application would need to show why this team wins distributor data access and why mid size brands will pay.
