## General assessment

Derya runs supply chain functions for customers using AI agents across robotics, commodities and food. The wedge is wide and brokerage margins are thin, which is why the seed is set a little below the B2B anchor. Analogs split: Mentum and Arnata raised seeds, Alima went inactive. Later steps follow the B2B anchors with a small discount for working capital needs, and a small acquisition is the likeliest exit. The estimates are anchored on the 2023 to 2025 B2B cohort and adjusted only with the stated reasons, keeping each step within 0.75 times its anchor. Reaching a later round years after the batch is common for companies with paying customers, and a pivot followed by a raise is part of the upside band, so the ladder asks whether a round is reached and not how fast.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Arrive at the batch with a sharp wedge and one named paying design partner.

1. Pick one narrow customer and workflow and write down the measurable result it should produce.
2. Sign a letter of intent or paid pilot before demo day.

You know it worked when: At least one pilot signed with a named buyer.

### Reach Seed

Prove gross profit per order in one commodity and customer type.

1. Close three paying customers in one vertical.
2. Track gross margin and repeat orders.

You know it worked when: A seed above the YC check backed by a repeat revenue base.

### Reach Series A

Show automation lowering cost per order and margin rising.

1. Productize the quoting and supplier matching steps.
2. Raise a Series A tied to gross profit growth.

You know it worked when: Gross profit above 300K dollars annualised.
