## General assessment

Didit is a 20 person team selling a broad identity and fraud API with pay as you go pricing. Identity verification is a proven paid need, which supports a seed above $500K within a year, and several YC peers such as Authologic and AiPrise reached Series A. The category is crowded and price competition is real, and two close analogs, Berbix and Invoid, ended in acquisitions. Later rounds are therefore plausible but far from assured, and a small acquisition is the most common good outcome. Reaching a round years later is common for companies with paying customers.

A live product with paying users.

1. Launch the free tier and track conversion.
2. Gather accuracy benchmarks.

You know it worked when: Dozens of paying integrations.

### Reach Seed

Revenue and a clear wedge.

1. Win one vertical such as crypto or neobanks.
2. Add reference customers.

### Reach Series A

Retention and pricing power.

1. Add AML and fraud upsells.
2. Get SOC 2 and local licences.

You know it worked when: About $2M to $3M annualized revenue with net revenue retention above 100%.

### Reach Series B

Multi region scale.

1. Hire enterprise sales.
2. Expand data sources.

You know it worked when: Revenue growth above 2x a year.

### Reach Series C and beyond

Category leader.

1. Launch fraud network effects.
2. Consider selective acquisitions.

You know it worked when: Revenue above $30M and a defensible data asset.
