## General assessment

Discovered Materials applies AI agents to semiconductor material discovery, led by a founder whose materials reached Intel and TSMC roadmaps. Investor appetite for AI science was very high in 2025 and 2026, which makes a seed above the YC deal likely. The risks are the long validation cycle and rivals with hundreds of millions. A Series A depends on a validated material or a paying chip partner. Analogs show AI discovery companies often stall after one large round, so the later steps stay near cohort anchors.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 15%

**How we got the number.** Company is assumed accepted. The prior is about 1 percent; deep technical credibility, with materials adopted into Intel and TSMC roadmaps, and an AI for science theme with large funding comps push this to the 15 percent top of the allowed range.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly decline if it could not see a near term revenue path, since materials discovery has a ten year validation cycle and chipmakers buy slowly. It would also question whether a four person team can compete with Periodic Labs and CuspAI, which raised hundreds of millions.
