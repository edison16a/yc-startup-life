## General assessment

Drafted arrives with funding, a research team and visible usage, so each funding step starts well above a typical YC company. The weakness is that floor plan design is episodic and incumbents can add similar tools, so the later rounds depend on turning free usage into paid professional use. It is a forecast from the input file and the base rates only, with no knowledge of what happened after the batch started, and the numbers should be read with wide error bars because the cohorts for these categories are small.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 15%

**How we got the number.** Assumed accepted in this test, so this is a note. Published rate is about 1 percent of over 10,000 applicants (references/yc_acceptance.md). This company stated 120k monthly visitors, 325k plans generated, a nine person technical team and $17.5M raised from named angels and funds, which lifts it far above the starting rate.

**Why it fails here.** YC could say no because consumer AI design tools often show viral traffic without paying users, and the website visits may be curiosity from social posts. Residential architecture is also slow to monetize, and the company already has large funding so does not need YC.
