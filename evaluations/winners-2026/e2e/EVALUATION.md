## General assessment

e2e sells the verification layer for agentic coding, which is a real and growing need as teams ship code written by AI. The same insight is shared by several funded startups, and the YC record shows testing companies tend to raise a seed and then struggle to scale, as Rainforest and Reflect did. The team of 8 gives it build capacity, and the timing helps a seed above 500K within a year. The path to a Series A depends on paying teams and a wedge that coding agents and CI platforms cannot bundle away. The description is thin, so uncertainty is wide.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly pass because the pitch is a category label with no stated wedge or customer. AI testing and verification for coding agents already had funded startups, and a reviewer would ask what e2e does that Momentic, QA Wolf or the coding agents themselves do not. ## Roadmap

### Get into YC

A clear wedge and a few real users of the verification product.

1. Choose one stack and one failure type and ship a working verifier on it.
2. Get three engineering teams using it on agent written pull requests.

You know it worked when: Three teams running it weekly.

### Reach Seed

Paying customers and visible usage that justify a round above 500K.

1. Charge per repo or per run and sign five paying teams.
2. Publish precision and false positive numbers and open source a core piece to build trust.

You know it worked when: Recurring revenue and a public accuracy benchmark.

### Reach Series A

Repeatable revenue from teams that did not know the founders.

1. Hire a first sales and customer success owner and add CI integrations.
2. Show net revenue retention and a pipeline of larger teams.

You know it worked when: About 1M to 2M in annual recurring revenue with strong retention.

### Reach Series B

A category position that agent vendors cannot copy.

1. Build cross vendor integrations and enterprise controls.
2. Collect data on failures that improves the verifier.

You know it worked when: Growth above 2x with enterprise logos.
