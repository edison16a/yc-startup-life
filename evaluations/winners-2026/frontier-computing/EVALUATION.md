## General assessment

Frontier says it grows biological neural networks at scale as a machine learning substrate, with 100M neurons targeted against about 1M in academic cultures and a Frogger demo as proof. Three founders in London, Summer 2026. Deep tech with a strong narrative can raise a seed on a demo, but every later step needs reproducible evidence that the biology beats GPUs on cost. Rain Neuromorphics is the nearest YC analog and it ended in a sale after a collapsed Series B. I put most weight on a stall at seed or A.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Have a reproducible demo and a credible lab team.

1. Document the Frogger run and share data with an outside lab.
2. Show culture viability over weeks.

You know it worked when: An outside lab reproducing the result.

### Reach Seed

Show scaling beyond the academic limit.

1. Show a culture above 10M neurons with learning curves.
2. Compare cost and sample efficiency to a small GPU baseline.

You know it worked when: A reproducible result at 10M neurons or more.

### Reach Series A

Show a benchmark that beats a silicon baseline on a task.

1. Publish peer reviewed or lab verified results.
2. Sign one paying research customer.

You know it worked when: One task where cost per learning step beats a GPU baseline.
