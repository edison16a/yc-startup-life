## General assessment

GBrain offers a cloud assistant with memory and skills that works overnight. Demand for assistants is proven, and a small team can build it fast, so a seed is plausible if usage is strong. The core problem is that frontier labs ship the same features with huge distribution, so the company needs a narrow wedge. Analogs such as Tasker AI, Khoj and Niles stayed small or died while Clara Labs raised an A and sold. Estimates are slightly below the B2B cohort anchors, and uncertainty is wide because the product is general.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Test assumes the company was accepted, so this is only a note. Prior about 1 percent (0.6 to 2 percent third party range). Up for a technical team and strong product feel, down for a crowded general assistant category with direct frontier lab competition and no narrow wedge in the description.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly pass because a general cloud assistant that does overnight tasks is exactly what the model labs ship themselves: there is no named customer group, no clear edge over a lab product and many similar agent applicants each batch, so reviewers ask why this team wins.

## Pros

- **Large and proven demand for AI assistants**: Hundreds of millions of people use assistants and many pay for them. (https://chatgpt.com)
- **Technically feasible for a small team**: The product can be built on frontier models, so the team can ship quickly and iterate on usage. (https://claude.ai)

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Pick a narrow high value use case

1. Interview 20 heavy assistant users about overnight jobs
2. Ship a prototype used daily by 20 people

You know it worked when: Ten users returning every week

### Reach Seed

Raise above $500K within 12 months of the batch

1. Reach paying subscribers or team accounts
2. Publish retention and usage numbers

You know it worked when: Revenue run rate and a lead investor

### Reach Series A

Prove retention against lab products

1. Add integrations and team features
2. Move to enterprise seats

You know it worked when: Net revenue retention above 100 percent and $1M recurring revenue
