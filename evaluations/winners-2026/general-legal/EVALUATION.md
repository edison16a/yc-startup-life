## General assessment

General Legal is an AI enabled law firm for fast growing companies with a 40 person team, which means it likely has clients and revenue today. Investors paid for AI native law in 2025, with Crosby's $20M A and Eudia's large backing. The risks are services economics, law firm ownership rules and the Atrium precedent, which raised $75M and closed its software arm. I put seed above the B2B anchor of 59 percent for the revenue and the hot category, keep A within 1.35x of its anchor, and use older cohorts for B and C.

## Roadmap

### Reach Seed

Prove margins and structure.

1. Report gross margin per matter.
2. Fix the ownership structure with ethics counsel.
3. Sign top growth company clients on fixed fees.

You know it worked when: Gross margin above 50 percent on fixed fee matters.

### Reach Series A

Show software like scaling.

1. Standardize ten matter types.
2. Cap lawyer hours per matter.
3. Grow clients without linear hiring.

You know it worked when: Revenue per lawyer 2x a traditional firm.

### Reach Series B

Expand across practice areas.

1. Add litigation and IP.
2. Sell the platform to other firms.
3. Build compliance.

You know it worked when: Eight figure revenue with repeat clients.
