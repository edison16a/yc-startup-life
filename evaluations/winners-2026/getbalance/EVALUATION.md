## General assessment

Balance runs monthly books for small businesses with AI agents. The category is hot, with large rounds for Basis and Rillet in 2024 and 2025, and a team of 20 at the start of the batch suggests real customers. The risks are service level margins, strong rivals, and small business churn. A seed above the YC deal is likely, and a Series A is better than the cohort rate because of existing revenue, but the base rate for small business fintech Series A is about one in four.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 4.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting rate is about 1 percent with a 0.6 to 2 percent band. The company is already accepted, so this is a note only. A London team of 20 at batch start implies real customers and revenue, and AI accounting is a clear YC theme, which pushes the odds well above the base rate.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because AI accounting is crowded and services businesses with human accountants in the loop have lower margins than software. A team of 20 also suggests a service company that could be seen as hard to scale, and the interview would test whether gross margin improves as agents replace staff.
