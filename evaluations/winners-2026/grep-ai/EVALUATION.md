## General assessment

Grep AI has the best signals in this group: founders from the Brex platform team, named enterprise customers in fintech and a claimed concrete outcome at a brokerage. That makes a round above 500K within a year very likely and lifts the Series A above the cohort anchor. The risks are breadth, slow regulated sales and competition from model providers and agent platforms that offer the same self improving idea. Close YC peers such as Sphinx and Coris raised seeds of a few million but needed more than a year, and Zaam shows what happens without revenue. Customer claims come from the company itself and are unverified.

## Pros

- **Founder market fit**: Founders ran the platform team at Brex and say they hit these workflow bottlenecks themselves.
- **Named enterprise customers and a measured outcome**: The description cites IG.com, Airwallex, Worldpay and Citizens Bank and an 80 percent automated onboarding result, unverified.
- **Costs fall as agents learn**: Self improving agents lower unit cost over time if the claim holds.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show paid production use in a narrow workflow.

1. Convert the strongest customer into a contract with measured savings.
2. Choose the first productized workflow.

You know it worked when: One paid production workflow with measured cost per case.

### Reach Seed

Evidence of repeatable deployment.

1. Deploy the same agent at two more customers in under a month.
2. Publish error rate and cost per case trends.

You know it worked when: Three customers on the same workflow and recurring revenue.

### Reach Series A

A product that scales without custom work.

1. Build a self service builder and evaluation tooling.
2. Hire enterprise sales and a security lead.

You know it worked when: About 2M annual recurring revenue and under 50 percent services share.

### Reach Series B

Expand across workflows and customers.

1. Land a second workflow in existing accounts.
2. Win a top ten bank.

You know it worked when: Net revenue retention above 130 percent.
