## General assessment

Haladir is a four person team selling AI decision intelligence to warehouses, fulfillment and transportation operators. Demand is real and funded: several peers raised Series B rounds in 2025, and incumbents are adding agents. The risks are a very broad pitch for four people, slow enterprise pilots and suites that bundle similar features. The base rates for recent B2B YC companies show about 59 percent reaching a seed, 26 percent an A, and a thin tail beyond. I stay close to those, with a small lift for the AI climate.

## Roadmap

### Reach Seed

Paid pilot with one named operator.

1. Choose one decision such as slotting or labor plans.
2. Run a four week shadow test against the customer's current plan.
3. Convert to a paid pilot with a written savings target.

You know it worked when: One signed paid pilot with a measured percent cost saving.

### Reach Series A

Repeatable multi site revenue.

1. Productize constraints into templates.
2. Hire a sales lead who has sold into 3PLs.
3. Expand from one site to a network at one customer.

You know it worked when: $1M ARR across at least three customers with net retention above 110 percent.

### Reach Series B

Scale beyond founder led sales.

1. Add integrations with the top WMS and TMS vendors.
2. Build a partner channel with 3PLs.
3. Prove software margin above 70 percent.

You know it worked when: Eight figure ARR with sales not led by founders.
