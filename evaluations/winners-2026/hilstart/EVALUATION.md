## General assessment

Hilstart builds physical harnesses that let AI agents test hardware. The theme is timely and the hardware test category is funded, but the input shows a team size of zero and no customer or background, so the forecast stays near cohort rates. Seed is uncertain because investors will want a working harness and a pilot. Nominal and large test vendors are strong rivals and custom harness work can limit margins. Later rounds follow cohort anchors with no extra discount since no analog shows a fatal problem.
