## General assessment

Hop Aero wants to deliver cargo by rocket to contested locations, a mission the US military already studies. Defense hardware had a strong funding climate in 2026, so a seed above the standard YC deal is likely if the team can show credible propulsion progress. Later rounds depend on test flights, which cost far more than a seed can pay for, and on a government customer who has favoured larger primes. Analogs such as SpaceRyde and Talyn Air stalled, while Inversion reached a Series A. The ladder is therefore generous at seed and harsh at A.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 15%

**How we got the number.** Company is assumed accepted. The prior is about 1 percent; defense hardware is a current YC priority and a six person team with a clear government buyer pushes it up, so this note sets the top of the allowed range 15 percent.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly decline if the team could not show a credible path from a paper design to a flight test with a few hundred thousand dollars, since hypersonic rocket logistics needs very large capital before any revenue and the Air Force program already funds Blue Origin and Anduril.
