## General assessment

Hubble builds an AI retrieval engine to unify medical records with one patient authorization. Two founders with Grow Therapy and Amazon One Medical backgrounds, Seattle, Summer 2026, already backed by Madrona and Basis Set as per the description. Seed odds are above the cohort average. The reference class is mixed: PatientBank raised from top funds and shut, while Metriport pivoted into the same space and reached a Series A after about four years. Healthcare is slow to Series A in the YC data, so the ladder is gated by distribution and data access, not by capital.
