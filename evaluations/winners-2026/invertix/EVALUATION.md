## General assessment

Invertix builds AI to operate power plants and datacenters autonomously. The theme is hot, with large rounds for neighbors like Emerald AI and GridCARE. But the pitch is broad, the description lacks customers, sales cycles are long, and plant owners are cautious about autonomy. A seed above the YC deal is likely but not certain, and the energy analogs show few Series A rounds, with raptor maps the one that went the distance after a pivot. The estimates rest on cohort base rates, a handful of real analogs and market searches limited to information available at the batch start, so they carry wide uncertainty and should be read as ranges, not points.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting rate is about 1 percent with a 0.6 to 2 percent band. The company is already accepted, so this is a note only. A team of 8 in San Francisco on AI for energy and datacenters matches a hot theme, but the description gives little about customers or founders, which holds the estimate near the low end.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because the pitch is a vision of autonomous power plants with a trillion dollar market claim and no evidence of customers or pilots. Utilities and plant owners move slowly and are risk averse, so reviewers would ask how a startup gets control access to real plants.
