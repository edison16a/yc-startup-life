## General assessment

Janeen automates prior authorizations and benefits investigation for infusion clinics, a segment where drug costs are high and denials directly hit clinic cash. The pain is real and payers' complexity gives a software opening. The risks are scale and structure: infusion clinics are a bounded market, the category is crowded with better funded AI prior auth companies, and the claim of removing 95 percent of manual work needs proof that margins look like software. Cohort data for healthcare shows slow seed timing and about 40 percent conditional odds of a Series A. I expect a modest seed and a coin flip or worse beyond that, with an acquisition by a revenue cycle or specialty pharmacy platform as a common ending.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 14%

**How we got the number.** Test assumes the company was accepted, so this is a note only. The published acceptance rate is low single digits, and a five person healthcare AI team with a narrow, paid wedge (infusion clinics) and claimed live customers would sit above that (an estimate of about 14 percent, three to five times the baseline), though the AI prior auth category was already crowded in W26 with several sibling YC companies.

**Why it fails here.** YC could say the infusion clinic wedge is too small and that AI prior authorization has many funded rivals including Infinitus and Cohere Health, with no clear reason this team wins. Claims of 95 percent labor removal and clinics across the country are founder statements that cannot be verified, and healthcare services style businesses with heavy human review can look like low margin outsourcing rather than software.

## Pros

- **Concrete pain with money attached**: Biologics cost $80K to $150K per patient per year and denials leave clinics holding the cost, which gives a clear ROI. (https://academic.oup.com/ajhp/article/82/15/e710/7990644)
- **Healthcare AI admin is a funded, active category**: Several YC peers such as Trellis and Ember raised seed rounds in this space. (https://www.ycombinator.com/companies)

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show paying clinics and a measurable denial reduction.

1. Collect before and after denial rates from each live clinic
2. Publish the share of cases fully automated

You know it worked when: Three or more paying clinics with documented denial rate reduction

### Reach Seed

Raise above the standard YC deal on proof of savings.

1. Convert clinics to annual contracts priced on collected reimbursement
2. Build gross margin reporting showing software like margins

You know it worked when: At least $30K monthly recurring revenue and 80 percent automation

### Reach Series A

Show a repeatable motion beyond infusion.

1. Add a second specialty drug workflow
2. Hire a healthcare sales lead and integrate with the top EHRs

You know it worked when: Revenue over $1.5M annualized and net retention above 110 percent
