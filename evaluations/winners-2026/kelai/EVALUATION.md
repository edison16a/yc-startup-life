## General assessment

KelAI pitches an autonomous research engine for hedge funds from a former WorldQuant portfolio manager. Investor interest in AI for finance was high in early 2026, so a seed above the YC deal is likely. The harder steps are proof and distribution: funds are secretive, verify alpha slowly and often build their own tools. Analogs such as Pit.AI struggled to raise, while some AI research tools did reach seed and beyond. Probabilities follow the cohort anchors with a lift for pedigree at seed, and no discount below anchor at later steps.
