## General assessment

Klaimee offers insurance and certification for AI agents. The need is real because carriers are excluding AI claims, and the founder has proven insurance product experience. But armilla-ai, a YC predecessor, already sells this and raised only a seed, and insurance startups need carrier capacity and licenses that take time. A seed is somewhat likelier than not, Series A is about one in four, and the path usually runs through an MGA model before any carrier. Wide uncertainty applies because this is a blind forecast from a short listing, the cohort data is thin, and the seed step is defined as a round above 500K within roughly a year of batch start.
