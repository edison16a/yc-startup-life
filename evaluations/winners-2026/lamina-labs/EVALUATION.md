## General assessment

Lamina Labs is a two person Spring 2026 company building near real time structured video generation, starting with a whiteboard style explainer product called Simi. Interest in generative video is high and the closest YC analog raised a 4.1 million seed within two months of launch. The risks are that model labs ship the feature, that a funded competitor already holds the niche, and that explainer videos are an occasional need with weak retention. The most likely outcome is a seed round if launch metrics are strong, followed by a difficult Series A.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 4.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting from the roughly 1 percent prior (range 0.6 to 2 percent). The company is already accepted, so this is a note: a hot generative video theme and a working demo product would move it up, while two founders, a direct YC competitor in the same wedge from the previous year and platform risk from model labs hold it down.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because whiteboard explainer videos from prompts already have a funded YC company, Golpo, and Google ships video overviews, so the interview would press hard on what is defensible, who the paying customer is, and why two founders can outrun the model labs and a funded peer over the next year.

Compute cost per video at near real time speeds can also crush margins, so a Series A investor may pass until unit economics are shown.
