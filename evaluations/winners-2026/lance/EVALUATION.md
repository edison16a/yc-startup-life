## General assessment

Lance sells AI agents that answer calls, sell rooms and run operations for hotels, using computer use agents on existing software. It claims 50 plus hotels under major brands and has ten people, which is stronger traction than most batch companies. The need is concrete, since hotels miss calls and pay for software. The weaknesses are a crowded segment led by Canary Technologies, bundling by property management vendors, and reliability limits of computer use agents. A seed above the YC check within a year is likely, a Series A is above the cohort rate, and later rounds follow base rates. About 38 percent of simulated paths stall or shut down.
