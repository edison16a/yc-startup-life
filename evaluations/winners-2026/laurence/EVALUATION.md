## General assessment

Laurence has a live product, 12 people and a quantitative claim in a market that spends heavily, so I put the seed above the 59 percent B2B anchor and the Series A just above the 26 percent anchor. The category is mature, with funded rivals and platform control by Amazon, which holds the upside back. The most likely stop is Series A or an acquisition by an ad tech or commerce platform. These numbers are anchored on the 2022 to 2025 cohorts, whose later rounds are not yet fully observed, so the true rates are probably somewhat higher than the known only figures and the uncertainty is wide.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 6.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting prior is about 1 percent with a 0.6 to 2 percent band (references/yc_acceptance.md). Up: a team of 12 with a live product, paying use implied by a live Amazon integration and a quantitative angle. Down: Amazon ad tooling is a mature market and the 'hedge fund' story is not unique. Known to have been accepted, so completeness only.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly decline because Amazon advertising automation is a crowded, mature category with funded vendors such as Pacvue, Perpetua and Teikametrics, and the description does not show revenue, retention or measured lift against them. A team of 12 also looks like it may need to prove it is not a services business.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show measured profit lift for paying brands.

1. Publish holdout test results.
2. Name five paying customers.

You know it worked when: Lift above 15 percent on managed spend.

### Reach Seed

Convert early customers to recurring revenue.

1. Raise percent of spend price.
2. Launch Meta publicly.

You know it worked when: 1M annual recurring.

### Reach Series A

Prove repeatable multi channel growth.

1. Add Google and retail media.
2. Reduce churn.

You know it worked when: 3M to 5M annual recurring.

### Reach Series B

Move to larger brands.

1. Add enterprise features.
2. Partner with agencies.

You know it worked when: 10M annual recurring.

### Reach Series C and beyond

Platform status.

1. Add new channels such as ChatGPT ads.
2. Acquire smaller tools.

You know it worked when: Net retention above 120 percent.
