## General assessment

Lucent is an AI product manager that studies user sessions to find bugs and UX issues. AI developer tools raise seed rounds readily, and a similar company raised a $5M seed within days in 2025, so a seed above $500K within a year is a bit better than even. The risk is that session replay vendors already ship the same AI, and Lantern, a near twin, went inactive for that reason. With two founders and no stated traction, later rounds depend on a pivot to acting on findings, not only reporting them. Data in this niche is thin.
