## General assessment

Luel sells rights cleared multimodal training data to frontier AI teams. Demand is strong and a team of twelve is large for a winter 2026 startup. The likeliest path is a quick seed, then a harder Series A where buyers and prices are tested. Scale AI, Mercor and other vendors are bigger. The comparable YC data companies mostly have no later rounds yet, so the base rate evidence is thin. The step odds are anchored on the 2022 to 2025 B2B cohorts and the older 2018 to 2021 cohort, which differ a lot, so treat the later rounds as wide ranges.

## Pros

- **Twelve person team in a hot AI infrastructure category.**: Team size in the input and a market growing fast.
- **Buyers are well funded labs.**: Frontier labs pay large sums for data. (https://www.extruct.ai/data-room/ai-training-data-marketplaces/)
- **Rights cleared data answers a real legal need.**: Developers cite access to cleared content as a constraint. (https://itbrief.co.uk/story/troveo-expands-ai-data-platform-into-five-categories)

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Land two paying lab customers.

1. Run paid pilot collections.
2. Document rights clearance.

You know it worked when: Signed contracts over 250K dollars.

### Reach Seed

Convert projects to recurring licensing.

1. Build an off the shelf catalog.
2. Add a second modality.

You know it worked when: Repeat revenue from the same buyers.

### Reach Series A

Show durable supply.

1. Sign exclusive supply partners.
2. Grow gross margin.

You know it worked when: Annualized revenue above 5M and under 70 percent concentration.
