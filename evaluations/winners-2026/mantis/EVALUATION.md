## General assessment

Mantis is a three person Winter 2026 company that combines language models with physics simulation to turn rare human behavior data into predictive digital twins. The idea is technically deep and the broader category of human simulation attracts investors, but the description names no customer, domain or result. The main risks are an unclear first buyer, hard to validate predictions and slow research heavy sales. The most likely path is a modest seed, if a vertical emerges, followed by a hard Series A.

## Roadmap

### Reach Seed

Name a buyer and prove one validated prediction.

1. Choose one vertical and sign a paid pilot
2. Benchmark predictions against held out real data

You know it worked when: One paid pilot and a published accuracy result.

### Reach Series A

Repeatable revenue from the first vertical.

1. Turn the pilot into a product with a standard price
2. Add two more customers in the same vertical

You know it worked when: Several paying customers and 1M annual recurring revenue.
