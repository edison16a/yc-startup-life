## General assessment

Maywood sells a proactive assistant to dealmakers that tracks relationships and active processes. Banks already pay for deal CRMs, and the compliance angle is a real differentiator against general assistants. The risk is that Intapp, Affinity and Microsoft add similar features, and that adoption is slow. A seed above the YC check is likely for a five person vertical AI team in 2026. Reaching Series A depends on retention across deal teams and security approvals. About 40 percent of forecast paths end in a stall or shutdown.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 40%

**How we got the number.** Note only because the company is already accepted. A random applicant starts near 1 percent. A five person team with a clear vertical, a compliance angle and a defined buyer would be above the base, held back by crowded AI assistants and unstated traction.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because the wedge sits between CRM and assistant products where Intapp DealCloud and Affinity already sell, and finance compliance procurement is slow. A reviewer would also ask why bankers would trust a new startup with sensitive deal data and whether a general AI product could do the same.

## Pros

- **Defined buyer and workflow with real spend**: Banks buy DealCloud and similar tools. (https://www.intapp.com/investment-banking/relationship-management/)
- **Compliance positioning could be a wedge**: General assistants rarely address finance controls.
- **Funding appetite for vertical finance AI**: Metal and F2 raised seed and later rounds.

## Roadmap

### Reach Seed

Three advisory firms paying for pilots.

1. Deploy to two boutique banks with a measurable lift in sourced targets.
2. Complete a security review.

You know it worked when: Three paying firms and a measured increase in outreach to relevant targets.

### Reach Series A

Seat expansion and retention across firms.

1. Get SOC 2 and bank approvals.
2. Integrate with DealCloud and Salesforce.

You know it worked when: Net revenue retention above 110 percent and ten paying firms.
