## General assessment

Menza sells an AI analyst to consumer brands that connects their data sources and looks for revenue gains, cost leaks and anomalies. A team of 3 in London joined the Winter 2026 batch. The wedge into consumer packaged goods is more specific than generic AI analytics, and 2026 seed money is available for AI products. The reference class is not kind: AI analyst tools from earlier batches mostly stayed small, and the one close CPG analytics peer ended in a small sale. The path to Series A runs through paying brands that renew, and the cohort rate of reaching A given a seed is about one in four.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Be a credible team with a sharp CPG wedge.

1. Interview ten brand finance leads and record the leaks they would pay to find.
2. Run the agent on two real brand datasets and write down each dollar finding.

You know it worked when: Two brands agreeing to a paid pilot.

### Reach Seed

Show paying brands and repeat use above the standard YC check.

1. Convert pilots to annual contracts with five brands.
2. Publish an accuracy benchmark with source row citations.

You know it worked when: Five paying brands and a weekly active analyst usage rate above 60 percent.

### Reach Series A

Show a repeatable sales motion and net revenue retention above 110 percent.

1. Hire a first sales lead and build a standard connector kit.
2. Move up to brands above 500M revenue.

You know it worked when: About 1.5M to 2M recurring revenue with growth above 15 percent a month at the start of A.
