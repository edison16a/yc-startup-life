## General assessment

MochaCare sells a managed agentic service for home care agencies, handling hiring, scheduling and intake. The pain is real because caregiver turnover is very high, but the buyers are small and slow, and healthcare cohorts take longer to reach seed and show a low Series A rate. A service model risks low margins, and incumbents such as AlayaCare are adding AI. A seed is roughly a coin flip within a year, with the Series A less likely than the B2B average. A pivot to pure software, or a roll up of agencies, is a plausible upside path, and paying customers can keep the company alive for years.

## Pros

- **Painful, measurable problem**: Turnover around 75 percent makes hiring and scheduling costly for agencies. (https://alayacare.com/blog/the-state-of-home-based-care-what-the-data-actually-shows/)

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

A few paying agencies.

1. Run the service for three agencies.
2. Measure hires and filled shifts.

You know it worked when: Paying pilots with measured savings.

### Reach Seed

Revenue proof.

1. Reach ten agencies.
2. Automate intake and screening.

You know it worked when: About $20K monthly revenue and a seed above $500K.

### Reach Series A

Software margins.

1. Productize workflows.
2. Add integrations with agency systems.

You know it worked when: Gross margin above 60 percent and $1M of annual revenue.

### Reach Series B

Scale in the vertical.

1. Expand to other care types.
2. Build partnerships.

You know it worked when: Revenue growth above 2x a year.

### Reach Series C and beyond

Platform.

1. Consider acquiring agencies or software.
2. Add payments.

You know it worked when: Large multi location customers.
