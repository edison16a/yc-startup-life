## General assessment

Moritz is an Oslo based AI-native law firm claiming same day turnaround on complex deals. The category was hot in 2025, with peers raising multi million rounds, and the team has strong angels, so a seed above $500K is more likely than not. The weak points are services margins, ownership rules and a self reported speed claim. Past tech enabled firms from YC mostly stayed small, so later rounds depend on proving software like leverage. The honest range is wide, because the data on this category is thin and recent.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 6.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting rate is about 1 percent (0.6 to 2 percent band, yc_acceptance.md). Test assumption: the company was accepted, so this note records the adjustments only. Up for a lawyer led team with claimed closed deals, a sharp speed wedge and strong angel backing before the batch. Down because law firm ownership rules limit the structure and a services business is a weaker YC fit. Test note: accepted in fact, so this step is not scored.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly worry that a law firm is a services business with linear headcount growth and regulatory limits on who may own it, so the upside looks capped. A claim of closed deals and fast turnaround is hard to verify, and Oslo based founders serving a global client base may lack a US client channel.
