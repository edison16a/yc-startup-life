## General assessment

Multiplier has an unusually strong early signal, six hedge funds live with long daily use, in a market where AI finance platforms have raised large sums. That makes a seed above the YC check more likely than the cohort average. The risks are a services heavy deployment, a narrow segment of mid size funds, and rivals such as Rogo and Hebbia with far more money. Later steps follow the cohort anchors with a modest uplift, and the upside path is a move upmarket to larger asset managers or a pivot to a horizontal governed agent platform. Reaching a round years later is common for B2B companies with paying customers.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 4.0%

**How we got the number.** In this test the company is assumed accepted to YC for Spring 2026. Prior is about 1 percent. Live usage at six paying looking funds is real traction, and the buyer is clear, which is a strong upward factor, but a three person team in a crowded AI finance space limits it. About 4 percent.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly pass because six small hedge fund customers is a thin market, sales cycles to funds are long, and well funded competitors such as Rogo and Hebbia already serve finance. Partners may doubt that three founders can handle forward deployed custom work. Without a verified result or paying customer in the application, Multiplier looks like many peers such as trata that were weighed against a base rate near 1 percent.
