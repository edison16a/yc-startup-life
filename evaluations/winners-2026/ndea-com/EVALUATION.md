## General assessment

Ndea is a research lab, not a product company, so the main question is whether investors fund a big seed for a 15 person team and whether results arrive. The data shows deep research bets rarely survive without a result, while AI capital is abundant. The ladder therefore starts high at seed and falls sharply afterward, with the upside path coming from a breakthrough or from a pivot to a product that commercializes the technology. Missing funding data is not failure, and reaching a round years later is common for companies with real results, so the later steps are held near the broad cohort rather than the censored recent one.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** In this test the company is assumed accepted to YC for Winter 2026, so this is a short note. Prior is about 1 percent. A 15 person team is far larger than the 2 to 3 person norm, which signals a serious technical group, but a pure research lab without a product is an unusual fit for YC and the pitch gives no evidence about the team, so roughly 3 percent.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly pass because a frontier AGI lab without a product, revenue or a stated path to customers is a poor fit for a three month program, needs capital orders of magnitude beyond the YC check, and the pitch gives no evidence on who the researchers are.
