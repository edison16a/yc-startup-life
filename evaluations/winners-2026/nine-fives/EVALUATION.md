## General assessment

Nine Fives is a three person hardware team aiming at the RF test market, which is large and steady but controlled by Keysight, Rohde and Schwarz and a few others. Industrials companies in recent cohorts reach seed quickly, with a median of three months, but only a minority reach Series A and the sample is small. Hardware sales cycles are long and margins depend on supply chain control, so a seed above $500K within a year is likely while later rounds are less certain. Slow and profitable paths are common for companies with paying customers, and a pivot to software or defense test systems is a plausible upside.

For reference the prior for a random applicant is about 1 percent (references/yc_acceptance.md) and a three person hard tech team targeting a large incumbent market would sit near the upper end of that range.

**Why it fails here.** Not applicable in this test because the company was accepted. If YC had said no, the likely reasons would be unproven founder credentials in RF engineering, no shipped instrument yet, and a capital intensive hardware plan that competes head on with Keysight and Rohde and Schwarz, with no named customer to show demand.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

A working prototype and one named design partner.

1. Build a prototype for a single measurement that engineers dislike on current gear.
2. Interview 30 drone and satellite hardware teams.

You know it worked when: A signed pilot or letter of intent.

### Reach Seed

Paid units and a credible seed story within 12 months.

1. Ship a first instrument to five design partners.
2. Publish accuracy against Keysight gear.

You know it worked when: First $100K of orders and a seed above $500K.

### Reach Series A

Repeat purchases and healthy gross margin.

1. Hire a sales engineer for aerospace accounts.
2. Fix the supply chain to lower cost.

You know it worked when: About $1M of annual revenue with repeat buyers.

### Reach Series B

A scalable sales motion.

1. Expand to a second product line.
2. Sign multi year defense or telecom contracts.

You know it worked when: Revenue growth above 2x a year.

### Reach Series C and beyond

Category leadership in modular RF test.

1. Add software subscriptions on installed instruments.
2. Open international sales.

You know it worked when: Recurring software revenue and positive margins.
