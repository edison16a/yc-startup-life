## General assessment

OpenHack is a solo founder AI security engineer product in the hottest corner of 2026 security investing. Demand is real, since XBOW raised large rounds, and B2B seed rates are near 59% within the cohort, so a seed above $500K is more likely than not. The same heat means many peers, including Escape, CodeAnt, ZeroPath and winfunc, pitch similar tools, and only a few reached Series A in the data. Large vendors can bundle scanning, and a solo founder must show findings and pilots quickly. Later rounds are possible if a wedge emerges, and a pivot to a narrower product is a plausible upside path.
