## General assessment

Orthogonal lets agents discover and pay for APIs per call. The theme is hot and Coinbase and Stripe both launched agent payment protocols, which validates the need but also shows who the competition is. A seed above the YC check is slightly more likely than the B2B anchor because of the theme. Later rounds need real transaction volume, a defensible take rate and a neutral position against giants that can add the same features. The ladder follows the anchors with small adjustments, and most paths end in a stall or a small acquihire.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 99%

**How we got the number.** The company was accepted to YC, which is the premise of the test, so acceptance is near certainty. For reference the random applicant prior is about 1 percent, and agent infrastructure with a technical two person team is squarely in a hot YC theme, but the pitch has no stated traction or distribution.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because a two sided marketplace for API calls needs providers and agent developers at the same time, and protocol builders like Coinbase and Stripe were releasing agent payment standards that could make a neutral aggregator unnecessary. No traction or take rate evidence is stated in the input. The acceptance step is set from the premise that the company was admitted.
