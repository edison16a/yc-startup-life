## General assessment

Patientdesk sells an AI booking and insurance agent to dental practices. Demand is proven, because Weave bought a rival in 2025, but several YC companies and larger vendors chase the same buyer. With five people the likeliest path is a seed, then a hard Series A. Healthcare cohort data is thin with only 24 researched companies. The step odds are anchored on the 2022 to 2025 B2B cohorts and the older 2018 to 2021 cohort, which differ a lot, so treat the later rounds as wide ranges.
