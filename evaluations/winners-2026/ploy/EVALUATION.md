## General assessment

Ploy has only a one line pitch about turning websites into growth engines. A team of 14 hints at an existing business, which helps with a seed. The category is crowded, with specialists like mutiny and bundled features from Webflow and HubSpot, and most peers in the data stayed small or sold early. The base case is a seed or a small exit, with Series A roughly one in four to three. Description is missing, so the forecast has wide uncertainty.
