## General assessment

Rainbrand offers brand context to agents through MCP. The area is hot but the competition already includes Typeface and small open products. With three people and no named customers, the likeliest path is a small seed, then a hard Series A. Comparable YC context companies mostly stopped at seed. Evidence on budgets is thin. The step odds are anchored on the 2022 to 2025 B2B cohorts and the older 2018 to 2021 cohort, which differ a lot, so treat the later rounds as wide ranges.
