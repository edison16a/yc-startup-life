## General assessment

Rational sells accounting as a service done by AI agents, which captures the full fee in a large market and fits the full stack AI services theme investors liked in 2026. The team is two founders with no stated traction, and tax and accounting require licensed signers, trust and accuracy, so the main question is whether the work can really be done without people. AI accounting is crowded with funded companies, and the closest YC analogs raised seeds of a few million and grew slowly. We rate the seed as likely and the Series A below the cohort anchor because of regulatory and seasonal friction. Data is thin on the company itself.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

**How we got the number.** Starting rate about 1 percent (YC investor line, third party range 0.6 to 2 percent). Test setup: already accepted, reference only. Up for a bold, YC friendly full stack AI services idea in a huge market and a large painful cost gap. Down for a two person team, no stated traction and regulatory exposure. Net about 4 percent as a plain applicant.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly worry that tax and accounting are regulated and trust dependent, so a firm with no humans cannot sign returns or take liability, and that AI accounting is already crowded with funded companies such as Basis and Pilot. A two person team with no named customers or filed returns makes the claim of zero humans look like a slogan rather than a working service.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Prove one narrow service works with no human touching the work.

1. Pick a single client type such as small startups and process their books end to end.
2. Resolve who legally signs filings with a licensed partner.

You know it worked when: Ten paying clients with books closed by the AI alone.

### Reach Seed

Show revenue and low error rates to justify a round above 500K.

1. Publish an error rate and human review rate for closed books.
2. Reach monthly revenue from recurring contracts.

You know it worked when: Recurring monthly revenue and under 1 percent corrections.

### Reach Series A

Survive a full tax season at scale.

1. File several hundred returns with automated checks and audit support.
2. Add a second client segment and hire a head of compliance.

You know it worked when: Net retention above 90 percent after a tax season and gross margin above 60 percent.

### Reach Series B

Become a recognized national AI native firm.

1. Expand state coverage, insurance and audit services.
2. Build referral channels with banks and software vendors.

You know it worked when: Revenue growth above 2x with stable margins.
