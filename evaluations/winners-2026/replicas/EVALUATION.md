## General assessment

Replicas rides a strong demand wave for coding agents, which makes a seed round likely under the cohort anchor of 55 to 59 percent. The same wave is the reason later steps are hard: Cursor, Codex and GitHub already ship cloud agents with Slack and issue triggers. I hold the Series A near the 26 percent anchor and expect most value to come from a pivot to environments or an acquisition by a developer platform. These numbers are anchored on the 2022 to 2025 cohorts, whose later rounds are not yet fully observed, so the true rates are probably somewhat higher than the known only figures and the uncertainty is wide.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 5.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting prior is about 1 percent with a 0.6 to 2 percent band (references/yc_acceptance.md). Up: technical founders in the hottest tooling category, a working product and a concrete workflow. Down: a thin layer over models and harnesses owned by labs, and many similar teams applying. Known to have been accepted, so completeness only.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly decline because cloud coding agents are the most crowded application of the 2025 and 2026 AI wave. Labs, GitHub and Cursor ship the same background agent features, and a reviewer sees little defensibility for a wrapper around Claude Code and Codex with two founders and no stated usage numbers.

### Get into YC

Show real engineering team usage.

1. Get 5 teams merging agent PRs weekly.
2. Publish merge rate data.

### Reach Seed

Prove paid retention.

1. Charge per seat.
