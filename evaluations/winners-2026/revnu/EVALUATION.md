## General assessment

Revnu sells an AI growth hire to founders, a useful job in a very crowded and copyable category. The team is three people with no stated traction in the description. Past YC marketing agents show few rounds beyond a small seed and incumbents are adding the same agents. The seed over 500K within a year is a bit under a coin flip and later steps follow the base rates, with the upside path coming from a pivot to a narrower customer or a deeper analytics layer.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 2.5%

**How we got the number.** In this test the company is assumed accepted to YC for the Spring 2026 batch, so the acceptance estimate is a placeholder note. The prior for a random applicant is about 1 percent (0.6 to 2 percent, references/yc_acceptance.md). A three person technical team in the hottest category (AI agents) with a clear paid job would sit above that, but the idea is copyable and crowded, so roughly 2 to 3 percent.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly pass because AI growth agents are a very crowded category with several funded entrants, the pitch has no stated traction, and a general purpose agent could copy the workflow. Interviewers would ask why this team wins distribution against better funded tools and whether founders will really pay for autonomous output.
