## General assessment

River Markets is a prime brokerage layer for event contract traders. Volume in prediction markets exploded through 2025 and early 2026, so a seed above the YC check is likely for a fintech infrastructure team in this wedge. The harder questions come later: two venues hold nearly all volume and can build institutional tools themselves, brokerage needs licenses and capital, and at least four YC companies were already in the same wedge. Fintech cohort data show about a quarter of seeded companies reach a Series A. I forecast a good seed chance, an Unlikely Series A, and acquisition by an exchange or broker as the most common positive outcome.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 12%

**How we got the number.** Test assumes acceptance, so this is a note. Baseline acceptance is low single digits to about 4 percent. A two person fintech infrastructure team in the hottest category of 2026 would be adjusted upward to about 12 percent, but several YC peers (Valence, Sequence Markets, Selfin, Dome) were already in this exact wedge.

**Why it fails here.** YC could say the wedge is already taken by three or four YC companies, that the two largest venues, Kalshi and Polymarket, hold about 95 percent of volume and can ship their own APIs and institutional tools, and that a two person team has no evidence of paying professional customers or regulatory standing for brokerage.

## Pros

- **Strong category tailwind**: Volume growth of about twelvefold in a year and large institutional backing of venues. (https://www.theblock.co/post/374421/kalshi-polymarket-record-1-4-billion-trading-month-institutional-backing-surges)
- **Real fragmentation problem for professionals**: Liquidity is split across venues with different APIs, which is the problem prime brokers solve in other asset classes. (https://www.fia.org/marketvoice/articles/fast-growing-prediction-markets-move-traditional-financial-areas-executives)

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show real professional users trading through the account.

1. Onboard five funds or quant traders on a pilot
2. Publish routed volume and slippage savings

You know it worked when: Five professional accounts and measurable best execution savings

### Reach Seed

Raise on volume and a path to licenses.

1. Sign a licensed broker partner
2. Ship the unified API and order management tools

You know it worked when: $10M monthly routed volume and recurring fees

### Reach Series A

Own institutional flow across venues.

1. Add new venues and tokenized assets
2. Add prime services such as credit and custody

You know it worked when: Revenue above $2M annualized and three venue partnerships
