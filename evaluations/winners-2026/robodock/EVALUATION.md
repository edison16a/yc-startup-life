## General assessment

RoboDock wants to automate charging and checks at depots for electric and autonomous fleets. The need is real as robotaxi and electric fleets scale, but the team has four people, no stated traction and a hard hardware problem. Funding for robotics is available, yet a seed above $500K inside a year depends on a pilot with a named operator. Later steps are discounted because buyers are few, operators may build in house and unit economics against cheap depot labor are unproven. Data on industrials in recent cohorts is tiny, so uncertainty is wide.
