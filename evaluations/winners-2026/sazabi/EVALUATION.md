## General assessment

Sazabi pitches an AI-native observability and security platform to fast-moving engineering teams. The team has ten people and strong angel backing, which raises odds of a seed. The market is large and funded, but Datadog, Grafana and Coralogix are adding AI features and open source tools set low prices. Past YC observability companies mostly raised a seed and then were acquired or stalled, so the Series A hinges on paying customers and a sharp wedge. These numbers are wide because the data is a blind description only and the cohorts are small and right censored.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly pass because observability is a mature, crowded market with Datadog, Grafana and many AI-labeled startups, and AI-native alone is a thin wedge.
