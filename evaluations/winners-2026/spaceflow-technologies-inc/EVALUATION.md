## General assessment

SpaceFlow offers AI assisted procurement services to manufacturers and logistics firms, taking on sourcing and contract work and converting savings into realised savings. The pain is real and the category has budget, but the batch start picture is thin: four people, no named customers and a services model that investors price at lower multiples. Zip shows that software can win in the category, and SourceBeacon shows that a concierge model can fail. A seed is possible if early customers show realised savings, and the later steps depend on moving from services to repeatable software. A stall as a small services firm is the most likely path, and a sale to a procurement vendor is the main alternative.
