## General assessment

Sparkles is a two person London team building a team workspace for coding agents with Slack, GitHub and cloud sandboxes. Demand is high and the AI developer category raises money easily, as the Greptile seed and A show. The danger is that Cursor, OpenAI, Devin and GitHub ship the same workflow, and Sparkles depends on their agents. I put the seed near the AI cohort anchor and shade later stages for platform risk while keeping them within 0.75x of the anchors.

## Pros

- **Right place in a fast growing category**: Background coding agents are being adopted widely and funded, and Greptile reached a $25M A in 2025. (https://siliconangle.com/2025/09/23/greptile-bags-25m-funding-take-coderabbit-graphite-ai-code-validation/)
- **Clear workflow with GitHub safeguards**: Runs end as pull requests, so existing reviews and branch protection stay, which eases enterprise trust. (https://slack.com/blog/developers/coding-agents-in-slack)

## Roadmap

### Reach Seed

Prove organic usage from non engineers.

1. Launch free for small teams with a public demo.
2. Track weekly teams with a merged PR from a non engineer.
3. Sign three design partners.

You know it worked when: Fifty teams active weekly and ten paying.

### Reach Series A

A defensible control layer.

1. Add governance, audit and cost controls across agents.
2. Sell to engineering leaders at mid size companies.
3. Pass SOC 2.

You know it worked when: $1M ARR with net retention above 110 percent.

### Reach Series B

Enterprise scale.

1. Build a sales team.
2. Add self hosted option.
3. Hold margins after model costs.

You know it worked when: Eight figure ARR.
