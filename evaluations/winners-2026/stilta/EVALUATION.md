## General assessment

Stilta sells to a small group of well paid buyers, patent litigators, in a category that investors clearly fund today. The cohort base rate for B2B seed within the YC set is near 59 percent and the legal AI wave pushes the first round to somewhat above that, while the Series A rate of 26 percent is held close to the anchor because the niche is narrow and Solve Intelligence already holds the best distribution. The most likely stop is after seed or Series A, with acquisition by an IP or legal suite as the most common good result.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 5.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting prior is about 1 percent with a 0.6 to 2 percent band (references/yc_acceptance.md). Up: four technical founders who have shipped AI systems at large organisations, a high-value paying buyer, and a wedge (auditability) that answers a real objection of lawyers. Down: no stated legal or IP background, no stated customers, and legal AI is crowded. The company is known to have been accepted, so this is for completeness only.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly pass because the team is consultants without a lawyer or patent examiner on board, the pitch does not state paying customers or pilots, and legal AI already has well funded players such as Harvey and a patent specialist that raised a Series B in late 2025. A reviewer would ask why four engineers in Stockholm win trust with litigation partners in the United States.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show legal credibility and a paying design partner before applying.

1. Sign one or two IP litigation firms as design partners with written feedback.
2. Publish an accuracy benchmark on claim charting against associate work.

You know it worked when: One named firm using it on live matters.

### Reach Seed

Convert pilots into paid seats and a lead round.

1. Price per seat and per matter and close five paying firms.
2. Add a US based legal advisor and a US sales lead.

You know it worked when: Five paying customers and 300K annual recurring revenue.

### Reach Series A

Prove repeatable sales beyond early adopters.

1. Add invalidity and prosecution modules.
2. Build SOC 2 and enterprise security reviews.

You know it worked when: 1.5M to 2M annual recurring with 100 percent net retention.

### Reach Series B

Show a second product line and large customers.

1. Land AmLaw 100 and large in-house teams.
2. Expand to trademark and other IP types.

You know it worked when: 6M to 10M annual recurring.

### Reach Series C and beyond

Show platform status in IP.

1. Build integrations with docketing and portfolio systems.
2. Consider a European and Asian expansion.

You know it worked when: Net revenue retention above 120 percent.
