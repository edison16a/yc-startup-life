## General assessment

Sunflower is a sobriety app with an AI sponsor and a claimed $1.5M run rate, now building a telemedicine clinic for addiction. Revenue at this stage is unusual and the topic attracts Medicaid focused investors, so a seed above $500K within a year is more likely than not. The main risks are numbers that do not reconcile, payer enrollment and licensing that take years, and well funded telehealth peers. Healthcare cohorts reach Series A late and rarely, so later steps are discounted to the cohort range and the data is thin.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 10%

**How we got the number.** Starting rate is about 1 percent (0.6 to 2 percent band, yc_acceptance.md). Test assumption: the company was accepted, so this note records the adjustments only. Up because there is real revenue of about $1.5M run rate, fast user growth and a growth operator founder. Down because the clinic step is a regulated services business and 100 MAU in 6 months is a small base for the stated 800,000 yearly new users. Test note: accepted in fact, so this step is not scored.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because the headline numbers do not agree: 100 monthly users cannot support $1.5M of run rate without extreme pricing, and the 800,000 user pace is a projection. The clinic adds payer contracts, licensing and clinical liability that a consumer growth team has not run before, which looks like a pivot into a harder business.
