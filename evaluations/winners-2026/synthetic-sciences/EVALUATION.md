## General assessment

Synthetic Sciences wants to build infrastructure for autonomous science: tools, evals, training environments and labs. The theme is timely and lab spending on RL is real, so a seed above the YC deal is more likely than not for a credentialed team. But the scope is very broad for four people and the RL environment market is crowded with funded vendors. YC analogs show small RL labs usually stall or are acquired after a seed. The ladder follows B2B anchors with wide uncertainty.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 2.5%

**How we got the number.** Test note: this company was accepted to YC, so this is only the forward estimate. Starting prior is about 1 percent for a random applicant (0.6 to 2 percent third party range, per references/yc_acceptance.md). Research lab on RL and verification for science is a priority topic for YC and AI labs (up), four person team with research ambitions (neutral to up), vague product with no revenue and a very broad scope (down), hot but crowded RL environment space (down). Net about 2.5 percent.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most likely say the scope is too broad for four people, spanning tooling, evals, training environments, algorithms and labs, with no customer or product. The RL environment market is crowded with funded vendors, and a research lab without revenue is hard to evaluate in an interview.
