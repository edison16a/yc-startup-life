## General assessment

Tolmo is a twelve person Spring 2026 company selling agentic security that triages, verifies and fixes issues across code, CI and cloud using a live knowledge graph. Interest and funding in this theme are strong and a larger team is an advantage, so a seed is likely. The risks are a crowded field of funded startups and incumbents, enterprise caution about autonomous changes to production, and a heavy integration load. The most likely path is a seed in 2026 and a Series A that depends on paid enterprise contracts.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 6.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting from the roughly 1 percent prior (range 0.6 to 2 percent). The company is already accepted, so this is a note: a twelve person team is large for the batch, the topic of AI security agents is a strong fit for current YC interest, and the product claims production integration. Crowded competition and no stated customers hold it down.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because AI agents for security triage and remediation are a crowded theme with many funded startups, and the pitch is broad, covering code, CI and cloud, without naming a customer or a proven result, so the interview would test whether Tolmo is anything other than a feature of larger platforms.
