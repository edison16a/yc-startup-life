## General assessment

Tsenta is an AI agent that applies to jobs for consumers and claims about $3M annualized revenue and 90,000 users. That traction makes a seed round more likely than for a typical consumer company. The weaknesses are natural churn as people find jobs, dependence on platforms that ban bots, and easy copying by LinkedIn and others. Consumer job tools in the YC data such as Simplify and Hiration stayed small, and Pathrise ended in an acquisition, so a Series A depends on retention and expansion to employers.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

The applicant prior is about 1 percent, and a company reporting about $3M annualized revenue would sit far above it, if the claim holds.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly pass if the revenue was seen as low-quality consumer subscription with high churn and refunds, and if auto-apply tools were viewed as spam against employers. Platform bans from LinkedIn and application systems make the model fragile. The interview would test whether the founders can show proof of demand, speed of shipping and a reason the market is open now for Tsenta.

### Reach Seed

Turn growth into a durable engine.

1. Add retention features beyond application
2. Diversify away from LinkedIn dependence

You know it worked when: $3M annualized revenue sustained for 6 months

### Reach Series A

Expand beyond job seekers.

1. Launch an employer side product
2. Show payback period under 3 months
