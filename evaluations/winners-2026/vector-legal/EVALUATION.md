## General assessment

Vector sells legal services to startups with senior lawyers and custom AI. In the winter 2026 cohort of 199 companies it joined a hot area: AI native law firms raised large rounds in 2025. The most likely path is a seed round of a few million dollars, then a harder Series A where investors ask whether margins look like software or like a firm. Atrium is the closest failure, and Draftwise shows legal AI selling software can raise a Series A. Data behind the numbers is thin because the cohort of 2022 to 2025 B2B companies has few Series B examples.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Roughly 1 percent base rate with a 0.6 to 2 percent band (references/yc_acceptance.md). Test is conditional on acceptance, so this is a note only: the company is a services business with real revenue and senior lawyers, and AI-native law firms were a visible funded category in late 2025, which pushes the estimate up from the base rate.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly worry that a law firm with software layered on is a services margin business that does not scale like software, and that the regulatory structure around lawyer ownership limits the equity story. The estimate leans on the cohort base rates in stage_benchmarks.md, where later rounds are rare and slow, and on what happened to comparable YC companies such as atrium and draftwise.

- **The category is funded and validated by investors in 2025.**: Crosby and Eudia rounds. (https://www.artificiallawyer.com/2025/10/08/hybrid-ai-law-firm-crosby-raises-20m-cooley-invests/)

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show paying startup clients and testimonials.

1. Convert existing client work into a recurring subscription plan.
2. Publish turnaround time and price versus Big Law.

You know it worked when: Monthly recurring revenue and net retention from the first 20 clients.

### Reach Seed

Prove software gross margin.

1. Automate drafting for priced rounds and contracts.
2. Track revenue per lawyer.

You know it worked when: Gross margin well above a normal firm.

### Reach Series A

Win a narrow category.

1. Own international flips and financings.
2. Add data room and cap table products.

You know it worked when: Repeatable revenue above 3M annualized.
