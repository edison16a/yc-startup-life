## General assessment

Volaren is a four person team building a text first AI agent for personal investing, with copy trading and user built agents. Demand for AI help in investing is real, but Robinhood and Public both launched agent trading in 2026 with huge user bases. Consumer fintech cohorts show about 57 percent reaching a seed and 23 percent an A, and investing app analogs such as Finary and TAI stalled without a channel. I stay near those anchors, with a small discount to seed for the crowded field and regulatory risk, and keep later stages within 0.75x of the base rates.

## Roadmap

### Reach Seed

Show retention of funded users.

1. Launch a text first beta with 500 users.
2. Track weekly active funded accounts.
3. Get a counsel opinion on advice rules.

You know it worked when: Thirty percent four week retention among funded users.

### Reach Series A

Unit economics and license path.

1. Add a paid tier.
2. Partner with a registered broker.
3. Cut acquisition cost with creators.

You know it worked when: $100M assets and revenue per user above CAC.

### Reach Series B

Scale or sell the engine.

1. Offer the agent to brokers via API.
2. Add products by region.
3. Build compliance staff.

You know it worked when: Eight figure revenue.
