## General assessment

Vorflux is a cloud coding agent that takes tickets to pull requests with QA video evidence. The market is large and growing, with Gartner counting about $10B and the leaders growing fast. The same facts make it the hardest category to stand out in, because Anthropic, OpenAI, Cursor and GitHub ship similar features. A seed above the YC check is as likely as the AI cohort base rate. Later rounds depend on a clear wedge, as Greptile found in code review. About 50 percent of forecast paths end in a stall or shutdown.

## Pros

- **Very large, fast growing market**: Gartner estimates about $10B annualized in 2026. (https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-20-gartner-says-the-market-for-enterprise-ai-coding-agents-is-entering-a-new-phase-of-expansion-and-competitive-realignment)
- **Specific and complete product vision**: The description includes QA with video, model routing and onboarding help, which suggests a working product.
- **Neutral across models is a real enterprise want**: Bring your own model is in the pitch and enterprises avoid vendor lock in.

## Roadmap

### Reach Seed

Ten teams in weekly use and a clear reason to pick this over bundled agents.

1. Win ten engineering teams with hands on harness setup.
2. Publish results on merged pull requests per week and bug escape rate.

You know it worked when: Ten paying teams and a measured gain in merged work.

### Reach Series A

Repeatable enterprise sales and net revenue retention.

1. Add SSO, audit logging and compliance.
2. Prove gross margin with model routing.

You know it worked when: Annual recurring revenue above $1M and gross margin above 60 percent.
