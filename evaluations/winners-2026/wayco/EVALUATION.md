## General assessment

Wayco builds a data layer for the medical side of legal cases, from intake to settlement. Plaintiff firms already pay for AI tools, legal AI has drawn large funding, and YC analogs like Hona reached a Series A, so a seed above the YC deal is more likely than not. The risk is crowding by EvenUp and Supio and the difficulty of getting medical data. The ladder sits slightly above the B2B cohort anchors at seed and A because the category is investor friendly, with wide uncertainty.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 2.0%

**How we got the number.** Test note: this company was accepted to YC, so this is only the forward estimate. Starting prior is about 1 percent for a random applicant (0.6 to 2 percent third party range, per references/yc_acceptance.md). Vertical legal and medical AI with a five person team (larger than typical, up), a defined paying buyer in plaintiff law firms (up), strong market timing from funded legal AI categories (up), but a crowded space (down). Net about 2 percent.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most likely say the medical legal records space is already served by well funded players such as EvenUp and Supio and by record retrieval vendors, and that the 'unified data intelligence layer' pitch is broad. Without proof of paying law firms or insurers and a clear wedge, partners may prefer a narrower product.
