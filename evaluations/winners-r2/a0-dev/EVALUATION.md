## General assessment

a0.dev generates React Native apps with AI for mobile developers. The founders have real app traction but the tool business is unproven and competes with many funded builders and general coding assistants. The window is 22.4 months from 2025-01-06 to 2026-11-17, long enough for most seeds, so the seed odds sit near the developer tools anchor of 55 percent. Series A given seed is about 10 percent in the window. The odds do not rank this company against others.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting prior is about 1 percent for a random applicant (0.6 to 2 percent range, references/yc_acceptance.md). Up for shipped consumer apps with hundreds of thousands of users, which show execution (real usage factor) and technical founders. Down because AI app generation is crowded and the pitch is vague about who pays. Net about 3 percent, conditional on the batch already given.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most likely say that AI generation of mobile apps is crowded and may be absorbed by general coding assistants, and that the founders' own consumer apps are a different business from the tool they now pitch. Without paying customers for the tool, partners may see a demo more than a company.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Prove developers pay for the tool.

1. Launch the tool to a waitlist of mobile developers
2. Charge from the first week

You know it worked when: First hundred paying developers.

### Reach Seed

Show retention and growth in paid subscriptions.

1. Ship a differentiating workflow such as store submission
2. Track monthly cohort retention

You know it worked when: Monthly recurring revenue growing month on month with retained cohorts.
