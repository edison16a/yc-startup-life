## General assessment

Aegis automates health insurance denial appeals with AI agents for provider billing teams. The pain is large and measurable, but the category was already crowded by April 2025 and the team is three people with no stated customers. Inside a 19 month window the seed probability follows the Healthcare cohort timing, not a lift for the hot sector. A Series A inside the window is unlikely. The odds do not rank Aegis against other companies. If it raises, the most likely size is a $3M to $6M institutional seed, and the paths that matter most are proof of recovered dollars, a few paying billing teams and a clear edge over large revenue cycle vendors that can copy the idea within a few quarters.

## Pros

- **Clear dollar pain and measurable ROI**: Denials cost providers billions and the appeal cost is quantified at $57 per claim, so recovery can be sold on results. (https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/strategy/outsmart-payer-ai-a-better-approach-to-denials-management/)
- **Healthcare AI tailwind**: Revenue cycle automation attracts funding and Candid Health and Arintra from YC reached later rounds. (https://www.hfma.org/fast-finance/ai-automation-payer-denials-management/)

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show real recovered revenue from at least a few billing teams

1. Run paid pilots with 3 billing companies on one denial type
2. Measure overturn rate and dollars recovered per claim

You know it worked when: Documented dollars recovered above software cost

### Reach Seed

Repeatable recovery results across payers

1. Publish case studies with overturn rates
2. Add integrations with the top 2 billing systems
3. Sign 10 paying customers

You know it worked when: $30K or more monthly revenue and 10 live customers

### Reach Series A

Hospital system contracts and a sales motion

1. Hire a healthcare enterprise seller
2. Get SOC 2 and HIPAA audits done

You know it worked when: $1M annual recurring revenue with low churn
