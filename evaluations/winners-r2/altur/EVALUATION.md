## General assessment

Altur builds voice agents that call debtors for lenders. Lenders pay for collection calls today, so need is high, but regulation puts liability on lenders and slows pilots, and several well funded voice collections companies are ahead. The seed estimate for the 16 month window is near the AI B2B anchor discounted for time, and does not rank this company against others. Series A inside the window is unlikely. Voice analogs in the data mostly ended in acquisition or stalled.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 50%

**How we got the number.** The company is already in the Summer 2025 batch, so this is a note only. A random applicant starts near 1 percent. Nine people at application time, a concrete paying buyer and a voice AI wave that YC favored would lift a cold application well above that, though an unregulated looking idea in a rule heavy industry would be questioned.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because voice collections is a compliance heavy category with many AI entrants already in YC, and a one line pitch gives little sign of why this team wins. Reviewers would ask about recovery rates against human agents, call consent rules and whether customers are lenders in Mexico or the United States.

## Pros

- **Clear buyer with existing call spend**: Lenders and agencies already pay for human collections calls and dialers. (https://www.retellai.com/blog/fdcpa-compliance-automated-calls)
- **Larger team than most batch companies**: Nine people at the start can ship and sell in parallel.

## Roadmap

### Reach Seed

Show recovery uplift with two paying lenders.

1. Run head to head pilots against a human team with measured recovery rate.
2. Ship consent management and audit logs.

You know it worked when: Two lenders on paid contracts with a recovery rate gain.

### Reach Series A

Repeatable enterprise sales.

1. Sign a larger lender or agency with volume commitments.
2. Expand to a second country.

You know it worked when: Several hundred thousand dollars of monthly recurring revenue and logo retention.
