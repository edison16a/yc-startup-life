## General assessment

Arlo Industries proposes a passive sensor mesh that tracks drones better than radar. Demand is real, as Ukraine and Europe pay for cheap wide area detection and defense investors were active in 2026. The company has three people and no stated revenue, and the claim that tracking accuracy rises exponentially with sensor count is unproven. Comparable YC defense companies such as Guardian RF and Milliray show the wedge is crowded and slow. The window from batch start to the outcome date is 7.4 months, so only a quick seed is possible, and later rounds are very unlikely inside it.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 4.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting rate of about 1 percent from references/yc_acceptance.md. Up for a founder with direct experience of the threat, a PhD teammate and demos shown to Ukrainian operators, and for YC interest in defense hardware. Down for a three person team on a physically hard claim and for hardware capital needs. Already listed in Spring 2026, so the ladder is the "if accepted" view.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say the technical claim is unproven: tracking stealthy drones with passive sensors that beat radar is a bold statement, and without independent field data from the team or a paying military customer the partners cannot tell a real advance from a research idea, while hardware also requires more capital than a typical YC check.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show tracking accuracy on real drones.

1. Run a field test with at least 20 sensors against known drone flights
2. Record the accuracy versus radar and share the data with two military contacts

You know it worked when: A written field report with positional error figures

### Reach Seed

Turn demos into a paid pilot inside 7 months.

1. Sign a paid pilot with a Ukrainian, European or US end user
2. Raise a seed from defense focused investors using the pilot as proof

You know it worked when: A signed paid pilot above $100K and a seed round above $1M

### Reach Series A

Move from pilot to recurring deployments.

1. Deliver a certified sensor kit at a repeatable unit cost
2. Win a program or framework contract with a government buyer

You know it worked when: Contract backlog above $5M
