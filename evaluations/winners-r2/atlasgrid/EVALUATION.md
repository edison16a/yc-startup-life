## General assessment

AtlasGrid sells screen graphs with analytics and agent insights to mobile product teams. The 13.4 month window gives about two fifths seed odds from the cohort anchor, and a Series A in that window is unlikely. The main risks are incumbents and an unclear buyer. The odds do not rank this company against others. The six person team sells to a buyer that is not yet clear, designers, product managers or growth teams, and sits between design libraries and analytics suites. The cohort anchors do not separate winners from losers in the earlier winners test, so treat the number as a base rate, not a ranking of AtlasGrid against other companies.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** AtlasGrid is already in Fall 2025, so shown for completeness. Starting rate about 1 percent. A team of six and a concrete product help; the niche and incumbents hurt. About 3 percent.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because the buyer is unclear (designers, PMs or growth) and there are free or cheap alternatives such as Mobbin and Sensor Tower. A reviewer would ask which team pays and what it replaces, and the one liner Accelerate Product Iteration does not say.
