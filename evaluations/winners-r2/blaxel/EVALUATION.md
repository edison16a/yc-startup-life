## General assessment

Blaxel sells sandboxes and storage primitives for autonomous AI agents with a team of 12 and a live product. Agent infrastructure drew heavy investor attention in 2025 and the YC data shows serverless AI platforms such as Beam and Cerebrium raising seeds within a year, but also Mystic and Cakework ending without a clear next round. In a 19 month window the anchor puts a seed above $1M near 50 percent and a Series A inside the window near 12 percent given seed. Hyperscalers and E2B, Modal and Daytona are the main threats. Odds here do not rank this company against others.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 2.0%

**How we got the number.** The company is in the Spring 2025 batch, so this is shown for completeness. Start from the roughly 1 percent prior for a random applicant (0.6 to 2 percent range). Adjust up for a twelve person team with a working product, an infrastructure wedge that fits YC's AI agent interest and technical credibility, then hold near 2 percent because no revenue is stated.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because agent sandboxes are a crowded, capital heavy category: E2B, Modal and Daytona already ship similar primitives, and hyperscalers can add microVM sandboxes. Without named paying customers or usage growth, the partners may see a performance claim of 25 ms resume rather than a reason developers switch.
