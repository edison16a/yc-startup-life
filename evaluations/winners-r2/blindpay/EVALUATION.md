## General assessment

BlindPay offers a stablecoin API for global payments with the compliance work handled for clients. The window is 22.4 months from 2025-01-06 to 2026-11-17, which covers nearly all eventual seeds, so seed sits at 0.55 against Fintech and payments anchors of 0.57 and 0.70. Series A given seed is 0.10 in the window. Funded rivals and licensing are the main drags. Earlier stablecoin YC companies Meson and Peg shut down, while Verto and NALA took years to reach a Series A, which is why the later rounds are priced low for a window under two years. The odds do not rank this company against others.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 4.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting prior about 1 percent (0.6 to 2 percent range). Up for a nine person team, a payments need that is clear and timing with rising stablecoin interest, and for a developer product in an API format that YC often funds. Down because the space has funded rivals and a compliance wrapped crypto pitch. Net about 4 percent, conditional on the batch already given.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say that stablecoin payment APIs are a crowded field with funded leaders such as Bridge and BVNK, and that a compliance wrapper depends on partners the company does not control. Partners may also want proof of live payment volume instead of a regulatory plan.
