## General assessment

Blueshoe is a law firm run with AI tools, serving consumers and plaintiffs. Plaintiff AI tooling has raised large rounds, which helps sentiment, but the money has gone to software vendors, and the closest firm analog, Spire Law, never raised past the YC deal. The seed estimate for the 19 month window sits near the B2B cohort anchor and does not rank this company against others. A Series A inside the window is unlikely because case cycles are long. The path that worked in the data was Darrow's, a software and data business sold to firms. A pivot toward licensable software is the main upside route.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 50%

**How we got the number.** The company is already in the Spring 2025 batch, so this is a note only. A random applicant starts near 1 percent (references/yc_acceptance.md). A law firm model run by two founders is unusual, and ownership rules for law firms limit outside investors, which would pull a cold application down, while the plaintiff AI theme and a clear paying customer type pull it up.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because a firm that owns case outcomes is a services business with contingency risk and licensing limits, not software. Reviewers would ask whether two founders include a licensed trial lawyer, how cases are sourced, and why this is not simply a lawyer using ChatGPT.

## Pros

- **Plaintiff AI is a funded, fast moving category**: Eve, Supio and Finch all raised from top funds in 2024 and 2025. (https://www.lawnext.com/2025/01/eve-intelligent-partner-for-pi-firms-raises-47m-in-round-led-by-major-vc-firm-andreessen-horowitz.html)
- **The firm owns the customer relationship and outcome data**: A firm gets case outcome data that tool vendors only see in part, which can power better intake and pricing.

## Roadmap

### Reach Seed

A visible case pipeline with settled results.

1. Sign a steady flow of consumer and plaintiff matters through a compliant entity.
2. Publish cycle time and settlement metrics against typical firms.

You know it worked when: Dozens of signed cases and the first settlements, with cost per case below the traditional average.

### Reach Series A

Show software like economics.

1. Raise revenue per lawyer through automation of intake and drafting.
2. Open a second case type or state.

You know it worked when: Revenue per attorney well above peers with stable margins.
