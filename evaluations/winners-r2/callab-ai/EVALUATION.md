## General assessment

Callab AI is an enterprise contact center platform that pairs AI agents with live human supervision and its own telephony stack. Buyers already spend on contact center labor and voice AI funding is large, but Vapi, Retell and incumbents compete and the delivery model looks services heavy. For a 7 month window the seed odds are set to about 38 percent, derived from a 64 percent ever anchor for AI companies and the 6 month median time to seed; the odds do not rank this company against others. Series A inside the window is only about 4 percent. The largest risks are margins from custom implementation, voice platform crowding and long enterprise sales cycles. The path that would change the numbers is a named reference customer and software margins.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Start from the roughly 1 percent applicant prior (band 0.6 to 2 percent). Adjust up about 2x for a team of seven that has built its own carrier grade telephony stack and says it already serves regulated enterprise customers, and for the fit with YC's interest in AI voice. Adjust down for a very crowded voice AI field and a services heavy delivery model that looks less like a product. Net about 3 percent.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say this reads as a large feature list in a category with many funded voice agent companies, and that building and implementing every agent for each customer looks like consulting rather than software. Without named customers or revenue in the application, a partner cannot see why this team wins against Vapi style platforms or incumbent contact center vendors.

## Pros

- **Human in the loop supervision answers the main enterprise fear of unsupervised AI**: The product lets supervisors watch, take over and hand back, which regulated buyers ask for. (https://enthu.ai/blog/voice-ai-agents-contact-centers/)
- **Legacy PBX connectors reach buyers cloud only rivals skip**: Native Cisco, Mitel, Avaya and SIP trunk support lets enterprises adopt without cloud migration, per the company description. (https://callab.ai)

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show paid enterprise deployments with measured containment and satisfaction.

1. Record call containment rate and human handoff rate for each live customer.
2. Obtain a written SOC 2 report or progress letter.

You know it worked when: Two enterprise customers paying monthly with a stated cost per resolved call.

### Reach Seed

Show software margins, not services revenue.

1. Make the AI builder usable by customer staff alone.
2. Price by usage or per seat of supervisor.

You know it worked when: $30K or more monthly recurring revenue with gross margin above 60 percent.

### Reach Series A

Repeatable enterprise sales on legacy PBX estates.

1. Package the Cisco, Mitel and Avaya connectors as the wedge into on-premises customers.
2. Hire two enterprise account executives.

You know it worked when: $1.5M annual recurring revenue across ten or more accounts.

### Reach Series B

Scale beyond custom implementation.

1. Build self serve onboarding for mid market.
2. Add channel partners such as telecom resellers.

You know it worked when: $10M annual recurring revenue with net retention above 115 percent.

### Reach Series C and beyond

Be a platform that contact centers standardize on.

1. Expand to quality management and analytics.
2. Add international carrier coverage.

You know it worked when: $40M or more annual recurring revenue.
