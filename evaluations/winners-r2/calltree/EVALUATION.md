## General assessment

Calltree sells enterprise voice and chat support agents that use internal tools. Investor appetite for voice AI is high, but Vapi, Synthflow and YC peers already raised and model vendors are moving in. Inside a 22 month window the seed probability follows the B2B timing curve, not a lift for the sector. A Series A inside the window is possible but less likely than not. The odds do not rank Calltree against other companies. The forecast window runs from the as of date to 2026-11-17, and later rounds are only counted if they close inside it.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because voice support agents were the most crowded AI category in late 2024, with several YC companies already in it, and the input offers no customers, revenue or proof that its internal tool use beats peers.
