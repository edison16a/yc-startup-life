## General assessment

Caseflood.ai appears to sell AI intake for law firms, a job with a measurable dollar value since firms pay for leads and lose cases to slow response. The record gives no description, customers or revenue, so the odds rest on the cohort: the B2B anchor for a seed is 65 percent ever and about 52 percent for recent batches, and a 22 month window trims little, so I use 45 percent, below the anchor for a two person team with no stated traction. The odds do not rank this company against others. Series A inside the window is about 10 percent. Main risks are a thin copyable product, funded legal AI companies moving into intake, and a small slow buyer base. A pivot into a broader case workflow would be the upside path.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 2.0%

**How we got the number.** Start from the roughly 1 percent applicant prior (band 0.6 to 2 percent). Adjust up about 1.5x for a clear vertical with a paid job and a sales led wedge, a fit with YC's interest in vertical AI agents. Adjust down for two founders with no stated traction, no description, and an intake category that already has funded legal AI rivals. Net about 2 percent.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say the pitch is one line with no evidence of customers, and that AI intake and call answering for law firms is a busy area with many small vendors and a funded legal AI player moving into intake. Without proof that firms pay or that the founders have legal sales access, interviewers may see a thin wrapper.

## Pros

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Prove firms pay for AI intake.

1. Sign three paying law firms and log signed cases per 100 calls.
2. Write a clear product description and customer quote.

You know it worked when: Three firms paying and a conversion lift over their baseline.

### Reach Seed

Show repeatable sales into plaintiff firms.

1. Choose one practice area and sell pay per signed case.
2. Integrate with two case management systems.

You know it worked when: $25K monthly recurring revenue across 15 firms.

### Reach Series A

Become the intake system of record for plaintiff firms.

1. Add marketing attribution and follow up automation.
2. Build a partner channel with legal marketing agencies.

You know it worked when: $1.5M annual recurring revenue across 200 firms.

### Reach Series B

Expand from intake into the case lifecycle.

1. Add case tracking and client messaging.
2. Enter a second practice area.

You know it worked when: $10M annual recurring revenue.

### Reach Series C and beyond

Own legal client acquisition operations.

1. Add international or large firm product.
2. Acquire a legal marketing service.

You know it worked when: $35M or more annual recurring revenue.
