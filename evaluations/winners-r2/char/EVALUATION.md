## General assessment

Char is an open source notepad where typing a checkbox triggers an agent to do the work. The idea is simple and fits where investors are putting money, as Granola's 2025 round showed. The weakness is that Notion, Microsoft and Granola can add the same feature, and several YC note and meeting analogs died or sold small. Over the 16 month window the chance of a round above $1M sits near the cohort window anchor. The odds do not rank this company against others.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

**How we got the number.** Start from about 1 percent with a band of 0.6 to 2 percent (references/yc_acceptance.md). Up: open source with a visible community, a team of three, a distinct idea of tasks completed from checkboxes. Down: productivity and notes is a crowded space with many YC deaths, consumer style usage often lacks repeat paid use (consumer 35 percent inactive), and the founders are based in Seoul and remote. Net about 3 percent.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because AI notepads and meeting assistants are already crowded and the pitch reads as a feature of Notion or Granola. Without strong usage numbers or a paying segment, interviewers would doubt that this notepad beats general assistants on the problems that matter.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show active daily usage and an example of completed tasks.

1. Publish the open source repo and track weekly active users
2. Collect examples of tasks completed without edits

You know it worked when: 1,000 weekly active users or 30 daily users completing tasks

### Reach Seed

Paid conversion and a clear segment.

1. Launch paid plans and measure conversion
2. Choose one segment such as founders or sales teams and ship a template for it

You know it worked when: $10K monthly recurring revenue and paid retention above 80 percent after three months

### Reach Series A

Prove the agent is the reason users stay, not the notes.

1. Show task completion rates and time saved
2. Add team plans with shared workspaces

You know it worked when: $1M annual recurring revenue growing 15 percent a month

## Assumptions and limits

- Team of three, open source product, no disclosed traction as of 2025-07-07.
- Web results dated after the as of date were ignored.
- The seed probability is for a window of 16.3 months and a round above about $1M. It does not rank this company against others: in the 2026 winners test the average seed probability was 0.65 for companies that raised and 0.62 for companies confirmed to have raised nothing beyond the YC deal.
- Series B and C plus steps are for ever reaching the round, not inside the window. Reaching a round years later is common for companies with paying customers.
- Knowledge base coverage: calibration cohorts are 2022 to 2025 B2B (n=150 researched), with funding coverage partial for most, so known-only rates are biased low.
