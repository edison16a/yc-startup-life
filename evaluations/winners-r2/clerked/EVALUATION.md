## General assessment

Clerked automates AP with AI agents controlled from chat. The forecast window is 13.4 months from 2025-10-06 to 2026-11-17. We estimate 0.38 for an outside round above about $1M closing in the window, from the 2022 to 2025 B2B anchor of 0.59 scaled for timing, with no hype lift. The analogs show AP companies commonly sit at the YC deal for a long time (Mercoa, Saldor) or need a niche (Plate IQ). These odds do not rank this company against others. Reaching a round later is common for companies with paying customers.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 1.5%

**How we got the number.** Prior of about 1 percent (yc_acceptance.md, range 0.6 to 2 percent). Up for a clear painful workflow, an AI native angle fitting YC priorities and a 4 person team; down because AP automation has strong incumbents and many YC entrants and no traction is stated. Net about 1.5 percent. The company is already in the Fall 2025 batch, shown for completeness.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly pass because AP automation is crowded with BILL, Tipalti, Ramp and earlier YC companies, and 'most advanced AI clerks in the world' is an unproven claim. Partners would want evidence that finance teams pay for a chat driven AP product rather than extending the system they already use.
