## General assessment

Confluence Labs aims at sample efficient AI for experimental science. The topic is attractive to specialist funds and large AI lab rounds in the category show appetite, but the pitch is a research direction with no product, and funded labs and established vendors occupy the same ground. The 10.4 month window caps the odds, and the seed figure sits slightly above the window adjusted cohort anchor, so the odds do not rank this company against others. Series A inside the window is unlikely.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 2.0%

Two technical founders and a topic aligned with AI for science, a priority area for frontier investors, move it up about 2 times. No product, traction or named customer holds it near 2 percent. The company is described as in the Winter 2026 batch, so this is shown for completeness.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because the application describes a research agenda with no product, customer or revenue path, and the domain needs wet labs, partners and years of validation. Partners may doubt that two people can beat well funded autonomous science labs, and the vision statement does not show which buyer pays first.
