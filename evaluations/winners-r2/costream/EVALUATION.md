## General assessment

Costream's input is one line about indexing human dialogue through digital products, with no description, so the forecast leans on cohort rates. The team of 8 is large for a Summer 2025 company and voice and conversation AI were attracting capital in 2025. Consumer cohorts have a high ever rate of seed but poor retention, and the closest analogs stayed at the YC check or sold early. Over the 16.3 month window a seed above $1M is close to even, set near the Consumer cohort pace, and a Series A inside the window is a small tail. These odds do not rank Costream against others, and the thin description widens the uncertainty. The likely good case is a seed of $3M to $6M if usage shows retention.
