## General assessment

Cranston sells AI tax prep and bookkeeping to CPA firms. The need is real because firms face a talent shortage, and YC has seen several companies in the same lane, with seeds in the $2M to $5M range. Over a 13.4 month window the cohort anchor for a B2B company reaching a seed above $1M is about 0.44 ever with most of that inside a year, and I use 0.55. The odds do not rank this company against others. The main risks are accuracy and liability, incumbent bundling and a seasonal revenue cycle.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Start from about 1 percent for a random applicant (published figure 0.6 to 2 percent, knowledge/yc_acceptance.md). Adjust up for a team of 8 with a working agent product and a vertical where AI for accounting has YC precedent (Soraban, Fondo, Finta, TaxGPT), and for a clear paid wedge; adjust down because AI accounting and tax is a crowded YC lane. Net about 3 percent. The company is already in the Fall 2025 batch, so this is shown for completeness.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say the pitch reads like several AI accounting companies already funded by YC and others: tax prep and bookkeeping for firms is a well-trodden idea, the description claims a broad firm operating system with no revenue or customer number, and incumbents such as Thomson Reuters and Intuit are shipping AI features. Without a founder with CPA firm experience or a signed pilot list the interview could end in no.

## Pros

- **Real paid pain: CPA firms lack staff in busy season.**: Thomson Reuters and other vendors describe capacity as the main driver of AI spending. (https://tax.thomsonreuters.com/blog/a-tax-automation-playbook-for-accounting-firms/)
- **Team of 8 gives more build capacity than most batch peers.**: Input lists team size 8 in San Francisco. (https://www.cranston.ai)

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show returns that reviewers accept with little rework.

1. Run 5 to 10 pilot firms on live returns
2. Publish a per-return error and review time metric

You know it worked when: Reviewer edit rate per return and hours saved

### Reach Seed

Paying firms and a filing-season result by early 2026.

1. Convert pilots to annual contracts before season
2. Sell bookkeeping year round to cover runway
3. Pitch funds that backed AI for accounting with the season metrics

You know it worked when: $300K to $1M ARR and a signed seed term sheet above $1M
