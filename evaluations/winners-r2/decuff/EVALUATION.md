## General assessment

decuff serves abliterated open source models by API with zero data retention. The demand signal is community usage of uncensored models and security testing, but paid demand is unproven and the product is the same weights that larger inference hosts serve. A solo founder, controversial positioning and a short 7.4 month window all push the seed odds below the cohort anchor, and the odds do not rank this company against others in the batch. Later stages are low because Series A takes a median of two years.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 1.0%

**How we got the number.** Start from the roughly 1 percent prior (0.6 to 2 percent band in yc_acceptance.md). A solo founder pulls it down slightly (shutdown 21 percent versus 17 percent for pairs), a copyable inference wrapper with no wedge pulls it down, and a controversial positioning with legal and payment risk pulls it down further. The company is described as in the Spring 2026 batch, so this is shown for completeness.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because the product is open source models behind an API with a policy stance as its only difference, and the stance carries abuse, payment processor and legal risk that partners may not want to underwrite. A solo founder with no named customers or revenue also leaves team capacity and focus unproven.
