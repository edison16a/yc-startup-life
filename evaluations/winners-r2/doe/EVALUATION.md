## General assessment

Doe promises agents that do your work across connected tools. Investor appetite in 2025 is real, with seeds of $12M and $13M, but those rounds went to teams with named backgrounds, and the product here is horizontal with no wedge, vendor bundling risk and no stated traction. The odds do not rank this company against others in the batch.
