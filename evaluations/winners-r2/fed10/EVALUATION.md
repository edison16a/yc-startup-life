## General assessment

Fed10 is a legislative consulting firm staffed by AI agents, run by three former lobbyists. In a 10.4 month window the B2B cohort anchor of 0.61 for ever reaching seed shrinks to about 0.38, because most seeds arrive later than that. Series A inside the window is about 0.04. Quorum and FiscalNote already sell AI bill tracking, and close analogs such as GovPredict, Parsagon and Axis stayed small or exited low. Founder market fit helps, but the odds here do not rank this company against others.

## Assumptions and limits

- Blind forecast made as of 2026-01-05 using only the YC batch description in the input file, with market-only web searches.
- Forecast window is 10.4 months, from 2026-01-05 to 2026-11-17.
- Seed means an outside financing round above about $1M that closes inside the window, not the standard YC deal of about $500K; the seed and Series A probabilities are window probabilities, not ever probabilities.
- The odds do not rank this company against other companies in the batch: in the 2026 winners test, team size, claimed traction and a hot sector did not separate raisers from non raisers, so the seed number stays near the cohort anchor.
- Cohort anchors come from older and 2022 to 2024 batches and are partly right censored; funding coverage in the data is incomplete (lower, known and upper ranges quoted).
