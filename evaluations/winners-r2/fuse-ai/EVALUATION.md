## General assessment

Fuse AI bundles data, enrichment and AI sequencing for sales teams, a wedge with strong paid demand but heavy competition from Clay, Apollo and many AI SDR startups. For 2022 and later B2B YC companies about 59 percent reach a seed and most do so inside 22 months, so the seed estimate is 50 percent for the window. Direct YC analogs (Ciro, Persana AI, Bluebirds) raised a seed then sold, which pulls the Series A chance well below the anchor. The odds do not rank this company against others.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting prior is about 1 percent for a random applicant (0.6 to 2 percent, references/yc_acceptance.md). Up for a team of twelve, which is unusually large and suggests shipped product, and a B2B AI sales pitch that fits YC's agent theme. Down because AI outbound is one of the most crowded YC wedges, with Topo, Champ, Ciro, Persana and Bluebirds in earlier batches, and an all in one pitch is hard to tell from Apollo or Clay. Net about 3 percent; the company is already in the batch so this is for completeness.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most likely say that AI outbound and all in one GTM platforms are crowded, that Apollo and Clay already bundle data, enrichment and sequencing, and that the pitch does not name a customer segment that Fuse serves better. Past AI SDR companies from the same wedge produced small rounds or sold early, so partners would ask what is different.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show reply and booked meeting rates for real customers

1. Run pilots with five revenue teams
2. Track meetings booked per week

You know it worked when: Five paying teams with a documented meeting rate

### Reach Seed

Show retention and revenue that survive past three months

1. Pick one segment and price by outcome
2. Fix deliverability with domain warm up
3. Publish retention by cohort

You know it worked when: Monthly revenue above 50K with gross retention above 80 percent

### Reach Series A

Differentiate from Clay and Apollo with a data or signal edge

1. Build a proprietary signal source
2. Add enterprise integrations

You know it worked when: Annual recurring revenue near 2M and net revenue retention above 100 percent
