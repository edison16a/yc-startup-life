## General assessment

Gojiberry runs AI outbound for B2B teams and plans a wider GTM agent. The seed estimate starts at the 59 percent B2B anchor and is cut to 0.34 only because the 7 month window includes about half of eventual seeds; I do not lift for the live product or hot category. Analogs are mixed: champ and slik stalled, coffeeai shut down, bluebirds sold small, and artisan took about two years to its Series A. Series A and later within the window are near zero on timing. The odds do not rank this company against others.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 2.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting prior about 1 percent (0.6 to 2 percent, references/yc_acceptance.md). Up for a live product with stated results, a team of 7, an AI agent theme with real paid spend, and European founders with a clear wedge. Down for a very crowded AI SDR space and unproven retention. Net about 2 percent. Blind note: Spring 2026 batch, forward estimate only.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say that AI SDR is the most crowded category of 2024 and 2025, with funded leaders such as 11x and Artisan, that outbound results decay as inboxes and LinkedIn limit automation, and that a claim of 2 to 5 times more replies is self reported. Partners may want a wedge beyond outbound.
