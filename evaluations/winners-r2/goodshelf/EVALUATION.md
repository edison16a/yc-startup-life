## General assessment

Goodshelf wants to be the store AI shopping agents buy from, a new angle on a business where past YC curated retailers such as Adla, In Stock and PerShop shut down. The Consumer cohort gives 56 percent seed ever, and a 22 month window captures most of that, so the estimate is 40 percent with a discount for retail margins and an unknown team. A Series A is a small tail. The odds do not rank this company against others.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 1.5%

**How we got the number.** Starting prior is about 1 percent for a random applicant (0.6 to 2 percent, references/yc_acceptance.md). Down for a consumer retailer idea with no stated traction or technical wedge, since consumer and marketplace companies with no repeat usage are discounted in the factor table and defensibility is weak. Up slightly because agent facing commerce is a new angle and YC has funded e-commerce infrastructure. Net about 1.5 percent; the company is already in the batch so this is for completeness.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most likely say that online retail is a thin margin, inventory and logistics heavy business that Amazon and Walmart dominate, and that the claim that AI agents will buy through a new retailer is untested. Partners would ask why agents would prefer Goodshelf over Amazon, which already answers shopping questions with its own assistant, and where defensible advantage lies.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show real orders from AI assistants or search

1. Launch a catalog in one category
2. Measure orders from assistant referrals

You know it worked when: First 100 orders and a measured gross margin

### Reach Seed

Prove agent sourced demand and repeat purchase

1. Expose a structured feed to assistants
2. Track repeat rate and contribution margin per order

You know it worked when: Monthly sales above 100K with positive contribution margin

### Reach Series A

Show scalable margin and acquisition

1. Add exclusive suppliers
2. Cut paid acquisition share

You know it worked when: Gross margin above 30 percent and repeat orders above 30 percent
