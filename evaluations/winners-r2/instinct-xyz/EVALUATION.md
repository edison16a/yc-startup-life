## General assessment

Instinct builds AI agents that research markets and trade around the clock for individuals. The market for retail trading is large and agent use on prediction markets and onchain venues was rising in 2025, but the pitch is broad and no traction is stated. Retail trading products depend on market cycles and regulation, the nearest YC analogs raised small rounds or stayed at the YC check, and large brokerages can add agents. A seed above $1M inside the 10.4 month window is plausible near the Fintech cohort pace given a five person team. Later rounds depend on proof that the agents earn returns and that users stay. These probabilities do not rank Instinct against others.

A random applicant starts near 1 percent (yc_acceptance.md, 0.6 to 2 percent range). **Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because the pitch is a broad vision with no live users or returns, and trading agents for individuals involve regulation and trust questions. Reviewers could also see agent harnesses for trading as a thin layer on top of general model providers.

## Roadmap

### Reach Seed

Live users with real capital and verified performance.

1. Open a paid beta with 100 or more funded users.
2. Publish audited live trade logs for the agents.

You know it worked when: Assets under management or monthly fees that grow for three months in a row.

### Reach Series A

Durable revenue through a market downturn.

1. Add equities or prediction market venues to reduce crypto dependence.
2. Secure a licensed partner for custody.

You know it worked when: Recurring fee revenue that stays within 30 percent of peak in a down month.
