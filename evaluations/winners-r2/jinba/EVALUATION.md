## General assessment

Jinba is a chat based workflow builder for enterprises with controls aimed at financial institutions. The window is 10.4 months from 2026-01-05 to 2026-11-17, and the seed number is for a round above about 1M closing inside it. The B2B anchor is 59 percent ever and the forecast is 0.50 because only part of seeds close within 10 months and the category is crowded. Series A inside the window is very unlikely because the median is 28 months. The odds do not rank this company against others.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 4.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting prior is about 1 percent for a random applicant (0.6 to 2 percent range, references/yc_acceptance.md). Up for claimed real usage at large financial institutions (real revenue or growing usage factor) and an 11 person team that is large for the stage. Down because workflow automation and agent builders is a crowded topic. Net about 4 percent, and the batch is already given so the forecast is conditional on acceptance.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most likely say that chat based workflow building is one of the most crowded categories, with Zapier, n8n, Microsoft Copilot Studio, UiPath and many YC companies shipping it. The claimed 40,000 users may be seats at a few pilot accounts, and enterprise sales cycles in finance are long, so partners could doubt a startup can displace incumbents.
