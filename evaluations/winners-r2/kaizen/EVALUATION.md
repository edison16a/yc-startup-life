## General assessment

Kaizen sells an AI digital employee for healthcare referrals, benefits checks, prior authorizations and credentialing. The 19.3 month window is long enough for a typical seed, and the B2B and AI cohorts show about half of companies ever raising a seed. A six person team in a hot category does not justify a lift per the winners test, so the odds are near 38 percent and say little about this company versus others in the batch. Healthcare back office automation is a hot category, and the six person team has a concrete paid task, but the input shows no customers. The scored numbers are window specific and anchored on older cohorts, and the remaining risk is mostly execution and timing rather than the idea alone.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show paying provider groups for one workflow

1. Sign 3 to 5 practices on paid pilots
2. Measure hours saved and days to appointment

You know it worked when: Paid pilots with a measured cut in referral turnaround

### Reach Seed

Reach repeatable revenue with a single wedge

1. Choose referrals or prior auth as the lead product
2. Build integrations for the two largest EHRs in the segment

You know it worked when: ARR above $500K with low churn

### Reach Series A

Move to health systems and multi site groups

1. Hire a healthcare sales lead
2. Pass security and compliance audits

You know it worked when: ARR above $3M and net retention above 110 percent

### Reach Series B

Repeatable expansion with a second product or segment and efficient growth

1. Add a second wedge sold to the same buyer
2. Hire a sales leader and a customer success team
3. Show net revenue retention above 110 percent

You know it worked when: Revenue growth above 2x for a full year with stable gross margin

### Reach Series C and beyond

Category leadership with durable margins

1. Expand to adjacent markets or geographies
2. Build channel or platform partnerships
3. Show a path to profitability or a clear exit buyer

You know it worked when: Large enterprise customers and net retention above 120 percent
