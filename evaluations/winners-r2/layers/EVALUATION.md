## General assessment

Layers has a broad thesis about measuring and improving appearance with a two person team. Within the 13.4 month window the cohort anchor for consumer seed rounds is about 42 percent ever, so the window figure is near 25 percent. Consumer appearance is a crowded category with weak monetisation data, so there is no reason to lift above the anchor. These odds do not separate this company from others in the batch. Appearance improvement is a large consumer interest, but the input names no product, customer or revenue, so investors would be funding a thesis. The scored numbers are window specific and anchored on older cohorts, and the remaining risk is mostly execution and timing rather than the idea alone.

This company is already in the batch named in the input, so the number is for completeness only. Two person team, consumer health, no traction stated: slightly below the typical applicant on team depth, near it on idea novelty, so about 1.2 percent.

**Why it fails here.** The description is a thesis, not a product: it names no measurement method, no customer and no revenue. YC partners tend to pass on consumer appearance ideas where the repeat usage is unclear, and the looksmaxxing app category is crowded with free face scoring apps, so the most plausible no is weak differentiation and unproven retention.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Ship a working measurement and tracking product to real users

1. Launch to a niche community with a clear before and after
2. Track week 4 retention and paid conversion

You know it worked when: Week 4 retention above 25 percent and paid users in the hundreds

### Reach Seed

Show revenue growth and a repeatable acquisition channel

1. Run paid and creator channels with payback under 6 months
2. Add a product or service attach to lift revenue per user

You know it worked when: Monthly revenue above $50K growing 15 percent a month

### Reach Series A

Show subscriber scale and low churn

1. Build brand and community
2. Hire a head of growth and a clinical advisor

You know it worked when: Revenue above $2M a year with stable churn

### Reach Series B

Repeatable expansion with a second product or segment and efficient growth

1. Add a second wedge sold to the same buyer
2. Hire a sales leader and a customer success team
3. Show net revenue retention above 110 percent

You know it worked when: Revenue growth above 2x for a full year with stable gross margin

### Reach Series C and beyond

Category leadership with durable margins

1. Expand to adjacent markets or geographies
2. Build channel or platform partnerships
3. Show a path to profitability or a clear exit buyer

You know it worked when: Large enterprise customers and net retention above 120 percent
