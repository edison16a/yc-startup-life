## General assessment

Light Anchor runs consumer brands with AI agents and already operates seven. That is more concrete than most pitches, and AI native agencies have raised seeds in this period. Against that, investors will ask whether it is software or a brand holding company, brand failure is common, the window is only 7.4 months and the team is in Seoul. The seed figure sits slightly above the window adjusted cohort anchor, and the odds do not rank this company against others in the batch.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Start from the roughly 1 percent prior (0.6 to 2 percent band). Running 7 live brands is real operating traction and two technical looking founders add up; against that, a consumer ecommerce portfolio is a services style business and Seoul based teams are less common in the data. Net about 3 times the prior. The company is in the Spring 2026 batch, so the figure is for completeness.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because running 7 brands may look like an agency or a holding company, not software, and consumer brand economics depend on ad spend and inventory more than on the AI platform. Partners could ask whether agents are the cause of any brand success and whether the model scales beyond the founders own attention.

Show that agents drive profit across the brands

1. Publish brand level revenue and margin
2. Compare to human run controls

### Reach Seed

Convince a seed lead of a software like model

1. Separate platform costs from brand costs
2. Offer the platform to outside brands

2. Hire a head of growth
