## General assessment

Lucis sells blood testing and AI interpretation to European consumers. Consumers already buy subscription tests in Europe and the 17 person team can build quickly, but retention is the open question and cheaper panels exist. The seed estimate for the 19 month window sits just under the consumer and healthcare anchors after discounting for the window, and does not rank this company against others. The funded analog in the data, SiPhox Health, had a hardware edge, and the lean ones raised small rounds. A Series A inside the window is unlikely.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 50%

**How we got the number.** The company is already in the Spring 2025 batch, so this is a note only. A random applicant starts near 1 percent. A seventeen person team, an existing product category with a proven US model and a clear paying consumer would lift a cold application well above that, while a European consumer health service with regulated lab partners is a harder fit.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because a copy of a US company for another region is judged on execution and speed, and consumer health subscription retention is hard. Reviewers would also ask how a 17 person team funds lab costs and clinicians, and why blood tests are not a commodity that retailers already sell.

## Pros

- **Team of 17 at the start of the batch**: A larger team can ship lab operations, clinical review and the app in parallel.
- **A proven US model and an open European field**: Consumer longevity testing exists in the UK with subscription products and no clear pan European leader in the market search. (https://anivahealth.com/blog/longevity-blood-testing-europe)

## Roadmap

### Reach Seed

Proof of retained paying members.

1. Reach several thousand paying members with 12 month retention data.
2. Publish outcomes such as biomarker improvement or follow up testing rates.

You know it worked when: Monthly recurring revenue with churn under 3 percent a month.

### Reach Series A

A multi country model with margin.

1. Launch in two more countries with local lab partners.
2. Add a recurring care product that raises revenue per member.

You know it worked when: Positive contribution margin per member across countries.
