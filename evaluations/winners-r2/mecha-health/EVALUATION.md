## General assessment

Mecha Health builds small foundation models to draft x-ray reports, reports a benchmark win over big general models, and says it works with large radiology groups. Radiology has a clear need and pays per scan, but the work faces clinical validation, possible FDA clearance, slow healthcare sales and funded radiology AI companies. The healthcare anchor for a seed is 66 percent ever with a 8.75 month median and 27 month p75, so a 22 month window trims it to about 50 percent. The odds do not rank this company against others. Series A inside the window is about 10 percent. The main killers are clearance delays, pilots that do not convert, and platform vendors adding reports.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Start from the roughly 1 percent applicant prior (band 0.6 to 2 percent). Adjust up about 3x for a technical team with a benchmark result, named category partners and a per scan pricing model, and YC's interest in AI for healthcare. Adjust down for regulated healthcare slowness, FDA exposure and the funded radiology AI field. Net about 3 percent.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly worry that report generation needs regulatory clearance and radiologist trust, that the partnerships are unnamed pilots, and that a benchmark win over general models does not show real world accuracy. Radiology AI also has well funded incumbents, so partners may doubt a four person team can win in healthcare.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Turn the two named partnerships into a signed pilot with measured reads per hour.

1. Run the model on a partner's historical cases and publish an accuracy comparison.
2. Get a letter of intent with a per scan price.

You know it worked when: One signed paid pilot and a third party accuracy comparison.

### Reach Seed

Show paid volume and a regulatory plan.

1. Start FDA discussions with a regulatory advisor.
2. Reach 10,000 scans a month in paid use.

You know it worked when: $50K monthly recurring revenue and a pre submission with FDA.

### Reach Series A

Clearance and multi site deployment.

1. Obtain clearance for the first indication.
2. Add two more radiology groups.

You know it worked when: $2M annual recurring revenue and a cleared device.

### Reach Series B

Expand modalities and reimbursement.

1. Add CT and MRI.
2. Show cost savings to payers or groups.

You know it worked when: $12M annual recurring revenue.

### Reach Series C and beyond

National scale in radiology reporting.

1. Sign health system enterprise contracts.
2. Expand internationally.

You know it worked when: $40M or more annual recurring revenue.
