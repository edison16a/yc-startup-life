## General assessment

Memoir is an agent that joins video calls and does work when asked. The demo is clear, but meeting AI is crowded, the platforms bundle similar features, and notetaker analogs such as Speck, Sonnet and Hera ended small or shut down. The seed estimate is for only 7.4 months, about half the time most raisers need, and the founder is alone, so the number is well under the ever anchor and does not rank this company against others. A Series A inside the window is very unlikely. The best case is a pivot toward infrastructure, as Recall.ai did.

**How we got the number.** The company is already in the Spring 2026 batch, so this is a note only. A random applicant starts near 1 percent. A solo founder is slightly down in the factor table, the agents in meetings idea fits the AI agent theme, and the meeting notetaker space is very crowded, so the number is wide.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because meeting assistants are a crowded and commoditizing category, with Google, Microsoft and Zoom adding the same features. A solo founder would be asked who the paying customer is, why a call is the right place for an agent, and what the product does that a notetaker with a draft button does not.

## Pros

- **A sharper idea than a plain notetaker**: The live, on demand action model differs from passive notes and gives a clear demo.
- **Fast moving agent theme**: The description argues that agents already work in email, browsers and code, a thesis many AI investors accept.

## Roadmap

### Reach Seed

Paid usage from a narrow user group.

1. Get 30 technical founders to use it weekly on real sales calls.
2. Charge for completed work, not notes, and track retention.

You know it worked when: Weekly active teams that pay and keep using after 8 weeks.

### Reach Series A

A durable product beyond bundled notes.

1. Open the platform so other agents join calls through it.
2. Add a second customer group beyond technical founders.

You know it worked when: Revenue growth over 15 percent a month for six months with strong retention.
