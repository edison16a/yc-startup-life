## General assessment

Minerva is an early AI accounting company whose one liner promises a whole financial institution. In a 19 month window the base rate for a B2B YC company to raise an outside seed above $1M is about half, and a Series A in that window is rare. The input gives no traction, so the forecast sits near the cohort anchor and the odds do not rank this company against others. The company describes itself as an AI native financial institution that starts with accounting, a sequence that needs both product depth and regulatory partners. Funded rivals such as Quanta and Basis were already visible when the batch started. The cohort anchors do not separate winners from losers in the earlier winners test, so treat the number as a base rate.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Minerva is already in the Spring 2025 batch, so this is shown for completeness only. Starting rate is about 1 percent (YC states roughly 1 percent of 10,000 applicants per cycle, range 0.6 to 2). A five person team with a technical story is above average, but the one line pitch shows no traction, so the estimate is about 3 percent with a wide band.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly pass because accounting automation is crowded (Basis, Pilot, Digits, Quanta) and the one liner does not say what makes the team better placed than others; an 'AI native financial institution' sounds like a large ambition with no wedge or evidence of customers. Without a clear first customer, a measurable accuracy claim or a reason this team will win the category, the application reads like a thesis and not a company.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show a paying customer base in one segment with measured accuracy.

1. Sign 20 paying small businesses in one niche
2. Publish error rate against human bookkeeper review

You know it worked when: Monthly recurring revenue above $20K with logo retention above 90 percent.

### Reach Seed

Convince outside investors that margins are software margins.

1. Cut human review time per client by half
2. Raise a $3M to $6M round from fintech investors after showing growth

You know it worked when: Gross margin above 60 percent with over $50K MRR.
