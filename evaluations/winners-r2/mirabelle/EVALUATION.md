## General assessment

Mirabelle wants to put a robot chef in the home. People clearly pay for cooking help, and Posha showed a $1,500 countertop robot can sell, but the category is littered with companies that ran out of money before reaching consumer cost and reliability. In the 10 month window the odds of a $1M plus seed are about a third: the Consumer and Industrials cohorts reach seed at 43 to 48 percent ever, and only part of that happens in 10 months. A Series A in the window is very unlikely for a hardware company. Hardware, capital intensity and a two person team are the main limits.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 2.5%

**How we got the number.** The company is listed in the Winter 2026 batch, so acceptance is already known; this reports the prior. Starting from about 1 percent with a 0.6 to 2 percent band (yc_acceptance.md), raised to 2.5 percent because physical AI and robotics are priority areas and a consumer robot is memorable, held down because consumer hardware with a team of two and no demo is a high risk copyable concept.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly decline because home cooking robots have failed repeatedly on cost and reliability, the description gives no prototype, price or customer evidence, and a two person team in Paris has no stated robotics or food background. The idea is easy to say and extremely hard to build to a consumer price.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

A video of the robot cooking a full meal start to finish.

1. Build a prototype that cooks five dishes unattended.
2. Record cost of ingredients and machine bill of materials.

You know it worked when: A repeatable demo of 5 dishes with under 10 percent failures.

### Reach Seed

Show demand and a credible unit cost to a hardware seed investor.

1. Collect refundable deposits from a waitlist.
2. Sign a contract manufacturer quote for 500 units.

You know it worked when: 500 deposits and a quoted unit cost under half the retail price.

### Reach Series A

Ship units and show repeat use.

1. Deliver 200 units to paying households.
2. Track meals cooked per week per unit.

You know it worked when: Over 3 meals per week per unit after 8 weeks.
