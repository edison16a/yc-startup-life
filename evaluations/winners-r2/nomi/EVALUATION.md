## General assessment

Nomi sells a real time sales copilot, a wedge with funded rivals such as Spiky and Second Nature and incumbents like Gong. The YC peers show a mixed record: wingman was acquired after a seed, silkchart reached seed, demodesk reached an A over years, and echo shut down. In a 19 month window the seed is about 42 percent and most likely $3M to $6M. A Series A is unlikely at 8 percent given a seed, and the realistic exit is a small acquisition.
