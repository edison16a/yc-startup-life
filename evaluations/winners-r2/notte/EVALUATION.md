## General assessment

Notte sells cloud browsers to agent builders in a wedge with funded leaders such as Browserbase and several YC peers. Developer Tools companies from 2022 to 2025 reached seed 55 percent of the time and A 21 percent, and none of the near YC browser infra analogs show a big round after the batch. Over a 16 month window, the seed is about 42 percent and most likely $3M to $6M if reached. An A is unlikely. Winning would require owning authentication and payments for agents rather than selling browser hours.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 97%

**How we got the number.** The input file places Notte in the Summer 2025 batch with the batch start as the as_of date, so acceptance is treated as already happened. The prior for a random applicant is about 1 percent (references/yc_acceptance.md), but conditional on being listed in the batch the residual doubt is only data error or a deferral. Seed odds below are conditional on being in YC.

**Why it fails here.** The only way the YC step fails here is a clerical error in the batch listing or a deferral to a later batch. Notte was reported in the batch directory as of the as_of date, so the reason to say no has already been resolved in its favor.

## Pros

- **Hot category with proven paid demand**: Leaders bill usage and ship concurrency plans, so developers do pay for cloud browsers. (https://browserbase.com/pricing)
- **Broad single API: sessions, auth, proxies, payments**: The product combines parts that agent builders otherwise stitch together, a plausible wedge if identity and payments are strong.

Show paid usage growth.

Show repeatable revenue and a moat beyond raw browsers.

1. Hire a sales engineer
2. Add SOC 2 and enterprise single sign on

### Reach Series B

Become a default layer for agent identity and payments.

1. Launch payments and identity rails
2. Add regional proxy coverage

You know it worked when: $10M in annualized revenue growing 3x

### Reach Series C and beyond

Platform scale.

1. Sign cloud marketplace deals
2. Expand into agent identity for enterprises

You know it worked when: $40M annualized revenue
