## General assessment

OneStop is an early invite-only consumer social app for hyperlocal posts in New York. The forecast window is 16.3 months from 2025-07-07 to 2026-11-17. We estimate 0.30 for an outside round above about $1M closing inside that window, below the consumer cohort ever-seed anchor of 0.72 because consumer seeds are slow and the target excludes the YC deal. These odds do not rank this company against others. Reaching a round years later is common for companies with paying customers, and a pivot then raise path is a real upside.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 1.2%

**How we got the number.** Prior of about 1 percent for a random applicant (yc_acceptance.md, range 0.6 to 2 percent). Down for consumer social with no stated repeat usage evidence (consumer 35 percent inactive in our sample) and a two person team with no revenue; slightly up for a clear geographic wedge and an invite-only launch that gives a testable density claim. Net about 1.2 percent. The company is already in the Summer 2025 batch, so this is shown for completeness.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly pass because local social is a graveyard category where Nextdoor, Facebook Groups and Instagram already own the habit, and a two person team had not yet shown that people in one neighborhood return weekly. Partners also ask what grows without paid acquisition, and a blocks-wide network needs density that an invite list in one New York area cannot prove early.

## Pros

- **Clear geographic wedge with a density claim that can be tested quickly**: Invite-only launch in a single New York area lets the team measure retention in a bounded zone.
- **Low build cost**: A two person team can ship iOS and Android, keeping burn low for the YC deal runway.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show repeat weekly use in Hell's Kitchen.

1. Reach several hundred weekly active invited users in a few blocks
2. Track week 4 retention and posts per active user

You know it worked when: Week 4 retention above 30 percent of invited users

### Reach Seed

Prove a second neighborhood and a revenue test.

1. Replicate density in two more New York areas
2. Test local venue promotion with paid pilots

You know it worked when: Organic growth in new areas and first paying venues
