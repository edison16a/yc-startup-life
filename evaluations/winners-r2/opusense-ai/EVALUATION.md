## General assessment

Opusense AI turns inspectors voice notes and photos into finished reports. The pain is real and quantified, and the founders know the buyer. The forecast window is 19.3 months from 2025-04-07 to 2026-11-17 and the seed number is for a round above about 1M closing inside it. The cohort anchor is about 59 percent ever, and the forecast sits slightly below it because the field is crowded with funded and incumbent products and the team is two people. Series A inside the window is unlikely because it takes a median 28 months. The odds here do not rank this company against others.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting prior is about 1 percent for a random applicant (0.6 to 2 percent third party range, references/yc_acceptance.md). Up for domain experience of 15 combined years and a PhD founder with a technical co founder (technical founder factor), and a clear daily pain with a measurable time saving. Neutral to down for a two person team with no stated revenue and a niche vertical. Net about 3 percent, and the forecast is conditional on acceptance as the batch is already given.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most likely say that field report writing for inspectors is a thin wedge with little evidence of paying customers, that several funded rivals already do voice and photo to report, and that Procore, Autodesk and Sitemate can add the same feature. With no stated revenue or pilots, partners may see a feature rather than a company.

## Pros

- **Founders with domain depth**: Combined over 15 years in construction and a civil engineering PhD plus a startup engineer, per the application. (https://ycombinator.com/companies/opusense-ai)
- **Measurable pain**: Report writing at about 20 percent of the week is a quantified time cost the founders claim to cut 5x. (https://apps.apple.com/app/inspectmind-ai/id6475725008)

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show real use by inspectors.

1. Run paid pilots with three inspection firms
2. Record time saved per report

You know it worked when: Three firms using it weekly and a measured saving.

### Reach Seed

Prove paid repeat usage in one inspection type.

1. Price per report or per seat and convert pilots
2. Integrate with common report formats and project tools

You know it worked when: At least 10 paying firms and recurring revenue growing monthly.
