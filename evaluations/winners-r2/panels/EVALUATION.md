## General assessment

Panels sells audio data to AI labs and the input says little beyond that. Lab demand for audio is real, but David AI already leads, the founder is solo and synthetic data can substitute at the low end. The window is 16.3 months, enough for a seed if early contracts land, while a Series A inside it is unlikely. The odds do not rank this company against others because the anchor is the cohort rate with a modest cut for solo and crowding. The key risks are a funded leader, supply building and synthetic substitution.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 1.5%

**How we got the number.** Starting rate about 1 percent for a random applicant (0.6 to 2 percent range, yc_acceptance.md). Adjusted up slightly for a timely buyer (AI labs spend on data) but down for a solo founder (slightly down in the factor table), an empty description, and a crowded data vendor category with a funded YC leader.

**Why it fails here.** YC partners may ask why a solo founder with no stated edge will beat David AI and other funded audio data vendors, and whether labs will buy from a one person company. Data vendors need supply, quality control and customer trust, none of which the input shows, and a data supplier can be copied by synthetic data.

## Pros

- **Buyers have money and need data**: AI labs spend on training data and conversational audio is scarce. (https://sacra.com/research/david-ai)
- **Fast to start**: A data business can start with a small team and a pilot dataset. (https://pangeanic.com/datasets-for-ai/speech)
