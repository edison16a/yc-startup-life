## General assessment

Panta is a three person commercial insurance brokerage that uses AI workflows and licensed brokers. The market is large and customers already pay commissions, and investors were funding AI native brokers in late 2025. The weakness is that brokerage revenue is a percent of premium and scales with producers, so it is a services business, and several funded rivals, including earlier YC brokerages, chase the same clients. Within the 10.4 month window from the batch start, a seed above $1M is plausible but not likely, near the cohort pace for Fintech, and a Series A is rare because median time to A is over two years. This probability does not rank Panta against other companies, as the 2026 winners test showed no skill in seed odds. Bands put most weight on $3M to $6M.

## Pros

- **Insurance is a huge paid market with an inefficient process**: Brokers earn commissions on commercial premium and the process is manual, which is where AI saves time. (https://neuronfeed.com/startups/1fort)
- **Revenue can start early**: A brokerage with licensed brokers earns commissions from the first placed policy, so it does not depend on software sales cycles.
- **Investors were funding the category**: AI native brokerage seeds of several million dollars were announced in late 2025. (https://neuronfeed.com/startups/1fort)

## Roadmap

### Reach Seed

Show premium placed and commission growth that look like a software ramp.

1. Land 20 or more paying business clients in one niche with a measured time to quote.
2. Obtain carrier appointments and publish gross margin per client.

You know it worked when: Commission revenue growing 20 percent or more month over month for three months.

### Reach Series A

Repeatable acquisition and retention.

1. Hire a senior producer and a sales lead for the chosen niche.
2. Show renewal rates above 90 percent.

You know it worked when: Annualized commission revenue of $2M or more with retention above 90 percent.
