## General assessment

Paper Instruments post trains open models for knowledge work. Investor appetite for open model labs and post training is high and a three person technical team can often raise a seed on that alone, so a round above $1M in 19 months is plausible, near the cohort anchor. But the idea needs compute and talent, general models keep improving, and fine tuning peers such as openpipe and airtrain ai were acquired or went inactive. The odds do not rank this company against others. Later rounds depend on a released model that wins a benchmark and a way to charge for open weights.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 1.5%

**How we got the number.** Start from the third party prior of about 1 percent (references/yc_acceptance.md). A three person technical team in post training matches a current YC priority in open models, but no model, benchmark or traction is stated and the space is crowded with funded labs, so about 1.5 percent.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because training frontier models for knowledge work needs large compute and data, and a three person team without a released model or customers is hard to separate from many fine tuning startups. Reviewers also ask who pays for an open model and how it beats closed labs.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

A released model or benchmark that beats open baselines on a knowledge work task

1. Release a post trained model with an open evaluation
2. Publish weights and a technical report

You know it worked when: Top of a public benchmark in one task

### Reach Seed

Paying users and a funding story for compute

1. Offer hosted inference
2. Win design partners who need private models

You know it worked when: $10,000 monthly revenue and 3 design partners

### Reach Series A

Revenue growth beyond early adopters

1. Add enterprise customization
2. Ship evaluation and tooling

You know it worked when: $1M annual revenue

### Reach Series B

A durable model franchise

1. Train a second generation model
2. Build distribution through partners

You know it worked when: $10M annual revenue

### Reach Series C and beyond

Frontier scale lab or large platform

1. Raise for compute
2. Expand model family

You know it worked when: $50M annual revenue
