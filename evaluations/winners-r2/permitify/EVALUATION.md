## General assessment

Permitify builds AI plan review for city building departments with a two person team in Salt Lake City. Cities clearly want faster permits and several piloted AI review in 2024, but rivals such as Archistar and Clariti already hold references and incumbents such as Accela own the system of record. In a 22 month window the B2B anchor puts a seed above $1M near 42 percent after discounting for slow public sales and a Series A inside the window near 8 percent. Odds here do not rank this company against others.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because selling to city governments is slow and the AI permit review space already has Archistar, CivCheck and PermitFlow style entrants. ## Pros

- **Real labor shortage and public budget pressure**: Building departments are understaffed and pilots in Austin and Harris County in 2024 show willingness to try AI review. (https://www.constructionowners.com/news/cities-turn-to-ai-to-speed-up-permit-reviews)
- **Adjacent success in YC**: PermitFlow (W22) raised a $5.5M seed and a roughly $31M Series A, showing investors fund AI for permits. (https://propmodo.com/?p=179490)

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Prove a reviewer saves real time on real plans

1. Run a free pilot with 2 building departments and time reviews with and without the tool
2. Document the code checks that catch real errors with cited sections

You know it worked when: Measured review time reduction of 30 percent or more in 2 departments

### Reach Seed

Convert pilots into paid annual contracts

1. Sign 3 to 5 cities at $20K to $60K a year under procurement thresholds
2. Build integrations to Accela or Tyler systems

You know it worked when: 3 paid city contracts and at least $100K annual recurring revenue

### Reach Series A

Repeatable municipal sales across states

1. Hire a government sales lead and channel partners
2. Expand to inspections and permit intake

You know it worked when: 15 or more cities and $1M ARR with renewal above 90 percent
