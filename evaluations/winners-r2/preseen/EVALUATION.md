## General assessment

Preseen builds forecasting agents for global macro events. The seed estimate starts at the 59 percent B2B cohort anchor and is cut to 0.45 for a solo founder, no named buyer and a 16 month window. Analogs in forecasting for finance, pit-ai, intelligent and payflow-2, shut down without a post YC round, which supports a lower Series A number. Public prediction markets and benchmarks mean buyers can get probabilities free, so willingness to pay is the central question. The odds do not rank this company against others, and the data behind this niche is thin.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 1.5%

**How we got the number.** Starting prior about 1 percent (0.6 to 2 percent, references/yc_acceptance.md). Up for a timely AI agents theme and a testable claim (forecast accuracy). Down for a solo founder, no traction, a one sentence description with no named buyer, and analogs in finance forecasting that failed. Net about 1.5 percent. Blind note: Summer 2025 batch, forward estimate only.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say that the founder has not named who pays for macro forecasts, that prediction markets and superforecasters already provide public forecasts, and that accuracy of an agent is hard to prove before launch. A one line description and a solo founder give partners little to hold onto.

## Pros

- **Timely AI research direction**: Studies show LLM forecasters improving quickly toward superforecaster accuracy. (https://forecastingresearch.substack.com/p/ai-llm-forecasting-model-forecastbench-benchmark)
- **Testable claim**: Forecasts can be scored publicly, so the company can build a verifiable track record cheaply. (https://forecastingresearch.substack.com/p/ai-llm-forecasting-model-forecastbench-benchmark)

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show a track record and one paying buyer.

1. Publish live forecasts with scored accuracy
2. Sign one fund or corporate on a paid pilot

You know it worked when: A scored public track record and a first invoice

### Reach Seed

Show repeatable revenue from a defined buyer.

1. Package a subscription for macro funds
2. Add a cofounder for sales

You know it worked when: Recurring revenue above $300K annualized from 5 or more clients

### Reach Series A

Prove an edge versus markets that clients pay for.

1. Show forecast value in client decisions
2. Expand to corporate risk teams

You know it worked when: Retention above 90 percent and net revenue retention above 110 percent
