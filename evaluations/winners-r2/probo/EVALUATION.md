## General assessment

Probo is a managed compliance service for startups, with a team of six in San Francisco. The compliance tag cohort gives 0.71 for ever reaching seed and the broader B2B cohort 0.61. Over the 19.3 month window I give 0.50 for a round above $1M, and 0.10 for Series A given seed. Vanta and Drata dominate and many YC entrants chase the same buyer, though a managed service wedge differs from pure software. The odds do not rank this company against others.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 99%

**How we got the number.** The company is already in the batch that starts at the as_of date, so the acceptance step is reported only for completeness: the prior for a random applicant is about 1 percent (0.6 to 2 percent), and conditional on being accepted the forecast below is what matters. Team of 6 is larger than typical; the product is a clear painful problem for founders but the space is crowded.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because compliance automation is crowded: Vanta, Drata and Secureframe have raised large rounds, several YC companies already sell similar AI compliance agents, and differentiation between them is thin. The managed service pitch of acting as the compliance team raises margin questions, though a team of six and a clearly painful problem for founders would help in the interview.

## Assumptions and limits

- Blind forecast made as of 2025-04-07 using only the YC batch description in the input file, with market-only web searches.
- Forecast window is 19.3 months, from 2025-04-07 to 2026-11-17.
- Seed means an outside financing round above about $1M that closes inside the window, not the standard YC deal of about $500K; the seed and Series A probabilities are window probabilities, not ever probabilities.
- The odds do not rank this company against other companies in the batch: in the 2026 winners test, team size, claimed traction and a hot sector did not separate raisers from non raisers, so the seed number stays near the cohort anchor.
- Cohort anchors come from older and 2022 to 2024 batches and are partly right censored; funding coverage in the data is incomplete (lower, known and upper ranges quoted).
