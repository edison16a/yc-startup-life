## General assessment

Q2Q is an AI outbound engine that books first calls between private equity buyers and business owners. PE firms are shifting to proprietary sourcing and already pay agencies and data vendors, so there is spend. The company is two people, the buyer pool is small, outbound email is saturated and data vendors can bundle outreach. A seed above $1M within the 10.4 month window is less likely than the B2B cohort average, given the services like economics and small team, and a Series A inside the window is rare. These odds do not rank Q2Q against others. The likely good outcome is steady revenue and a seed around $3M to $6M, or sale to a data vendor.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 45%

**How we got the number.** The company is already in the batch that starts on the as of date, so this is a note only. A random applicant starts near 1 percent (yc_acceptance.md, 0.6 to 2 percent range). Two founders with a clear customer and a measurable output (booked meetings) fit YC, but the application states no traction and cold email tools were abundant.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because AI outbound is a crowded category of agency like tools, the buyer pool of PE firms is small, and two founders without a stated PE background must explain why PE firms would trust them with outreach to business owners.

## Roadmap

### Reach Seed

Show booked meetings at a clear cost.

1. Run paid pilots with 5 or more PE firms and track cost per booked meeting.
2. Show a closed deal that began from a Q2Q meeting.

### Reach Series A

Expand beyond sourcing.

1. Add deal tracking and owner follow up.
