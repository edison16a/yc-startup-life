## General assessment

Relaw offers an AI legal OS to AI native law firms. Legal AI funding is hot, which helps, and the company is early with two people. The 13.4 month window is shorter than the median time to a Series A and close to the time to a seed. The odds do not rank this company against peers because the anchor is the cohort rate and the lifts are small. Risks are a small first customer base, funded horizontal rivals and ownership rules.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 2.5%

**How we got the number.** Starting rate about 1 percent for a random applicant (0.6 to 2 percent range, yc_acceptance.md). Adjusted up about 2.5 times for a timely wedge and a vertical with many funded AI legal companies. Held back because the description is a single sentence with no customers named, and the AI legal space is crowded.

**Why it fails here.** YC partners will see many AI legal applications and a customer set of AI native law firms is tiny today. The one line description gives no traction, no named firms and no distinct technical edge versus Harvey, Legora or the firms building their own tools.
