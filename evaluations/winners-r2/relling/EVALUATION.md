## General assessment

Relling states the aim of deploying the first billion robots in manufacturing, and the input offers no product, team or traction. The window is 16.3 months from 2025-07-07 to 2026-11-17, long enough for most seeds, so seed sits at 0.50 against anchors of 0.57 to 0.59. Series A given seed is 0.06 in the window. The odds do not rank this company against others, and the robotics boom is not credited beyond the anchor.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting prior is about 1 percent for a random applicant (0.6 to 2 percent range, references/yc_acceptance.md). Up for the hot robotics theme and a San Francisco hardware ambition that fits YC priorities. Neutral on team, since none is stated. Down because a one line pitch of a billion robots has no stated customer or product. Net about 3 percent, conditional on the batch already given.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say that deploying the first billion robots is a vision and not a wedge, and that hardware manufacturing automation needs capital and long sales cycles that do not fit a three month demo. Without a named customer or a working robot, partners may prefer a founder with prior factory deployments.

## Pros

- **Hot sector with returning capital**: Industrial robotics funding rebounded after 2025 per public trackers. (https://robotsandstartups.substack.com/p/insights-into-2025-robotics-funding)
- **Large, paying buyer base**: Manufacturers already spend on automation and labour shortages push demand for robots. (https://www.therobotreport.com/robotics-investments-top-43b-in-july-2025/)

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show one real factory task automated.

1. Pick a single task such as machine loading
2. Run a paid pilot at one plant

You know it worked when: One customer paying monthly for a running robot.

### Reach Seed

Repeat the pilot at three or more sites.

1. Standardize the cell and installation
2. Price as robots as a service

You know it worked when: Three paying sites with robots running above 90 percent uptime.
