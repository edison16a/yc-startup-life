## General assessment

Revion automates technician paperwork for dealership service departments. The need is measurable and Toma shows dealership AI can raise a Series A, but the space was already crowded in January 2026 and DMS vendors ship similar assistants. The window is only 10 months, so the probability uses the B2B timing curve, not a lift for team size. A Series A inside the window is very unlikely. The odds do not rank Revion against other companies. The forecast window runs from the as of date to 2026-11-17, and later rounds are only counted if they close inside it.
