## General assessment

Riverbank sells infrastructure maps across heterogeneous environments. The need is proven by Armis and Axonius, and investors have funded this category heavily, but those companies have large teams, strong distribution and platforms behind them. A solo founder with an abstract description has little to separate from them yet. The B2B 2022 and later cohort reaches seed at 52 percent known only, and the estimate for a 16 month window is 38 percent. Series A in the window is unlikely, and outcomes resemble Appcanary and EdgeBit: small exits by platform owners.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 1.2%

**How we got the number.** The company is listed in the Summer 2025 batch, so acceptance is already known; this reports the prior. Starting from about 1 percent with a 0.6 to 2 percent band (yc_acceptance.md); kept near that because a solo founder is slightly negative, the description is abstract with no customer, and the security infrastructure space is crowded with funded firms such as Armis and Axonius, offset by YC interest in AI native infrastructure.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly decline because a solo founder with an abstract mapping pitch gives no evidence of a customer pain, a design partner or a wedge against Armis, Axonius and cloud vendors. YC prefers a sharply stated first user and a founder with proof of building and selling to security teams, and the description supplies neither.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

One design partner with a named mapping problem.

1. Interview 20 security or OT teams about unknown assets.
2. Ship a mapper for one environment.

You know it worked when: A signed design partner letter.

### Reach Seed

Paying pilots and a second team member.

1. Convert pilots to paid annual contracts.
2. Hire a go to market co-founder.

You know it worked when: Three paying customers above $30K annual.

### Reach Series A

A repeatable sales motion in one segment.

1. Build integrations with major security platforms.
2. Show net revenue retention above 110 percent.

You know it worked when: Annual recurring revenue above $1M.
