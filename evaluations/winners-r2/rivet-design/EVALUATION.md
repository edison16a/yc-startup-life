## General assessment

Rivet pitches design exploration through coding agents. The market has active funded players and investor interest in AI design, but the description is one sentence, team size is unknown and the product depends on agents that platforms can absorb in months. A seed above $1M inside 13 months is plausible given AI heat, yet below the cohort anchor because of copy risk and no stated traction. The odds do not rank Rivet against other companies. Later rounds need a paying design team base, which has not been shown.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because the pitch is thin and sits in a crowded set of AI design tools, from prototyping products to canvas tools for agents. Without an obvious paying buyer or traction, reviewers see a feature that Figma or the coding agent vendors can ship.
