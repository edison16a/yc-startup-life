## General assessment

Runtime sells an agent harness to fintech payment and risk teams. The forecast window is 7.4 months from 2026-04-06 to 2026-11-17. We give 0.34 for an outside round above about $1M closing in that window, derived from the 2022 to 2025 B2B anchor of 0.59 scaled by timing, with no lift for sector hype. Analogs show both fast seeds (Sphinx $7.1M, Coris $3.7M) and slow ones (Diligent after 2.5 years). These odds do not rank this company against others. Reaching a round later is common for companies with paying customers.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 2.0%

**How we got the number.** Prior of about 1 percent (yc_acceptance.md, range 0.6 to 2 percent). Up for a team of 7, a precise buyer (fintech payment and risk ops) and strong fit with YC's AI agent interest; technical founders are in 74 percent of accepted companies. Down because agent harness and workflow tools are crowded and the description gives no traction. Net about 2 percent. The company is already in the Spring 2026 batch, shown for completeness.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because horizontal agent platforms are a crowded category and this pitch reads as tooling that every AI lab and workflow vendor is also building. Partners would want evidence that named fintechs run agents in production on payment operations, and without design partners or revenue the wedge into regulated money workflows looks unproven.
