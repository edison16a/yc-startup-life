## General assessment

Ruvo is a dollar account for Brazilians that connects Pix, stablecoins and Visa. Demand is clear: Brazil sees very large stablecoin flows and dollar earners pay fees and taxes. Competition is heavy, from Wise and Nubank to exchanges such as OKX that offered Pix linked cards. Comparable YC companies split: Aspora grew to Series B, Ping raised a large seed and went quiet, and Broad shut down. The window is 16.3 months so seed is the realistic outcome and later rounds are unlikely. The odds do not rank this company.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting rate of about 1 percent from references/yc_acceptance.md. Up for a team of 11 and a large local market with a clear cross border pain (dollar income, IOF tax, slow banks); down for a crowded neobank field and regulatory risk with no stated traction. Already listed in Summer 2025, so the ladder is the "if accepted" view.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say that dollar accounts for Latin America are a well worn idea with many funded rivals, and that the founders have not shown why they win against Wise, crypto exchanges and Nubank, nor how they handle Brazil banking rules around crypto.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Prove users keep dollars in the account.

1. Reach thousands of active freelancer accounts
2. Track average USD balances and repeat conversions

You know it worked when: Monthly active accounts and balance per user growing

### Reach Seed

Raise a round above $1M within 16 months.

1. Show monthly volume growth of 20 percent or more
2. Pitch LatAm fintech and crypto funds using volume and revenue per user

You know it worked when: $5M or more monthly volume with positive take rate

### Reach Series A

Show a durable regulatory and unit economics position.

1. Secure licenses or tier one partner banks in Brazil
2. Expand business (CNPJ) accounts for higher revenue per customer

You know it worked when: $1M or more annual revenue at positive contribution margin
