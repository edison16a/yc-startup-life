## General assessment

SalesPatriot sells AI that automates quote to cash for industrial distributors and procurement teams. With a 22 month window the seed estimate sits at the cohort anchor of about 0.6, since team size and sector do not separate winners in the test. The risks are services heavy deployments and funded rivals in order entry, shown by the slow paths of jiga and recurrency. The odds do not rank this company against others. Series A inside the window is cut to 0.16 for timing, and later rounds are near zero for the same reason, not because of a view on quality. Revenue proof from named industrial customers is the evidence that would move these numbers most.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 2.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting prior about 1 percent (0.6 to 2 percent, references/yc_acceptance.md). Up for a timely AI plus supply chain and defense industrial theme, a real operating team of 30, and a concrete workflow (quote to cash). Down for a crowded order entry space and an abstract superintelligence pitch. Net about 2 percent. Blind note: batch was Winter 2025, so this is a forward estimate only.

**Why it fails here.** YC partners would most plausibly say that quote automation for distributors already has funded players, that integrating with every ERP is a services heavy slog, and that a pitch of superintelligence is broader than any evidence of customers paying. A team of 30 also raises the question of who pays them and how.

## Pros

- **Large team and concrete workflow**: Thirty people listed and a defined quote to cash use case, unusual for a batch company. (https://www.mdm.com/news/breaking-news-in-wholesale-distribution/proton-ai-launches-automated-order-quote-entry-system/)
- **Clear paying buyer in a measurable process**: Distributors measure quote turnaround and order errors, so value is easy to test. (https://distributionstrategy.com/2023/08/transform-distributor-order-entry-with-ai/)

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show paying distributor or manufacturer customers.

1. Land 3 named industrial customers on paid pilots
2. Measure hours saved per quote

You know it worked when: Pilot revenue and a measured quote time reduction

### Reach Seed

Convert pilots to repeatable software contracts.

1. Standardize the integration layer for two or three ERPs
2. Move to annual contracts priced per transaction

You know it worked when: Annual recurring revenue above $1M with under 12 month payback

### Reach Series A

Show growth without forward deployed headcount growing at the same pace.

1. Reach 15 or more customers
2. Show gross margin above 65 percent

You know it worked when: Net revenue retention above 110 percent
