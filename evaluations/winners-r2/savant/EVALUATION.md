## General assessment

Savant sells an enterprise knowledge layer for people and agents, a category that attracted many seed rounds in early 2026 and also many YC peers and platform rivals. The cohort anchors say about 59 percent of 2022 and later B2B YC companies reach a seed ever, but only about half of those close by month 8, so a 7.4 month window gives about 35 percent. Later rounds are nearly impossible inside the window. Analogs such as Slite, Station and Opzi show the ceiling and the failure modes of knowledge tools. The odds do not rank this company against others.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting prior is about 1 percent for a random applicant (0.6 to 2 percent, references/yc_acceptance.md). Up for a company brain pitch that matches the 2026 AI agent theme and a team of seven, which implies technical founders and some build capacity. Down because the company brain space is crowded with funded rivals and nothing in the input shows customers or revenue. Net about 3 percent, and the company is already in the batch so this is for completeness.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most likely say that company brain and enterprise memory products are a crowded category where Glean, Notion and the model labs already ship connectors and agent memory, and that a seven person team has not shown a customer who pays. Partners may also doubt that a horizontal knowledge layer can win a first wedge.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show one enterprise using the product with real agents

1. Install at three design partners
2. Measure agent accuracy with and without Savant

You know it worked when: One paying or committed pilot with a measured accuracy gain

### Reach Seed

Convert pilots to paid contracts before the seed pitch

1. Price per seat or per agent
2. Publish a case study with numbers
3. Pick one function (support or sales ops) as the wedge

You know it worked when: Two to three paid customers and monthly recurring revenue above 20K

### Reach Series A

Prove repeatable enterprise sales and retention

1. Hire a first enterprise seller
2. Add security certifications
3. Track net revenue retention

You know it worked when: Annual recurring revenue near 1M to 2M with growing seats
