## General assessment

Scheduling Wizard schedules physicians for hospital departments with its own scheduling language and AI workflows, and states 20 departments at 16 hospitals as clients. The window is 10.4 months from 2026-01-05 to 2026-11-17, short for healthcare where the median seed comes near 9 months and the p75 near 27, so seed is 0.30 against a Healthcare anchor of 0.50 for ever. Series A given seed is 0.04 in the window. The odds do not rank this company against others.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 4.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting prior about 1 percent (0.6 to 2 percent range). Up for real paying clients at 16 hospitals including well known academic centers, which is traction. Down for a three person service heavy team in a slow procurement field and healthcare IT competitors. Net about 4 percent, conditional on the batch already given.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say that physician scheduling outsourcing looks like a service business with limited scale, and that incumbents QGenda and Amion own the category. Partners may also doubt that a three person team can scale to triage and provider management together, and may ask why hospitals would not simply buy software.
