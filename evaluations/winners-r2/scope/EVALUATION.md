## General assessment

Scope measures and improves how coding agents find and use developer products. The problem is topical, and YC has funded neighbors such as AthenaHQ and The Prompting Company, whose seeds show money exists for the lane. A 2 person team with no stated revenue has about 7.4 months, against a cohort median of about 6 months to seed among those that raise at all. I use 0.38 against an ever anchor of 0.44, and the odds do not rank the company against others. The biggest risks are a tiny early buyer set and agent vendors absorbing the function.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 2.0%

**How we got the number.** Start from about 1 percent (0.6 to 2 percent published). Adjust up for a technical founder with interpretability research and GEO work, and a topical problem that fits YC's interest in agent-era infrastructure; adjust down for a 2 person team, no stated revenue, and a market where the customer base (dev-tool companies) is small and the GEO lane already has several YC companies (AthenaHQ, The Prompting Company). Net about 2 percent; the company is already in the Spring 2026 batch.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say the buyer set is thin and the product is a measurement dashboard that may become a feature of existing GEO and observability tools. A two person team with no named paying customers, in a lane where AthenaHQ and The Prompting Company already sell agent and AI-search visibility, gives interviewers little reason to believe in a large market.
