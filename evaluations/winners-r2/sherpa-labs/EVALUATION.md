## General assessment

Sherpa Labs appears to sell frontier training data to AI labs. The forecast window is 22.4 months from 2025-01-06 to 2026-11-17, long enough for most seed timing. We give 0.52 for an outside round above about $1M closing in the window, from the AI cohort anchor of 0.64 with no hype lift. Analogs split between Centaur and Sieve, which raised, and Sepal AI, which stayed at the YC deal. These odds do not rank this company against others. Reaching a round years later is common for companies with paying customers.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 1.5%

**How we got the number.** Prior of about 1 percent (yc_acceptance.md, range 0.6 to 2 percent). Up for an AI data niche fitting YC's AI focus and a five person team; down because the pitch is a single phrase and data labeling has a dominant incumbent in Scale AI. Net about 1.5 percent. The company is already in the Winter 2025 batch, shown for completeness.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because 'Frontier Data' does not say what is sold, to whom or why a lab would buy from a five person startup instead of Scale AI or Surge, and customer concentration among a few labs is a known risk.
