## General assessment

Shofo says it has indexed billions of videos and uses agents to deliver custom labeled datasets to AI labs. Demand for video data is real and prices are visible in press, and the sector drew heavy investor interest, but a four person team must prove paid deliveries and solve the legal rights problem. In a 10 month window only about 60 percent of eventual seed raisers have raised by then, so the seed probability is 0.35 and a Series A inside the window is very unlikely at 3 percent. Odds here do not rank this company against others.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 2.0%

**How we got the number.** The company is in the Winter 2026 batch, so this is shown for completeness. Start from the roughly 1 percent prior for a random applicant (0.6 to 2 percent range). Adjust up for AI data demand and a technical four person team that fits YC's AI infrastructure interest, and hold near 2 percent because data rights and customer proof are unstated.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because the pitch rests on a huge video index whose rights are unclear: scraped video raises copyright and platform terms of service questions that labs and YC partners both know. Data labeling is also crowded with Scale, Encord and many small vendors, and no named lab customer or revenue is stated.

## Pros

- **Strong and growing demand for video data**: Labs pay per minute or per hour for licensed video and the AI training data market is forecast to quadruple by 2030. (https://the-decoder.com/tech-giants-are-on-a-billion-dollar-shopping-spree-for-ai-training-data/)
- **On demand custom delivery is a differentiator**: Delivering a labeled dataset in days instead of months is a measurable improvement over manual procurement. (https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/ai-giants-pay-thousands-for-creators-unused-footage-to-train-models/)

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show a lab will pay for a custom video dataset

1. Deliver a paid pilot dataset to one lab with provenance documents
2. Document the cost per labeled hour versus competitors

You know it worked when: One paid dataset delivery above $25K

### Reach Seed

Show repeat revenue from several labs with defensible rights

1. Sign 3 labs to recurring dataset orders
2. Switch the index to licensed or contractually sourced video

You know it worked when: $50K to $100K monthly revenue and a legal opinion on data rights

### Reach Series A

Become a standing supplier to frontier labs

1. Add physical AI and robotics video categories
2. Build evaluation datasets as a recurring product

You know it worked when: $3M annualized revenue across 5 or more labs
