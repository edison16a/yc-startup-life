## General assessment

Sira offers AI agents plus time, scheduling, payroll and HR for hourly teams. The forecast window is 16.3 months from 2025-07-07 to 2026-11-17. We estimate 0.42 for an outside round above about $1M closing inside the window, from the 2022 to 2025 B2B anchor of 0.59 scaled for timing, with no AI hype lift. Analogs include Zeal and Instawork that raised, and Shift Messenger that went inactive. These odds do not rank this company against others. Reaching a round later is common for companies with paying customers.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 1.2%

**How we got the number.** Prior of about 1 percent (yc_acceptance.md, range 0.6 to 2 percent). Up for AI agents solving a concrete scheduling pain and a clear buyer; down because the description is a broad suite against Deputy, Homebase and Rippling, and payroll is a heavy regulatory build for four people. Net about 1.2 percent. The company is already in the Summer 2025 batch, shown for completeness.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly pass because a full time, scheduling, payroll and HR suite for hourly workers is a large scope for four founders and the market has Homebase, Deputy and 7shifts. Partners would want a narrow wedge such as shift filling with paying customers before expanding to payroll.
