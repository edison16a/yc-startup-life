The scored numbers are window specific and anchored on older cohorts, and the remaining risk is mostly execution and timing rather than the idea alone.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Get two manufacturers using a narrow module in production

1. Interview 50 plants and pick one subsector
2. Build the module beside their current ERP

You know it worked when: Two paying plants using it weekly

### Reach Seed

Show paid deployments and a migration playbook

1. Replace one legacy ERP end to end
2. Document implementation time and savings

You know it worked when: 5 to 10 paying plants and ARR above $300K

### Reach Series A

Scale deployments with a repeatable motion

1. Hire implementation engineers and a sales lead
2. Build integrations to common machines

You know it worked when: ARR above $3M and logo retention above 90 percent

### Reach Series B

Repeatable expansion with a second product or segment and efficient growth

1. Add a second wedge sold to the same buyer
2. Hire a sales leader and a customer success team
3. Show net revenue retention above 110 percent

You know it worked when: Revenue growth above 2x for a full year with stable gross margin

### Reach Series C and beyond

Category leadership with durable margins

1. Expand to adjacent markets or geographies
2. Build channel or platform partnerships
3. Show a path to profitability or a clear exit buyer

You know it worked when: Large enterprise customers and net retention above 120 percent
