## General assessment

Soren is an AI-native engineering firm modernizing legacy systems for traditional businesses. The seed estimate starts at the B2B cohort anchor of 59 percent and is cut to 0.42 for a 13 month window and a services model that investors discount against software. Later rounds are near zero in this window on timing alone, since the median time to Series A is 28 months. The odds do not rank this company against others. Named analogs, varos, rethoric and prosights, are service led and stayed modest, while greenboard raised an A as a software product.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 1.2%

**How we got the number.** Starting prior about 1 percent (0.6 to 2 percent, references/yc_acceptance.md). Up for the AI-native services theme that YC has funded and a San Francisco engineering team. Down for a services model with software margins unproven, a generic pitch of AI transformation, no stated team size and no named customers. Net about 1.2 percent. Blind note: Fall 2025 batch, forward estimate only.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say that an engineering consultancy is hard to scale, margins depend on hiring, and the claim of cheaper and faster delivery is shared by many new AI-native firms. Without named clients or revenue the pitch is generic, and partners prefer products to services.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show paying clients and margins.

1. Sign 3 clients with named projects
2. Report gross margin per project

You know it worked when: Project revenue above $30K a month with margins above 50 percent

### Reach Seed

Show productization beyond hourly work.

1. Package a repeatable legacy modernization offer
2. Move clients to retainers or usage pricing

You know it worked when: Recurring revenue above $1M annualized with 60 percent margin

### Reach Series A

Prove a software like business inside the services shell.

1. Ship internal delivery platform to customers
2. Reach 20 clients in one vertical

You know it worked when: Gross margin above 70 percent and net revenue retention above 110 percent
