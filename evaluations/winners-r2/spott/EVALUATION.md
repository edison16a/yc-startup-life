## General assessment

Spott sells an AI native ATS and CRM to recruiting agencies. The team is unusually large and AI adoption among agencies is rising, but Bullhorn dominates and YC peers Stardex and Crew already sell the same pitch. Inside a 22 month window the seed probability follows the B2B timing curve, not a lift for team size. A large existing team may also reduce the need for an announced round. A Series A inside the window is unlikely. The odds do not rank Spott against other companies. The forecast window runs from the as of date to 2026-11-17, and later rounds are only counted if they close inside it.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 2.0%

**How we got the number.** Already in the batch, so this is reported for completeness only. Start from the roughly 1 percent prior for a random applicant in references/yc_acceptance.md (band 0.6 to 2 percent), then adjust: a clear buyer and a team of 50 lift it about 2x, an AI native pitch fits current YC interest, and the crowded ATS space with peer YC companies takes some back; no traction is stated.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because AI native ATS for agencies already had YC companies (Stardex, Crew) and a dominant incumbent in Bullhorn, and a European team of 50 with no stated revenue is a heavy cost base for a batch company. The acceptance estimate is a prior for a random applicant and does not rank this company, and since it is already in the batch it matters only as context for the ladder.
