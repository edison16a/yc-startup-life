## General assessment

Standout is a Spring 2026 two sided hiring marketplace using agents. The window is only 7.4 months, and in B2B YC data only about half of eventual seed raisers close in that time, so the seed probability is about a third. Hiring marketplaces have a mixed record and the odds do not rank this company against others. The company claims 10,000 talent, 60 companies and 100 intros in its first month, which shows supply interest but not revenue, and the market already has funded AI recruiting rivals. The cohort anchors do not separate winners from losers in the earlier winners test, so treat the number as a base rate, not a judgment of quality.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Standout is already in the Spring 2026 batch; the estimate is shown for completeness. Starting rate about 1 percent. Claimed early usage of 10,000 talent in one month and a team of seven help, but the marketplace model is a negative in our factor table (marketplace shutdown 43 percent of n=14), so about 3 percent.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because AI recruiting is crowded and the traction numbers (sign ups and intros) do not show employers paying or candidates being hired. Triplebyte and similar hiring marketplaces in our data needed years and large rounds to show liquidity, and YC partners know the cold start problem of two sided markets well.

**Why it fails here.** Series C needs a category defining marketplace with take rate economics. Hiring is cyclical and spam from both sides can erode trust in intros; most analogs plateau at small revenue. Hiring cycles also fall in downturns, so revenue is cyclical and investors discount it.

A large outcome needs the anonymous agent matching to become the default way passive candidates are found. Marketplace winners are few, and the data show most small recruiting platforms end as acquihires or fade. The agent to agent design adds a novel trust question, whether candidates and employers believe automated intros are better than human recruiters.
