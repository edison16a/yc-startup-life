## General assessment

Stormy sells an AI back office to field and equipment service shops, from call answering and quoting through scheduling, parts and invoicing. The window is 16.3 months from 2025-07-07 to 2026-11-17, long enough for most seeds, so seed sits at 0.52 against the B2B anchor of 0.59. Series A given seed is 0.07 in the window. Several funded trades AI companies and field service platforms compete directly. The odds do not rank this company against others.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting prior about 1 percent (0.6 to 2 percent range). Up for a clear paying buyer in trades, AI timing and a product that already spans the job record. Down because the founders' domain background is not stated and the space has several funded trades AI companies. Net about 3 percent, conditional on the batch already given.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say that AI for trades is a crowded theme with many agents from the same batches, and that a bundle across call answering, scheduling and invoicing lacks a single sharp wedge. Partners may also doubt whether a five person team can integrate with the dominant field service software.

## Pros

- **Clear paying buyer and measurable loss**: Missed calls cost trades shops jobs, and buyers already pay for answering tools. (https://sameday.ai/post/ai-answering-services-features-comparison-for-hvac-and-plumbing-businesses)
- **Full job record gives a path to system of record**: The pitch runs every task off one job record, which can lock in customers. (https://stormy.ai)

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show shops paying and staying.

1. Sign ten equipment service shops
2. Measure calls answered and jobs booked

You know it worked when: Ten paying shops with a booked job lift.

### Reach Seed

Prove retention and one clear wedge.

1. Integrate with the main field service system
2. Charge by booked job

You know it worked when: Monthly recurring revenue growing with under 3 percent monthly churn.
