## General assessment

Stratify automates user research with AI agents run by a two person team in San Francisco. Demand exists, but Outset, Rally and Great Question already sell to the same teams, and a two person team has limited sales capacity. In a 19 month window the B2B anchor gives a seed above $1M near 40 percent after discounting for crowding, and a Series A within the window near 8 percent given seed. Takeover risk is high because incumbents can add AI interviews within months. Odds here do not rank this company against others.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 1.5%

**How we got the number.** The company is in the Spring 2025 batch, so this is shown for completeness. Start from the roughly 1 percent prior for a random applicant (0.6 to 2 percent range). Adjust up for a clear AI moderated interview wedge and a technical two person team; adjust down because several YC companies, including Outset, Rally and Great Question, already hold this space and no traction is stated.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because AI moderated user research already has YC backed competitors with funding and enterprise logos, such as Outset and Rally, plus Great Question and UserTesting. Without a differentiated recruiting panel or paying customers, the partners would see a thin wrapper over interview scripting and summarization.
