## General assessment

Synthio sells compliant agentic conversation to pharma commercial teams. The buyer has money and the need is real, but sales cycles are long, incumbents such as Veeva are adding agents, and a 29 person team needs funding soon. Inside a 19 month window the seed probability follows the Healthcare cohort timing, not a lift for team size or the AI theme. A Series A inside the window is unlikely. The odds do not rank Synthio against other companies. The forecast window runs from the as of date to 2026-11-17, and later rounds are only counted if they close inside it.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 2.0%

**How we got the number.** Already in the batch, so this is reported for completeness only. Start from the roughly 1 percent prior for a random applicant in references/yc_acceptance.md (band 0.6 to 2 percent), then adjust: a clear buyer with budget and compliance pain lifts it about 2x, a 29 person team signals real execution, and the crowded pharma AI field takes some back; no traction is stated in the input.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because pharma engagement AI is a crowded enterprise category with incumbents like Veeva, the input states no customers or revenue, and a 29 person team without named pharma buyers is hard to read as early product market fit. The acceptance estimate is a prior for a random applicant and does not rank this company, and since it is already in the batch it matters only as context for the ladder.
