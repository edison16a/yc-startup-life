## General assessment

Tago Health sells AI agents that work denials and A/R for US provider billing teams. The pain is large and measurable, but the field has many YC and funded rivals and healthcare sales are slow. The window is 7.4 months from 2026-04-06 to 2026-11-17, which cuts the seed odds to 0.44, the floor of the calibration rule against the 0.59 B2B anchor. Series A in the window is about 3 percent. The odds do not rank this company against others.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting prior is about 1 percent for a random applicant (0.6 to 2 percent range, references/yc_acceptance.md). Up because healthcare revenue cycle AI is a current YC theme with clear dollar value per recovered claim. Down because many YC companies already pursue the exact wedge and the team is three people with no stated customers or revenue. Net about 3 percent, conditional on the batch already given.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most likely say that denial management and A/R follow up is a crowded wedge, with multiple recent YC companies and funded rivals doing appeals and payer calls, and that without paying billing teams or a data advantage this is another agent wrapper. Payer integrations and HIPAA make sales slow.

## Pros

- **Clear dollar value per claim**: Denied claims and aged A/R are measurable and appeals are often overturned, giving a contingent pricing model. (https://www.hfma.org/revenue-cycle/denials-management/battle-of-the-bots-intensifies-over-denials/)
- **Works inside existing systems**: The product avoids new logins or migrations, lowering adoption friction. (https://tagohealth.com)

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show recovered dollars for a real billing team.

1. Run contingency pilots with two billing teams
2. Log appeals won per claim

You know it worked when: Dollars recovered and win rate on appeals.

### Reach Seed

Convert pilots into recurring contracts.

1. Add payer call agent coverage for the top five payers
2. Get SOC 2 and a trust center to unblock procurement

You know it worked when: Five paying customers and recovery dollars per month.
