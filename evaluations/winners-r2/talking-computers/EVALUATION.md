## General assessment

Talking Computers is a two person AI lab building real time interaction models for voice agents. Voice is one of the most funded AI categories, and PlayAI and Vapi from earlier YC batches show that investors pay for voice models and infrastructure. The team has no stated customers and sits against Deepgram, Phonic, Smallest.ai and the large labs that all push latency down. For the 10 month window I take the AI cohort timing for seed and add only a small lift for the hot category. A Series A inside the window would need a breakout. The odds do not rank this company against others, and if it raises the most likely size is $3M to $6M or higher than usual for software.

**How we got the number.** Already in the Winter 2026 batch, so reported for completeness. Start at the roughly 1 percent prior, lift for a research style technical team in a category investors fund heavily, and discount because there is no stated traction and model labs need capital that a two person team may not convince investors it can use well.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because foundation voice models are capital hungry and led by well funded players (Vapi W21 on top of others, PlayAI W23 acquired by Meta), and a two person lab without public customers must show a model result that beats open models from larger teams.

A public result that beats open models

### Reach Seed

Paying users and compute funding

1. Convert developers to paid usage
2. Pitch labs focused funds with the benchmark and usage curve

### Reach Series A

Defensible model lead and enterprise contracts

1. Train a second generation model
2. Sign enterprise voice agent customers

You know it worked when: $1M annual revenue and retained model lead for two releases
