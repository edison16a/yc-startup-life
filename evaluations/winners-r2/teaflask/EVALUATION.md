## General assessment

Teaflask wants to be the layer that puts a task doing agent into any SaaS product in a day. The need is real because every SaaS vendor feels pressure to ship an in-product agent. The trouble is that frameworks, model vendors and in house teams cover much of it, and a solo founder must prove paying customers fast. Over the 11 month window the chance of a round above $1M is near the cohort anchor, and a Series A inside the window is very unlikely. The odds do not rank this company against others.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Start from about 1 percent of applicants with a band of 0.6 to 2 percent (references/yc_acceptance.md). Up: a clear enterprise pain with named technical detail (streaming, crash recovery, guardrails) that reads as founder experience, and fit with the agent infrastructure theme YC funds. Down: a solo founder (102 of 662 researched solo, shutdown 21 percent against 17 percent for two), no stated traction, a crowded embedded copilot category. Net about 3 percent.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because a single founder with no stated customers is selling a horizontal agent layer that looks copyable by model vendors and open source SDKs. The interview would test whether any SaaS company has paid for this, and without a design partner the story reads as a framework looking for demand.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show one live SaaS customer with real end user usage.

1. Integrate with one design partner and measure tasks completed by end users
2. Write a clear teardown of why building in house is slower

You know it worked when: A named customer in production with weekly active end users of the agent

### Reach Seed

Three to five paying customers and a repeatable one day install.

1. Convert design partners to paid contracts
2. Add a cofounder or first engineer and publish the controls and security story

You know it worked when: At least $20K monthly recurring revenue and a deployment under one day

### Reach Series A

Show that agent usage grows inside each customer and retains.

1. Ship product analytics from agent usage that customers cite for roadmap decisions
2. Hire a sales lead for mid market SaaS

You know it worked when: Net revenue retention above 120 percent and 15 or more customers

## Assumptions and limits

- The company is solo with no disclosed traction as of 2026-04-06.
- Competition is recorded as of April 2026.
- The seed probability is for a window of 11.4 months and a round above about $1M. It does not rank this company against others: in the 2026 winners test the average seed probability was 0.65 for companies that raised and 0.62 for companies confirmed to have raised nothing beyond the YC deal.
- Series B and C plus steps are for ever reaching the round, not inside the window. Reaching a round years later is common for companies with paying customers.
- Knowledge base coverage: calibration cohorts are 2022 to 2025 B2B (n=150 researched), with funding coverage partial for most, so known-only rates are biased low.
