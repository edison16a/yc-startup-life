## General assessment

Text.ai builds Alfi, an AI participant for friend group chats that plans meals, calendars and images. The idea is easy to build and sits where Gen Z behaviour is moving, but consumer social products have a poor record in YC data: Squad, Guilded and Glimpse ended in acquisitions, mostly soft landings, and Rume and Assorted Bits closed. The Consumer 2022 and later cohort reached seed at 43 percent known only and none reached Series A so far. A 19 month window gives a seed probability of 0.40, close to the anchor, because the winners test showed hot sector lifts do not separate raisers. Series A inside the window is unlikely.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** The company is listed in the Spring 2025 batch, so acceptance is already known; this reports the prior for completeness. Starting rate is about 1 percent with a 0.6 to 2 percent band (yc_acceptance.md). Up to 3 percent for a team of 4 building a live AI product in a hot area, held there because consumer social without repeat usage is penalised and no traction is stated.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly decline because consumer group chat apps depend on every member adopting at once, the pitch gives no retention or usage number, and the largest messaging platforms can add an AI participant in weeks. A founder story about Gen Z leaving broadcast social is a thesis, not evidence of a paying or compounding habit.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show that one group uses Alfi weekly without prompting.

1. Launch in 20 real friend groups and record weekly active groups and messages the bot starts.
2. Interview group planners about what they pay with time today.

You know it worked when: At least 10 groups still active after 8 weeks.

### Reach Seed

Retention and a first revenue signal that investors can compare to other consumer apps.

1. Run a paid tier test for memory and planning.
2. Publish week 8 group retention and invites per group.

You know it worked when: Group retention above 30 percent at week 8 and a measured paid conversion.

### Reach Series A

A repeatable growth loop with margins that survive model costs.

1. Make the invite loop product led so each active group brings another.
2. Cut inference cost per active group below revenue per group.

You know it worked when: Organic growth above 10 percent per month and positive gross margin.
