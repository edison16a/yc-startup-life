## General assessment

Thirdlayer sells continual learning and alignment for enterprise agents. Neighbors Credal and Unify raised seeds, which shows investors back the theme, but the pitch is abstract, the team is two, and model providers are adding the same features. The window is 22.4 months, long enough that nearly all companies that will ever raise a seed have done so, so I use the cohort anchor lifted to 0.55, the cap. The odds do not rank the company against others. The biggest risk is that no concrete product emerges.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 2.0%

**How we got the number.** Start from about 1 percent. Adjust up for a timely topic (enterprise agents and evals) and a technical pitch; adjust down for a two person team with no stated traction, a very abstract pitch ('learning and alignment layer') with no named customer, and a crowded set of context-layer and eval companies such as Credal and Unify. Net about 2 percent. It is already in the Winter 2025 batch.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say the pitch is a layer of buzzwords with no named customer or product proof: ingest context, grade agents and learn from feedback describes retrieval, evals and fine-tuning, each owned by funded players. With two founders and no revenue, the interview would test whether anyone has asked for this.
