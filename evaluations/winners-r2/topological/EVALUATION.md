## General assessment

Topological builds a physics based model for topology optimization, selling to mechanical engineers. The need exists and the technical claim is notable, but incumbents such as Altair and Ansys already offer related tools and the team is two people. Inside a 16 month window a seed above $1M is a bit below the cohort anchor because there is no named customer, and a Series A is unlikely before the median of about 28 months. The ladder does not rank this company above others. The main failure path is that vendors add similar features and hardware teams do not change habits.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

A reproducible benchmark on public design problems.

1. Publish compliance error and runtime against a standard solver.
2. Release a demo part that is printed or machined.

You know it worked when: A benchmark table and one manufactured part.

### Reach Seed

A design partner that pays, to support a $3M to $6M seed.

1. Run two paid pilots with hardware teams.
2. Add manufacturability constraints for one process.

You know it worked when: Two paid pilots and a lead investor.

### Reach Series A

Repeat revenue inside engineering teams.

1. Ship a CAD plug in.
2. Hire a sales engineer.

You know it worked when: About $500K ARR.

## Assumptions and limits

- The 1930 times faster and under 5 percent error figures are company claims, not verified.

- Coverage limit: 662 of 6,399 companies are researched, and the B2B 2022-2024 cohort has 107 companies, so cohort rates are wide.
- Probabilities do not rank this company against others; the winners test found no separation between raisers and non-raisers.
