## General assessment

Totalis proposes a derivative layer for prediction markets. Volume on these markets has grown sharply and market makers and institutions are arriving, so the timing is real, but the input gives no description, team size or traction, and the venues Kalshi and Polymarket can restrict or copy what a layer on top does. In a 7 month window the seed probability is 0.30: the Fintech cohort reached seed at 56 percent known only, but only about half of eventual seeds land that early. Series A in the window is very unlikely. Peers pitching infrastructure to the same investors make it a crowded field.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 1.5%

**How we got the number.** The company is listed in the Spring 2026 batch, so acceptance is already known; this reports the prior. Starting from about 1 percent with a 0.6 to 2 percent band (yc_acceptance.md), nudged to 1.5 percent because prediction markets are one of the hottest areas for new fintech infrastructure, and held there because the input has no team, traction or description and regulation can stop a product overnight.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly decline because the pitch is a one line category claim with no stated users, volume or technical edge, and many applicants were also pitching infrastructure on prediction markets in the same batch window. Derivative products on event contracts also face legal ambiguity that a small team cannot resolve alone.

**Why it fails here.** Prediction market infrastructure startups depend on two venues, Kalshi and Polymarket, which can build or restrict what a layer on top does. Better Opinions and Inkling show that prediction market companies in YC history ended as small or niche businesses. A derivative layer could be absorbed by a venue or a market maker, or blocked by regulators, which limits the number of paths to over $100M.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

A working product on one venue with real users.

1. Build a first structured contract on one liquid market.
2. Trade it with own capital to prove pricing.

You know it worked when: Volume in dollars from outside traders.

### Reach Seed

Show growing volume and fee revenue plus legal clarity.

1. Get a legal opinion on the product structure.
2. Sign a venue or market maker partner.

You know it worked when: Monthly volume above $5M with positive fee revenue.

### Reach Series A

Multi venue neutral product with institutional users.

1. Integrate with Kalshi and Polymarket.
2. Win two institutional desks.

You know it worked when: Monthly volume above $100M.
