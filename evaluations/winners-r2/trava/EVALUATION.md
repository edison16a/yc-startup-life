## General assessment

Trava audits customs entries and automates tariff classification for importers. The 22 month window gives the highest window fraction of the five, but a solo founder and the licensed broker constraint pull the seed probability down to about 40 percent. A Series A in the window is unlikely. The odds do not rank this company against others. With one founder, the main uncertainty is whether a co-founder joins and whether the audit recovery revenue shows up early. The tariff environment in 2025 raises the value of getting classification right, which helps the need, but the cohort anchors do not separate winners from losers in the earlier winners test, so treat the number as a base rate.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because a solo founder in a regulated niche with licensed broker constraints is risky and the description gives no customer or revenue numbers.
