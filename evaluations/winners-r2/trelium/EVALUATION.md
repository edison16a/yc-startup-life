## General assessment

Trelium sells AI agents for back office order handling to print, merch and apparel distributors, with a team of 11. The need is real and paid, yet the category drew many funded AI worker companies in 2024 and 2025, and a niche vertical can cap the size investors see. For a 13 month window I put the seed probability at 0.45, a little above the window adjusted anchor of about 0.42 and under the 1.25 times cap, because the winners test showed no skill from traction claims. If a seed closes, the most likely sizes are $3M to $6M and $6M to $12M since a team of 11 with a live product fits the AI native shape. Series A inside the window is unlikely at 0.07. The central risk is a service like business in a small vertical.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Already in the Fall 2025 batch, so this is the prior for a random applicant. YC cites about 1 percent acceptance with a third party range of 0.6 to 2 percent. I raise it to 3 percent for a vertical with a concrete painful workflow, a team of 11 and real customers named in the description, but lower the lift because AI back office agents were an RFS style topic and thus common among applicants.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly decline because AI agents for back office work was among the most crowded application themes in 2025, the description gives no usage numbers or revenue, and a team of 11 for a company at this stage might read as pre-product spending on headcount rather than founder focus.

## Pros

- **Concrete paid workflow**: Order entry and PO to invoice matching are daily tasks with a clear labor cost for distributors, which supports willingness to pay.
- **Team of 11 at batch start**: A larger team than most batch companies suggests a live product and early customers, though it also raises burn.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Prove an agent can enter orders accurately for paying customers.

1. Measure straight through rate and error rate on real orders.
2. Sign three paying distributors on usage pricing.

You know it worked when: Over 85 percent of orders processed without human edits at three paying customers.

### Reach Seed

Show repeatable revenue and a path beyond print and merch.

1. Package the agent templates for two more distribution verticals.
2. Reach $30K monthly recurring revenue before the raise.

You know it worked when: $30K or more monthly recurring revenue and net revenue retention above 100 percent.
