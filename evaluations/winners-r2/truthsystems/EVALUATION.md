## General assessment

Truth Systems builds a compliance agent that enforces company policy on employee AI use in real time. The need is measured, with most security leaders suspecting prohibited AI use. Close analogs Unbound, Credal and Dynamo AI raised seeds of $4M to $4.8M, while Galini and others went quiet or repositioned. Large security vendors can bundle similar controls. For the 16.3 month window the seed probability is near the windowed cohort anchor and later rounds are unlikely. The odds do not rank this company.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting rate of about 1 percent from references/yc_acceptance.md. Up for a timely enterprise problem (shadow AI use was reported by 69 percent of surveyed security leaders) and AI security being a YC interest; down for a crowded field and no stated customers or security founder pedigree. Already listed in Summer 2025, so the ladder is the "if accepted" view.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say the pitch is generic: AI governance and guardrails are already offered by funded startups and by large security vendors, and the description names no customers or proof that policy translation into real time guardrails works better than rules based data loss prevention.

2. Measure blocked risky actions versus false blocks

Convert pilots into paid contracts inside the window.

1. Sign 3 paid annual contracts above $30K
2. Raise a seed on pilot to contract conversion data

You know it worked when: $100K annual recurring revenue and a seed above $1M

Show enterprise repeatability.
