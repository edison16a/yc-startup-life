## General assessment

Unisson sells AI subject matter experts to customer success and implementation teams. The wedge is real, with peers that raised seeds, but older YC peers sero and onboard-io raised little and lume was acquired. With only 10 months from the batch start to the judging date and a two person team, the seed is about a third likely and a Series A is a floor. A seed if reached is most likely $3M to $6M, and the success path needs one workflow with measurable savings.
