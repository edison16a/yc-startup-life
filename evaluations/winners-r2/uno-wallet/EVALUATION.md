## General assessment

Uno Wallet wants to replace Apple Pay with an AI wallet. The need for a replacement is weak and Apple controls tap to pay on the iPhone, though regulators are pushing it open. Past wallets such as Coin and Flinto either raised hype money and faded or raised little. Over a window of only 7 months a seed above $1M is about a quarter, below the B2B anchor because of the consumer payments risk and the short time. A Series A in the window is very unlikely. The ladder does not rank this company above others. The main failure path is no reason for users to switch.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

A working app that makes a real payment.

1. Get a card issuing partner and complete a live transaction.
2. Define one customer group with a reason to switch.

You know it worked when: First 100 live users making payments.

### Reach Seed

Usage that supports a $3M to $6M seed.

1. Show weekly repeat payments.
2. Sign one bank or merchant partner.

You know it worked when: 1,000 active users and a partner agreement.

### Reach Series A

Volume and unit economics for a Series A.

1. Add interchange revenue.
2. Complete compliance audits.

You know it worked when: Meaningful monthly payment volume with positive margin.
