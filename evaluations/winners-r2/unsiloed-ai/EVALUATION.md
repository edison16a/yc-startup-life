## General assessment

Unsiloed AI offers an API that turns complex multimodal documents into LLM ready data. The market is real and Reducto, Extend and Pulse have shown that investors fund it, but they also took the early customers and attention. The two person team claims strong benchmarks and usage that are not verified. For the 13 month window the seed probability follows the cohort timing for AI infrastructure, with a small lift capped at the allowed range. A Series A inside the window would need a breakout like Reducto. The odds do not rank Unsiloed against other companies, and the most likely seed size is $3M to $6M if it raises.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Prove accuracy and paying usage

1. Publish a reproducible benchmark with the dataset
2. Convert two startup users into paid contracts

You know it worked when: Paid monthly revenue and an external benchmark result

### Reach Seed

Show retention and a niche leaders do not serve

1. Pick one vertical such as financial charts or scientific documents
2. Sign three enterprise pilots
3. Show net revenue retention above 100 percent

You know it worked when: $20K monthly recurring revenue with three enterprise logos

### Reach Series A

Enterprise scale and defensibility

1. Add evaluation and workflow tooling
2. Hire sales and solutions engineers

You know it worked when: $1M annual recurring revenue with growth above 15 percent per month
