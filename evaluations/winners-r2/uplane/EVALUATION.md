## General assessment

Uplane pitches AI that replaces marketing agencies, generating ads and landing pages and steering spend across channels from CRM and ERP data. The team is 26 people, which is large for a batch company, but AI ad automation is crowded with funded rivals and platform tools. Inside 13.4 months the seed probability is about 0.47, close to the B2B cohort anchor of 0.61 times the share of seeds that arrive that early, and Series A inside the window is unlikely at about 0.08. Past analogs in the same wedge reached only seed or died.

**How we got the number.** The company is already in the batch that starts at the as_of date, so the acceptance step is reported only for completeness: the prior for a random applicant is about 1 percent (0.6 to 2 percent), and conditional on being accepted the forecast below is what matters. Team of 26 is unusually large for a batch company, which signals existing funding or revenue and raises odds modestly, but the ads category is crowded.

**Why it fails here.** YC would likely hesitate because AI ad generation and agency replacement are widely pitched, platforms such as Meta and Google build competing tools, and a 26 person team raises the question of why it needs the YC deal; the pitch also claims both enterprise and SMB.

## Roadmap

### Reach Seed

Show repeatable revenue and measurable return on ad spend gains within a year of the batch

1. Run paid pilots with five enterprise accounts and publish measured lift
2. Add a self serve SMB tier to produce revenue within weeks

You know it worked when: At least $50K monthly recurring revenue and two named case studies with lift against an agency baseline

### Reach Series A

Prove profit based optimization beats platform native tools

1. Ship deep CRM and ERP connectors with attribution to profit
2. Land annual contracts with ad budgets above $1M

You know it worked when: $1M plus ARR, net revenue retention above 110 percent

## Assumptions and limits

- Blind forecast made as of 2025-10-06 using only the YC batch description in the input file, with market-only web searches.
- Forecast window is 13.4 months, from 2025-10-06 to 2026-11-17.
- Seed means an outside financing round above about $1M that closes inside the window, not the standard YC deal of about $500K; the seed and Series A probabilities are window probabilities, not ever probabilities.
- The odds do not rank this company against other companies in the batch: in the 2026 winners test, team size, claimed traction and a hot sector did not separate raisers from non raisers, so the seed number stays near the cohort anchor.
- Cohort anchors come from older and 2022 to 2024 batches and are partly right censored; funding coverage in the data is incomplete (lower, known and upper ranges quoted).
