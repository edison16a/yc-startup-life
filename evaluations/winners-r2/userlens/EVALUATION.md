## General assessment

Userlens builds Lumi, an agent that watches product usage and nudges users toward the next feature at the right moment. The need is real, but product adoption is an established category with Pendo, Appcues and Userflow, and past YC entrants such as Frigade, Bento and Layup raised little or died. Over an 11 month window the chance of a round above $1M is near the cohort window anchor, and a Series A inside the window is very unlikely. The odds do not rank this company against others.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Start from about 1 percent with a band of 0.6 to 2 percent (references/yc_acceptance.md). Up: a team of four, a clear thesis about lean AI native companies needing proactive adoption, and a named product (Lumi). Down: the description gives no traction, the space is full of incumbents and YC analogs (Command AI, Frigade, Bento, Layup, Sero), and the pitch is easy to copy as a feature. Net about 3 percent.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because in-product guidance is a mature category with incumbents and several past YC entrants, and the pitch is a promise that an agent will nudge users at the right moment without any numbers on adoption lift. The Doubter question is whether a lean team buys another tool when their analytics vendor adds the same agent.

## Pros

- **Real budget category with a clear owner**: Product adoption software is a paid category with established vendors, so buyers exist. (https://www.pendo.io/pendo-blog/the-top-8-in-app-guidance-tools/)

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show measured adoption lift at a few customers.

1. Run pilots with five SaaS companies and report feature adoption change
2. Publish before and after numbers

You know it worked when: A named customer with a 20 percent lift in a target feature

### Reach Seed

Paid customers and a repeatable sale.

1. Convert pilots into annual contracts
2. Integrate with the main analytics tools

You know it worked when: $20K monthly recurring revenue across 10 customers

### Reach Series A

Show expansion and a defensible data advantage.

1. Use cross customer data to improve nudge timing
2. Hire sales for growth stage SaaS

You know it worked when: Net revenue retention above 120 percent and $1.5M annual recurring revenue

## Assumptions and limits

- Team of four, no disclosed traction as of 2026-04-06.
- Competition is recorded as of April 2026.
- The seed probability is for a window of 11.4 months and a round above about $1M. It does not rank this company against others: in the 2026 winners test the average seed probability was 0.65 for companies that raised and 0.62 for companies confirmed to have raised nothing beyond the YC deal.
- Series B and C plus steps are for ever reaching the round, not inside the window. Reaching a round years later is common for companies with paying customers.
- Knowledge base coverage: calibration cohorts are 2022 to 2025 B2B (n=150 researched), with funding coverage partial for most, so known-only rates are biased low.
