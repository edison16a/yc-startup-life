## General assessment

Vantel sells AI automation to commercial insurance brokerages from Stockholm with five people. The category has real pain and funded competitors, so investor interest is likely but not a ranking advantage. In a 22 month window the cohort anchor puts a seed above $1M near 45 percent, and a Series A inside the window is unlikely because the median A arrives about 28 months after the batch. The main risk is bundling by Applied and Vertafore plus slow broker sales cycles. Odds here do not rank this company against others.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 2.0%

**How we got the number.** The company is in the Winter 2025 batch, so this is shown for completeness. Start from the roughly 1 percent prior for a random applicant (0.6 to 2 percent range in yc_acceptance.md). Adjust up for a five person team, a defined buyer and a sector where YC has funded many insurance workflow companies, and keep it near 2 percent because no traction is stated.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because AI for insurance brokers is a crowded theme with several funded entrants, and a Stockholm team with no stated broker customers or revenue gives little proof that brokers will switch from their agency management system. The partners would also ask why this team beats funded rivals such as Herald and the mulligan style startups already in earlier batches.

## Pros

- **Clear buyer and painful manual workflow**: Commercial brokerage submissions and documents are manual and brokers are a defined, reachable buyer set. (https://fintech.global/2025/04/23/ai-driven-insurtech-1fort-raises-7-5m-to-transform-broker-workflows/)
- **Active investor interest in the category**: Herald closed a $12M Series A in October 2024 for commercial broker infrastructure. (https://coverager.com/herald-raises-12-million-to-revolutionize-insurance-connectivity-with-ai-powered-solutions/)

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show brokers will pay, not just try

1. Sign 3 brokerages to paid pilots before demo day
2. Record hours saved per submission from real broker files

You know it worked when: 3 paying brokerages and a measured time saving per submission

### Reach Seed

Turn pilots into repeatable ARR that a lead can underwrite

1. Hire a US based sales lead and a US customer reference
2. Integrate with Applied Epic or Vertafore so deployment takes days

You know it worked when: At least $15K to $25K monthly recurring revenue across 10 or more brokerages

### Reach Series A

Prove expansion across brokerage staff and lines

1. Add a second workflow such as renewals or certificates
2. Reach 2x net revenue retention in the first cohort

You know it worked when: $1M ARR and net revenue retention above 110 percent
