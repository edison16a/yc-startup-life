## General assessment

Verne builds general purpose physical AI and deploys robots. Investor money for robot foundation models is plentiful, but funded labs such as Physical Intelligence dominate and a team of 8 in Sacramento has to show real deployments. Inside a 16 month window the seed probability follows Industrials timing, not a lift for the theme. A Series A inside the window is unlikely. The odds do not rank Verne against other companies. The forecast window runs from the as of date to 2026-11-17, and later rounds are only counted if they close inside it.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most plausibly say no because general purpose robot models are dominated by lab teams with research pedigrees and huge funding, and the input gives no demo, customer or founder background that shows Verne can beat them with eight people. ## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show a robot learning a real task fast on real hardware

1. Teach 3 distinct tasks in hours on one arm
2. Record success rates and data needs

You know it worked when: Task success above 90 percent on video

### Reach Seed

Turn demos into a paying deployment

1. Deploy at one manufacturer
2. Collect proprietary task data from the site

You know it worked when: A paid deployment of $100K or more

### Reach Series A

Repeatable deployments across several customers

1. Productize the training pipeline
2. Add safety and reliability testing

You know it worked when: Three live sites with recurring revenue
