## General assessment

Vocality sells voice AI interpretation to hospitals that already pay for human interpreters. The need is large and legal, the technical bar is high and incumbents can add AI. The 22.4 month window is long enough for a seed round above $1M to land if a pilot converts, but a Series A inside it is unlikely. The odds do not rank this company against others in the batch because the anchor is the cohort rate. The main risks are hospital procurement time, commoditization and accuracy liability.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting rate about 1 percent for a random applicant (0.6 to 2 percent range, yc_acceptance.md). Adjusted up about 3 times for a clear large paid incumbent market, a specific buyer and a technical voice AI angle. Held back because two founders, no stated traction and hospital sales cycles are slow, and the factor table gives no lift for a pitch alone.

**Why it fails here.** YC partners may doubt that a two person team can win hospital procurement, HIPAA review and clinical accuracy standards against LanguageLine and other incumbents. General translation models keep improving, so they may also ask what stays defensible if hospitals can use a foundation model directly.
