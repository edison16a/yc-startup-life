## General assessment

Waffle is a template based AI video editor for short form content. Video creation demand is large, but CapCut and Meta give AI editing away free, funded AI editors and clippers compete, and consumer apps are marked down in the YC factor table for retention risk. A two person London team with no stated traction would need a paying niche to raise a seed above $1M inside 19 months. The probability is set below the consumer cohort anchor because of takeover risk and named analogs that failed. The odds do not rank Waffle against other companies, and later rounds are unlikely.
