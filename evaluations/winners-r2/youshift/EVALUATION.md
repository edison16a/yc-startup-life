## General assessment

YouShift automates hospital shift scheduling and aims to become a workforce OS. The 22.4 month window is long, covering most seed timing for healthcare, where 53 percent of 2022 to 2024 companies reach a seed. A ten person team and clear ROI help, but hospital procurement is slow, so the estimate is about 42 percent, with no lift above the anchor per the winners test. The odds do not rank it against other companies. Hospital scheduling has a clear buyer and ROI. The scored numbers are window specific and anchored on older cohorts, and the remaining risk is mostly execution and timing rather than the idea alone.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Show live hospital customers saving schedulers time

1. Run paid pilots in two hospitals
2. Quantify overtime and agency spend cut

You know it worked when: Two hospital contracts live with savings measured

### Reach Seed

Show multi hospital contracts and repeatable onboarding

1. Standardise integrations with timekeeping
2. Sell to health systems via champion departments

You know it worked when: ARR above $1M and 5 or more sites

### Reach Series A

Scale across health systems

1. Add adjacent modules like float pool
2. Hire a health system sales team

You know it worked when: ARR above $5M

### Reach Series B

Repeatable expansion with a second product or segment and efficient growth

1. Add a second wedge sold to the same buyer
2. Hire a sales leader and a customer success team
3. Show net revenue retention above 110 percent

You know it worked when: Revenue growth above 2x for a full year with stable gross margin

### Reach Series C and beyond

Category leadership with durable margins

1. Expand to adjacent markets or geographies
2. Build channel or platform partnerships
3. Show a path to profitability or a clear exit buyer

You know it worked when: Large enterprise customers and net retention above 120 percent
