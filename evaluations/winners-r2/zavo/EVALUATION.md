## General assessment

Zavo is a data research lab selling to frontier AI developers, a category with huge demand in 2025 and with strong leaders in Surge, Mercor and Scale. Cohort anchors for AI and B2B companies give about 59 to 64 percent seed ever, and about 70 percent of those land in 13 months, so the window estimate is 45 percent. Later rounds in the window are tail events. Customer concentration and thin margins are the main risks. The odds do not rank this company against others.

## Round by round: where and why it fails

### Getting into YC: 3.0%

**How we got the number.** Starting prior is about 1 percent for a random applicant (0.6 to 2 percent, references/yc_acceptance.md). Up for the timing, since model labs spent heavily on expert data in 2025, and for a team of eight with a research framing. Down because the description is thin and names no customers, and because Surge, Mercor, Scale and a wave of YC data labs already serve the same buyers. Net about 3 percent; the company is already in the batch so this is for completeness.

**Why it fails here.** YC would most likely say that selling data to a handful of frontier labs is a concentrated, lumpy business where Surge, Mercor and Scale hold the relationships, and that a London team with no named lab customer has not shown it can win a contract. Partners would also ask what domain edge a new lab has that the larger vendors cannot buy.

## Roadmap

### Get into YC

Win one named frontier lab project

1. Offer a paid pilot in one scarce domain
2. Produce a benchmark that labs cite

You know it worked when: One paid project from a recognised model developer

### Reach Seed

Show repeat orders and margin

1. Sign two more labs
2. Track gross margin per project
3. Build tooling for quality control

You know it worked when: Monthly revenue above 100K from at least three customers

### Reach Series A

Diversify beyond project work

1. Build a recurring evaluation or data product
2. Reduce dependence on one lab

You know it worked when: Largest customer under 40 percent of revenue and positive gross margin
