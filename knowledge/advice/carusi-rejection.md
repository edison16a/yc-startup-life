Claims: A YC rejection says little about a startup's quality or potential, and no single investor decides success. The author applied with a video product at an early stage and was not invited to interview.

Evidence from the author: He cites a tweet from Sam Altman saying about 6,500 applicants applied that summer, the wording of the YC rejection email (too many promising groups for the interview slots), and the well known cases of Buffer and Dropbox being rejected. He found the public Twitter discussion among applicants and partners helpful.

Corroboration: The scale of rejection is consistent with other published applicant counts handled in part 1 notes. The post is emotional support more than evidence, so it carries little weight for forecasting.

Short quote: "Please don't take it personally." (from the YC email as quoted)
