date: 2021-03-18

Claims: Cherry, an employee perks platform that passed through YC, raised over $700k in angel money and had 40 plus paying customers, then wound down. The founder says a rushed minimum product was not scalable and attracted customers who did not fit long term goals, fundraising was harder than expected, and a seed raise failed during COVID.

Evidence from the author: Founder recollection only, with figures limited to the raise and customer count. The page is a marketing post by Pilot, whose CEO did the interview. The founders closed gracefully, notified customers and returned money.

Corroboration: Speed first building with poor fit also appears in the Skyvern prior startup story. Anecdote level evidence.

Short quote: "we just wanted to have a lot of integrity about our customers"
