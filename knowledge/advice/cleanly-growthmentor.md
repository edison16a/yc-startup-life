Claims: Cleanly, a laundry delivery startup, was accepted on its third application after two rejections. Forer says the main YC benefits were credibility, mentors, Demo Day exposure and the alumni network, and that YC raised the company from good to better rather than from zero.

Evidence from the author: He says the first two applications took a few hours each, and an interview question asked why they beat a competitor already in YC. By the third, revenue was reportedly $60k to $70k a month gross in a few Manhattan zip codes, and the founders had turned down two small investment offers. They filed ten minutes late and still got a second interview. He estimates 50 to 70 percent of his batch no longer operates, with no source. All self reported and unaudited.

Corroboration: Traction before applying and a clear answer on competitors recur in the Relate and Vitable notes. The batch failure estimate is a rough anecdote and should not be used as a rate.

Short quote: "they are looking for products that people love"
