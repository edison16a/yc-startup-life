Claims: The first application in 2008 got no interview. After a year of building, the second application in 2009, written in a couple of hours, led to an interview and acceptance. The author says YC speeds up teams that already move fast and that applicants should commit to building rather than to getting in.

Evidence from the author: Between applications the team gained pilot customers, press, investor interest and a stronger demo and pitch, while the team and core idea were largely unchanged. He suggests Paul Graham remembered him from Hacker News comments. Commenters questioned the deal terms, and he defended the small stake and lack of board seats. Dated to the 2009 era, so terms and processes differ now.

Corroboration: Progress between attempts is the common thread with Relate and Vitable. The Hacker News recognition point is one person's guess.

Short quote: "Y Combinator operates on the first derivative of your speed."
