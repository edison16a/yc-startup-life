Claims: A rejection was still worth it because the application and interview clarified the goals. The author encourages any team with an idea to apply.

Evidence from the author: The first application a year earlier came when the idea was unvalidated with no revenue model. The second reached a video call and an in person interview with three partners. The pace surprised them, they interrupted each other, gave long answers, and focused on short term goals instead of the larger vision. The rejection email gave reasons they found useful. Self reported.

Corroboration: Long rambling answers as a failure mode recur in the Faria and Skyvern notes and in partner advice on clarity. Anecdote.

Short quote: "Because YC is neither Hogwarts nor Sour Grapes."
