Claims: Of more than 2,200 YC companies listed at the time, over 400 were inactive, about 20 percent, rising to about 40 percent when only the first 17 batches are counted because recent companies are too young to have failed. The article profiles failed companies: Atrium (premature scaling of a full stack legal service), uBiome (federal raid over billing), Meta (failed round and foreclosure), Call9 (payment model and burn), Boosted (thin hardware margins and tariffs), Airware (hardware investment overtaken by Chinese rivals) and Homejoy (worker classification suits, costly acquisition, weak retention).

Evidence from the author: Counts come from the YC company directory, company details from news reports. Causes are the author's interpretation, and the article says some official reasons differ from likely underlying ones.

Corroboration: Weaker than the base rates computed in this repo from the full dataset, which should override the 20 and 40 percent figures. Useful for failure mode examples only.
