Claims: The author and a co-founder who had never worked together were accepted by YC, and he declined because the co-founder relationship and the project did not feel right. He concludes acceptance alone is not a reason to start.

Evidence from the author: He says a partner emailed him earlier advising him to find a co-founder, that the interview was set with under 48 hours notice, lasted about ten minutes, and that mock interviews focused on metrics, user stories and jargon free explanations. A partner offered acceptance after asking who was in charge, with a day to decide. Personal account with partner emails described, none shown.

Corroboration: YC partners stress co-founder fit in the Seibel mistakes notes, and the short notice and ten minute format match other accounts. Anecdote about one pair.

Short quote: founding is "like dying over and over again and finding a way to get back up." (a friend's words as reported)
