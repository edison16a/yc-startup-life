Claims: Preparing for and sitting the YC interview is useful even when rejected. The author argues the decision looks like a classifier on founder traits, and that his profile (solo, older, first time, immigrant, not from an elite school) made acceptance unlikely whatever the idea.

Evidence from the author: He had applied seven times before while employed, then got a Mountain View interview on 2017-12-05. It lasted about ten minutes with four interviewers, covered users, market size, monetization and roadmap, and did not allow a demo. The rejection email the same evening cited doubts about podcast revenue, a crowded space, and weak durable advantage. He says about 500 of 8,000 to 9,000 applicants were interviewed and about 150 accepted, from memory. The 2021 update says the company thrives without YC.

Corroboration: The ten minute format and fast, blunt questions match the PostHog, Kamath and Skyvern notes. The applicant counts are his recollection and unverified. The classifier theory is opinion.

Short quote: "I got rejected in the end."
