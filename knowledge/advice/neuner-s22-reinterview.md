Evidence from the author: He submitted May 15, had a first ten minute interview May 23 and a reinterview May 24. Questions covered replacing versus complementing Slack, go to market, cofounder plans, prototypes and user acquisition, where he lacked a strong answer. He reports 5 to 10 percent replies to cold outreach and about five likely early adopters. Self reported.
