Fetched 2026-11-17, with a web search (same day) giving similar third party claims.

Claims: YC does not publish an official acceptance rate or per batch applicant counts. The article cites YC's investor facing line that over 10,000 companies apply every three months and about 1 percent get in, and gives a 0.6 to 2 percent range from third party analyses. It lists company counts: Winter 2022 398, Winter 2024 249, Fall 2025 148, Winter 2026 199, Spring 2026 193. Other third party pages in the same search claim 12,000 to 40,000 applications per cycle, 27,000 for Winter 2024, and 0.6 percent for Summer 2025; these figures disagree.

Evidence: Secondary aggregation; the batch sizes appear to come from a startup directory, and the applicant counts are not sourced to YC beyond the generic line. The only first hand counts in this folder are the Listen Notes estimates (8,000 to 9,000 applicants, about 500 interviews, about 150 accepted for Winter 2018), which are one founder's guess from a sticker and the room.

Corroboration: The roughly 1 percent figure is repeated widely but traces back to a few sources. Use about 1 percent with a wide uncertainty band and do not cite any single per batch rate as fact.
