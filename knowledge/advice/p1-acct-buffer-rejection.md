Fetched 2026-11-17.

Account: Buffer applied to YC very early, with about 1,500 users, 45 paying customers and around $280 a month in revenue, and was rejected. He published the application text and the rejection letter and says some answers now look naive. Buffer later joined the AngelPad incubator in August 2011 and went on to become a large company.

Author's evidence: The full application and letter published on the blog. His takeaway is that accelerators look for a world changing idea, a star team or strong traction, and Buffer did not yet show enough of any.

Corroboration: Early, weak revenue as the cause of rejection matches the Relate and Stable accounts. A famous rejected company does not show YC erred; it shows rejection is a weak negative signal. Anecdote weight, one case, 2011 era.
