Account: Over about five years he applied seven times. His first idea, an online education product, was rejected six times. He then partnered with two developers and built a charitable giving app. On the seventh application a founder he had consulted emailed a recommendation to Paul Graham, who replied with an interview invitation within two hours. The team then raised about $1.2 million within two weeks of the program, per his account. He reports that Close now has $30 million in ARR.

Author's evidence: Only his own narrative. The figures are unverified. Lessons he draws: busy work like branding and decks is not progress, build a viable product and talk to users, widen the network beyond aspiring founders, treat rejection as information.

Corroboration: Repeated applications leading to later success also appear in the Relate account here. The warm introduction route is a single case and could reflect the early YC era. Anecdote weight.
