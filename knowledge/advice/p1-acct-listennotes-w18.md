Account: After seven earlier rejections he applied in late November 2017 for Winter 2018 and interviewed on December 5, 2017 in Mountain View. Four interviewers, a 10 minute session, ended slightly early, which he read as a bad sign. A rejection email arrived that evening. Reasons given: no clear revenue path for podcasts, a crowded space, and a search approach not seen as a lasting edge, though it praised his technical skill and about 40k monthly uniques.

Author's evidence: His own timeline, the rejection email, and what he saw on interview day: a sticker marking the 8th interview day, other founders with one to two years full time, revenue or hardware. His estimate of 8,000 to 9,000 applicants, about 500 interviews and about 150 acceptances is a guess. He lists likely negative traits: solo, first time, two months full time, older, immigrant, no elite degree. The 2021 update says the company thrives without YC.

Corroboration: Interview length, four interviewers and same day emailed reasons match the other accounts here (Stable, Neu-ner) and YC's own pages. The self assessed bias traits are his opinion. Anecdote weight, one case.
