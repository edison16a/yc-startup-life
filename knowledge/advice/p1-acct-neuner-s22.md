Fetched 2026-11-17.

Account: He applied for Summer 2022 on May 15, was invited, and had two short interviews on May 23 and 24 covering the product, Slack's scaling problems, go to market, prototypes, differentiation and cofounder plans. YC rejected him that midnight. He wrote the rejection reasoning said it is incredibly hard to get users to notice a new tool, and incremental gains over Slack may not be enough.

Author's evidence: A dated timeline, lists of interview questions and the written feedback. He admits he had no good answer on customer acquisition. Afterwards he narrowed to remote first companies of 20 to 100 employees and reports 5 to 10 percent response to cold outreach.

Corroboration: Fast turnaround and written feedback match YC's FAQ and the other accounts here. Crowded space plus weak acquisition answer matches Listen Notes. His later reframing of the feedback is opinion. Anecdote weight.
