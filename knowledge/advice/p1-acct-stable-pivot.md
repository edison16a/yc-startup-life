Account: Her cofounder spotted a deadline two days away and they applied with a meeting feedback Slack bot and no built product, though 60 plus teams had signed up through a landing page. Testing showed feedback would be hard to collect, so they pivoted to automating care packages for remote workers and kept their jobs while doing user interviews for two weeks. They got an interview, faced four interviewers, felt every weakness was probed, and were rejected that evening. Six months later they were full time with a pre-seed round, and the post says they later got into YC.

Author's evidence: Landing page sign ups, 25 plus user interviews, conversion rates on three landing pages, and two letters of intent for the pivot. Lessons: be concise, find a non-obvious insight, show metrics driven traction in a short time.

Corroboration: Four interviewers and a same day emailed result match Listen Notes. The later YC acceptance is stated but deferred to another post that was not read. Anecdote weight.
