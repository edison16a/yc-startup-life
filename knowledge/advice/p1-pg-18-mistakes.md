author: Paul Graham
date: 2006-10
Fetched 2026-11-17.

Claims: Nearly all failure traces to not making something people want. He lists 18 contributing mistakes: single founder, bad programmer hires, founder fights, half hearted effort (keeping the day job), bad location, marginal niche, derivative idea, obstinacy, wrong platform, no specific user in mind, slow launch, launching too early, raising too little, spending too much, raising too much, poor investor management, sacrificing users for profit, and not getting hands dirty.

Evidence: Observation of early YC batches, no counts. Era note: 2006, before the current seed market.

Corroboration: The solo founder and half hearted points are partly echoed by applicant accounts (for example the Listen Notes account in this folder). Useful as a failure factor checklist for failure_modes work; the dataset should test it.
