Claims: The most ambitious startup ideas tend to frighten people, which is why founders dismiss them. Approach them indirectly: start with something modest that works, such as a small tool or niche user base, and expand. Do not announce the grand goal up front. He lists seven areas he wanted to see, including a new search engine, a replacement for email, alternatives to universities, internet delivered drama and continuous medical diagnosis.

Evidence: Reasoning from YC experience; the list is a wishlist, not validated demand.

Corroboration: Later YC Requests for Startups follow the same pattern of publishing wished for ideas. Read as a prompt for brainstorming, low weight as a predictor.
