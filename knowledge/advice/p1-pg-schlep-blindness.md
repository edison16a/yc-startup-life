Claims: People unconsciously avoid ideas that involve tedious, unpleasant work, so many good ideas sit in plain sight. Payments is his example: many developers saw the pain but chose easier projects. Daunting ideas face less competition and are worth more. A practical test is to ask what problem you wish someone else would solve for you. Ignorance of obstacles helps founders start.

Evidence: The payments example and founder remarks about not starting had they known. No data.

Corroboration: Same theme as the "don't filter for glamour" advice in startup ideas essays and many YC regulated or boring vertical successes. Medium to high weight as insight, not a measured rate.
