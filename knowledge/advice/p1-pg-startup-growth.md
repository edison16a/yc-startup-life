Claims: A startup is a company designed to grow fast, and everything else follows from that. The key metric is weekly growth rate, not raw counts. He gives roughly 5 to 7 percent a week as good, 10 percent as exceptional, and about 1 percent as a sign the founders have not found what works. Small local businesses are not startups in this sense. Most startups fail, but expected value is driven by the rare huge winners.

Evidence: Reasoning and YC batch experience, no published dataset.

Corroboration: The weekly growth framing is repeated by YC partners in Startup School talks. Useful as a heuristic for traction in an application; it is not a measured acceptance criterion.
