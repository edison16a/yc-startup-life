Claims: Apply as soon as you have a team and an idea you are excited about; waiting is described as a mistake. Submit one application per batch, using your favorite idea. Previous recommendations carry over but each application is new. Material changes such as adding or removing a cofounder go through an update form. Late applications are still reviewed. Decisions usually take about a month. Detailed feedback is provided only if you are invited to interview.

Evidence: Official policy statements. The page gives no acceptance rate or applicant counts, only a reference to application volume.

Corroboration: Matches the applicant accounts here that report an email decision on the interview day with reasons (Listen Notes, Stable, Neu-ner). Highest weight for process facts.
