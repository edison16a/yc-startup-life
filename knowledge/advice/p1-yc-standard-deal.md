Claims: The standard deal is $500,000 in total. It has two parts: a $125,000 post money SAFE that converts into 7 percent of the company, including any existing option pool, at the priced round; and a $375,000 uncapped MFN SAFE that converts on the terms of the lowest capped SAFE (or other more favorable terms such as a discount) issued between the MFN start date near the start of the batch and the priced round. The page's example: at a $15 million post money cap the MFN part equals 2.5 percent. YC gets a pro rata right to invest in later rounds. There are no fees and no milestones, and YC commits when a company is accepted.

Evidence: This is YC's own terms page.

Corroboration: The Relate applicant account in this folder (2022) describes the older 7 percent for $125k plus $375k structure, so the shape has been stable since about 2022. Older eras used smaller checks and larger equity, so do not apply these terms to pre-2022 companies. Highest weight: official primary source.
