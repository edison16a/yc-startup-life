author: Avni Patel Thompson (Poppy founder) with partner Kat Manalac, host Craig Cannon
date: 2019-01-30

Claims: Poppy, a childcare booking marketplace from YC W16, shut down in December 2018. The founder says unit economics worsened at scale, caregivers were transient and began to act like gig workers, growth of about 5 to 10 percent stayed below what she wanted, and runway fell to six to eight months.

Evidence from the author: She says many experiments (membership pricing, group care, programs) helped slightly but did not change the economics. A pivot would have cut about 75 percent of staff, likely acquirers declined, so she chose to close. Advice: keep investors updated monthly, decide the timing yourself, ask acquirers for a direct no, and allow time to grieve. Hosted by YC, so tone is supportive.

Corroboration: Thin marketplace margins appear in Ridejoy and Homejoy. Founder account, not independently audited.

Short quote: "there is power in choosing how this went down."
