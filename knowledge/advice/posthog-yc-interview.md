Claims: With little time before the interview, the team treated growth as a testable funnel and made quick progress, then was accepted despite feeling it had gone badly.

Evidence from the author: He says they spent $2k of an $8k budget on display ads with tracking tags, signed up 400 users in three weeks at about $10 each, and planned $10 per user monthly pricing, with two companies saying they would pay and first MRR around $600 weeks later. The interview on November 7 included Dalton Caldwell and Geoff Ralston, and a Wi-Fi failure blocked their demo. They did mock interviews. The acceptance call came about a week later. Self reported.

Corroboration: Mock interviews and showing early user numbers are repeated in the Skyvern and Kamath notes. A flawed interview still leading to acceptance shows feelings after the interview predict little.

Short quote: "That magical phone call came around a week later."
