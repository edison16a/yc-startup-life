Claims: Relate applied six times over three years, was rejected five times, and was accepted for S22. Each rejected round followed a pivot. The author says rejections traced to vague ideas, no proof of execution, and weak interviews, and that paying customers made the difference in the final application.

Evidence from the author: A recollection of the S21 interview where a partner pressed on lack of revenue and a consumer focus. Between applications the team charged early customers (eight pre-product deals of a few hundred dollars each, first paying customer June 2021) and wrote in plainer language with metrics. He describes the deal as 7% for $125k plus $375k at the next priced round. No YC documents or revenue figures are given, and the post ends as a sales pitch.

Corroboration: The advice to show paying users matches the Close, Cleanly and Vitable accounts and YC partner statements that plain language matters. Single company account, self reported.

Short quote: "Why aren't you making money?" (as recalled by the author)
