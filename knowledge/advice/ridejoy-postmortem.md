author: Jason Shen (Ridejoy co-founder)
date: 2017-01-11

Claims: Ridejoy, a long distance ride sharing marketplace from YC S11, shut down after raising $1.3M. The author says the founders could not reach venture scale, laid off staff and returned about half the seed money.

Evidence from the author: Causes he lists: rides tied to a route and date often expired unmatched, driver income on long routes was too small for full time sellers, growth depended on Craigslist and ended after a cease and desist in fall 2012, funding led to hiring and spending ahead of evidence, effort on an iPhone app did not drive growth, and the founders could not commit for the long haul. He cites about 45,000 users and 69,000 rides over the life of the site. Written by a founder, so hindsight and self serving framing are possible.

Corroboration: Marketplace liquidity problems and dependence on one channel also appear in the Failory note on Homejoy and the Poppy note.

Short quote: the founders "weren't ready to commit to Ridejoy for the long-haul"
