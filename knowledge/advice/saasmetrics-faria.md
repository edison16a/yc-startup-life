Claims: The company was rejected by Techstars, 500 Startups and YC. The author says the rejections reflected being early, light traction and weak differentiation, and he concludes that customers matter more than approval from accelerators.

Evidence from the author: He claims 500 plus sign ups across 44 countries, a couple dozen paying customers and about 30 percent monthly growth, and reproduces the rejection emails. He says the YC interview lasted ten minutes with three partners, deliberation took about five minutes, the email arrived about three hours later, and over 80 percent of questions were about customers and lessons learned. Metrics are self reported.

Corroboration: Short interview and customer focused questioning match several other applicant notes. Single company, no verification.

Short quote: "The only ones you need to impress are your customers."
