date: around 2019 to 2020 (undated page, estimated)

Claims: YC learned more from failure than success. Common post Demo Day mistakes: treating a seed round as proof of product market fit, hiring too fast, mismatching sales approach to price and customer, expecting investors to add more than money, skipping management basics, hiding financials, leaving founder roles vague, avoiding hard founder talks, and assuming a Series A is as easy as the angel round.

Evidence from the author: Justin.tv shared cash and runway openly, cut burn when it had about two months left and turned profitable. He cites YC totals of over 2,000 companies, and for B2B suggests reaching roughly $150k to $250k monthly revenue before a Series A. These are speaker statements relayed by a third party, undated.

Corroboration: Premature hiring and spending appear in the Ridejoy note, and weak fundraising preparation in the Cherry note. Reasonably corroborated.

Short quote: "Don't let an investor convince you that you are further along than you are."
