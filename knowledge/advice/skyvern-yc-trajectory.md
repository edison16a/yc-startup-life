Claims: Skyvern was the third attempt. YC gave a founder peer group, an internal forum with warm intros and exposure through launch events. The author says the YC brand and fundraising mattered less than expected, and that most YC advice is public.

Evidence from the author: The first application reached an interview but failed because the engineers could not answer why anyone would use the product. Their earlier startup failed because they built without talking to users. The second application, submitted on the last day with a demo video, had a first customer who then signed a contract. The interview lasted about ten minutes with two to three days notice, after which YC asked for a follow-up call and then accepted. Self reported.

Corroboration: Customer conversations before building match the Seibel mistakes notes. Building without users as a cause of failure also appears in post mortems here.

Short quote: "it turns out no one wanted to buy what we had built"
