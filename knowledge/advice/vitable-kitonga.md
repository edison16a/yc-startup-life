Claims: After three years, three interviews and two rejections, the author was accepted for S20. He credits real traction, growth, a clear story and luck.

Evidence from the author: In 2019 the company was a consumer urgent care service with high churn, he had recruited a co-founder only because he believed YC disliked solo founders, and the co-founder quit days before the interview trip. In 2020 he applied solo with an employer payroll deduction model. He reports MRR rising from about $1k in January 2020 to roughly $2k in February, doubling in March and April, and about 80 percent growth in May, partly helped by COVID demand. He did mock interviews with alumni. No outside verification.

Corroboration: A changed business model plus growth before reapplying matches Relate and Cleanly. A solo founder being accepted counters the belief that YC refuses solo founders, but it is one case.

Short quote: "I had real traction, growth, compelling story arc"
